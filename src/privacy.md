*Last updated: August 21, 2026*

The short version: this is a static website that collects **no personal data** from you.

## What we collect

**Nothing, directly.** There are no accounts, no comment forms, no newsletters, no contact forms, and no third-party tracking or advertising scripts. The one exception is our host's anonymous aggregate page counts, described below.

## What stays on your device

Your light/dark theme choice is stored in your browser's `localStorage`. It never leaves your device, is not a cookie, and is not readable by anyone but you (clearing your browser data removes it).

## Cookies

We do not set any cookies. None for tracking, none for preferences, none at all.

## Hosting logs and anonymous page counts

Like every website, our host (Vercel) processes standard technical request data — IP address, user agent, requested URL — in server logs for security and operation. This happens at the hosting layer, outside our control and inaccessible to us as personal profiles. Vercel's own practices are described in [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

We also count anonymous, aggregate page views through our host's Web Analytics — no cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal data, nothing that identifies a visitor. It tells us which pages get read; it cannot tell us who you are.

## Affiliate links

Our [shop page](/shop/) links to Amazon with an associate tag. Clicking those links takes you to Amazon under [Amazon's privacy policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJCE4ZB8KG6XQ); a purchase may earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you. We set no affiliate cookies ourselves, and nothing about your visit here is shared with Amazon unless you click through.

## External links

Servers listed on this tracker have their own websites with their own privacy practices. If you register on any private server, you are subject to their policies, not ours — and given the scene's history, assume any data you give a private server could become public when it shuts down.

## Future changes

Analytics and affiliate links already exist as described above. If we ever add anything beyond that — accounts, newsletters, advertising beyond the shop page — this page will be updated **before** those features go live. Check back here if that matters to you.

Questions about anything above? The absence of a contact form is itself part of the privacy story — we couldn't leak what we never asked for.
