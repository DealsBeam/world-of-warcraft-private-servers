Emberveil runs on a phone now. Full client, not companion app — and they want you to break it.

## TL;DR

Android beta opens: combat, quests, chat, proximity voice, auction house, handheld. Isolated realm, fresh chars, wiped after. Wave invites after device survey.

## What it is

Real client on Android, tested in-pocket for months by the team. Separate test realm: nothing carries over or back, everything wiped at end. "We would rather find out a build eats your gold somewhere the gold does not matter."

## What it is not

Finished. Tested on builders' own phones — tells nothing about everyone else's devices. Hence the form: what phone, which Android, how much you play. First wave stays small on purpose.

## Why it matters here

Second mobile client story this month next to [WoWee's Android builds](/blog/wowee-native-vulkan-client-soundtrack/). Mobile goes from novelty to pattern when two independent teams ship it. [Emberveil](/servers/emberveil/) opened Aug 15 on UE5; three weeks later it's in pockets.

## What's next

Apply via their form (game account, one minute). Watch wave pace — fast waves mean confidence, slow waves mean device fragmentation pain.
