Blizzard published the operational details for **Early Name Reservation** on **October 20**, in a post titled "Claim Your World of Warcraft: Forever Character Names". The window was already known. The start times, the ruleset counting rule and the install steps were not.

## When it opens

| Region | Opens |
|---|---|
| Europe | October 27 at 18:00 CET |
| North America | October 27 at 1:00 p.m. PDT |
| Korea | October 28 at 07:00 KST |
| Taiwan | October 28 at 07:00 CST |

North America and Europe open on the same day, and the Asian regions open the following morning at the same local hour. The window closes on **November 3**, the day before launch.

## Three characters, counted across every ruleset

This is the detail that changes how you plan. The post says players can "create and reserve names for up to three characters total across all Rulesets", and gives the example: reserve two on a **PvP** ruleset and you have one left for a **Normal** or **Role-Play** ruleset.

Two consequences follow. The cap is on your account, not on each ruleset, so splitting your three across PvP, Normal and Role-Play does not buy you more. And the limit applies only to Early Name Reservation, so it does not restrict how many characters you can create after launch.

The ruleset choice is made at creation time, which is the first official mention of the three ruleset types as a player-facing choice.

## Who qualifies

The footnote is explicit: **purchase of a World of Warcraft: Forever Digital Upgrade or Collector's Edition is required**, and the benefit applies **only to the specific WoW account it was purchased for**. The body of the post names the eligible pre-purchases as the **Skyborne Heroic Pack**, **Skyborne Epic Pack** and the limited-time **Warcraft Forever Collection**.

Names are **first come, first served** and Blizzard says outright that **desired names cannot be guaranteed**. That is the whole risk of a reservation window: it is a queue, not a claim.

## You need the Forever game version installed

The post's own checklist is short:

1. Update your graphics drivers.
2. Install the Battle.net desktop app and let it update.
3. Select World of Warcraft from the games list.
4. In the **Game Version** menu above the Play button, select **World of Warcraft: Forever**, then Install.
5. When the install finishes, select Play and **choose your Ruleset**.
6. Create up to three characters with your chosen names.

That is the same Game Version path as the stress test client, which opens today, so anyone who installed the stress test is already most of the way there.

## What to do with the weekend

Reservation opens in six days and closes the day before launch, which means the useful part is preparation rather than speed. Names on the popular list are gone in minutes, and the ones left after the first hour are usually either obscure or already taken by another account's earlier attempt.

Practically: pick three names you would genuinely accept, have a second tier ready, and check the spelling of anything from a language that does not use Latin script before you commit. Blizzard's post says character names are limited to first-come, first-served, and the cost of a bad choice is permanent for that character.
