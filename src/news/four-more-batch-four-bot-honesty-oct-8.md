Fourth nostalgic batch: four audited first-party, two bands from live counters, one `dev`, one `unknown`.

## EvolvedWotLK: 749 online, and 748 of them are labeled bots

The standout finding in weeks of population reads. EvolvedWotLK's widget reports **Online Characters 749**, then breaks it down: **1 human, 748 playerbots.**

Every private server with bots faces the same choice about what to publish, and almost all of them publish the undifferentiated total. A project that labels its own bots in the headline count is reporting more truthfully than servers showing larger numbers with no breakdown. The tier is `tiny` on the single human player, which is what the figure means once read correctly rather than as printed.

## The other three

**OmeWoW**, a 255 funserver with PvP, arena and voter rankings, carries one headcount: **12 website visitors online**. That measures web traffic, not players, and the entry records it as such with the tier `unknown`. Its news is dated August 2026.

**Stealth WoW**, a European TBC 2.4.3 custom realm, shows a player panel reading **Players Coming soon, Live at launch**. Not yet open, recorded `dev` on its own pre-launch status.

**Dark Storm Reloaded**, a TBC realm with top-player ladders and a VIP tier, reads **no players currently online**. Tier `tiny` on the site's own zero.

## Six more dead domains

Skufograd, Stormcrown, Foxiservers, Strikefall, SigmaWoW and DarkzoneWoW all fail to resolve. Noted without entries, as ever: a dead domain is evidence of no website, not of a shutdown.
