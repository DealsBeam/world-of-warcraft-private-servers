September 23, 2026: the community-list reconciliation keeps paying. Three more entries, each verified against a live first-party site before adding, each filed honestly at its real size.

**Azekai Reincarnated** (azerei.net) is the find of the pass. A **strict text-based roleplay** server on a custom 3.3.5a core, founded **July 15, 2026**, so it is genuinely young. The pillars are unusual: full-loot PvP where a loss can drop your equipped gear, **permanent consequences** (ordinary defeats are PKs, in-character companies can petition for a permanent char kill on high treason), and **dynamic property** through official companies, real-map territory, player buildings and private instances. The playerbots are edited for realism: they farm, trade and gear up with limited gold and cannot generate items. Ships with a bundled 27 GB client. Two players listed on Warperia, filed as tiny.

**Elderhide** (elderhide.com) is the opposite: a **Vanilla 1.12.1 PvP** realm on open-source VMaNGOS, original client and world, open registration, x1 rates. Nothing custom claimed, nothing to verify beyond the site being live and the core being the one it says. Tiny.

**Project Rx** (projectrx.net) is a **3.3.5a all-GM roleplay** server with a custom phasing system that creates private worlds players can build in, full GM commands from the start, and gear designed from scratch. A one-man project, explicitly not progression-focused: it is a stage, not a journey. The only server in the tracker where that is a feature. Tiny.

Not added, with reasons: **Amdir** (47 custom systems, Ukrainian, x5) and **Digital World** (Australian, LGBT+, 18+) both have directory entries but no first-party site I could reach, so they stay off until verified. **Zamolxia** and **SKYWOW** returned 200 but describe systems I could not corroborate beyond Warperia's own copy. **Project Ebonhold** is not a WoW server at all, it is a Vampire Survivors-like, so it never enters the tracker.

SOURCES: first-party sites azerei.net, elderhide.com, projectrx.net (all checked Sep 23); Warperia and TopG directory listings for reconciliation.
