## Sanctuary is the most distinctive design in weeks

Three ideas, stated on the site as the whole pitch: **positional voice chat** carrying 5 to 100 yards at the speaker's choice, **no visible names** with strangers reading as their race plus consistent stranger-names for talking about people never met, and **consent-based PvP** with town notice boards taking real bills.

Whether any of it works in practice is unverifiable from the outside. But a roleplay realm whose mechanics are all about information control, who hears you, who knows you, what violence you consent to, is designing at a level most entries never reach.

## Moeni's zeros are maintenance, not emptiness

The front page shows zero Alliance, zero Horde, and a **scheduled maintenance banner**. Zeros beside a maintenance notice measure the maintenance, and the entry records them that way rather than as a population reading.

This is the same discipline as the ArgusWoW correction in reverse: read what the page actually says about its own numbers before repeating them.
