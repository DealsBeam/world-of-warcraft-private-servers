Blizzard published the first episode of its World of Warcraft: Forever Podcast on September 24. The 62-minute video features Senior Game Designer Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield, Lead Classic Designer Tim Jones, host Countdown To Classic, and guest Xaryu.

The episode focuses on the Forever beta launch and player experiences. Blizzard's article provides the video but no transcript, patch notes, Server Slam date, level-cap update, or new system claim. Treat it as official discussion content, not evidence for unresolved tracker dates.
