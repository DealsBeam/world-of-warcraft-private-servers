Blizzard published "Cost of Honor" on **November 13** at 18:00 UTC. The announcement presents it as a fireside telling from **Demar Brant**, with the young people of the **Riverglades** gathered around a campfire while a storm passes overhead.

## The credits

- **Written by:** Jonathan Maberry.
- **Illustrated by:** Surfside 3D.
- **Narrated by:** Peter Jessop as Demar Brant.

Blizzard's page also offers a downloadable PDF of the story.

## The frame Blizzard gives it

The publisher's description is short enough to quote in full:

> As a storm rages in the skies above, the young people of the Riverglades gather around a campfire to hear the stories behind their history's legends from the great Demar Brant. Through tales of war, sacrifice, and hard choices, they learn that the true strength of a people lies not in victory alone, but in the courage to protect one another.

That is the frame, not a plot summary: a storyteller, an audience, and a set of legends about the region's past. The story is set in the Riverglades, one of the new zones Blizzard built for Forever, and it uses the campfire as its stage.

## Where it sits in the week

The story landed on the same day as Blizzard's weekly roundup, which collected the lore podcast, the story, the upgrade editions and the week's videos. The [lore podcast](/news/forever-lore-podcast-nov-12/) had gone up a day earlier, with Sean Copeland and Josh Greenfield talking about Forever's lore. The short story is the fiction side of the same push.

It sits alongside the November 13 hotfix notes, which are a separate piece of news; the story is not a patch note and does not describe gameplay changes.

## What we are not claiming

We have not read the full story or listened to the narration, so this brief records the credits, the frame and the publication date. It does not summarize the plot beyond Blizzard's own description, and it does not treat the story as canon for any character or event unless Blizzard says so.
