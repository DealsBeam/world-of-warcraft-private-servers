BlizzCon 2026 opens September 12 in Anaheim, and for the first time since WoW Classic launched in 2019, a genuine mystery product sits at the center of it: **Project Camelot**, the Classic+ project our [tracker hub](/classic-plus/) has followed through 32 datamines and leaks since October.

Here is what the evidence supports, what is plausible but unconfirmed, and what we expect to stay hidden.

## Near-certain (multiple independent leaks agree)

**1. The name and the pitch.** Datamined store licenses from June name an internal project codenamed Camelot; the [June 18 roadmap leak](/classic-plus/) described it internally as a "new expansion of Classic." Expect official branding on stage — "Infinite Classic" was the working title in the [October 4chan leak](/classic-plus/), but marketing may have renamed it since.

**2. New content on the Vanilla map.** Three separate leaks ([Jun 25 zones rumor](/classic-plus/), the Jul 22 Karazhan leak, Aug 15's Dwarf Shaman clues) agree: new zones, at least one new race combination, and reworked endgame. Karazhan as a level-60 raid appeared in two independent sources.

**3. A paid edition structure.** The Jun 25 datamine showed Heroic and Epic editions plus a battle pass. Blizzard has shipped tiered editions for every launch since Shadowlands — this one is already priced in their systems.

**4. Wrath of the Lich King Anniversary.** State of Azeroth promised Classic news after the August 27 Black Temple opening, with BlizzCon named as the venue. A Wrath Anniversary reveal alongside Camelot fits the pattern set by Vanilla and TBC Anniversary editions.

## Plausible (single-source or inferred)

- **Spellblade class** — rumored June 20, never corroborated. If a new class appears, this is the front-runner.
- **Level cap above 60, phased** — the [July timeline math](/classic-plus/) suggested 2027 release, which implies a longer leveling arc than Vanilla's.
- **Fresh-start seasonal realms at launch** — consistent with how Anniversary editions launched, no direct leak yet.

## What we don't expect on stage

**A release date.** Everything points to 2027. Expect a window at best ("next year"), with beta signups opening immediately after the panel — that's how Diablo IV and Midnight handled it.

**Private-server QoL concessions.** Dual spec, group finder, guild banks — community wishlist items that leaked feature surveys touched on. Blizzard historically announces these quietly in patch notes, not keynotes.

## What it means for the private scene

The [shutdown campaign](/blog/blizzard-2026-private-server-crackdown/) that cleared Turtle WoW, Stormforge, Everlook and others through spring looks different in hindsight: the field was being cleared for this announcement. Whether official Classic+ retains the players who fled to successors like [Ravencraft](/servers/ravencraft/), [OctoWoW](/servers/octowow/) and [Capybara WoW](/servers/capybara-wow/) is the scene's defining question for 2027 — our [Turtle successor rankings](/blog/turtle-successors-ranked/) cover who's positioned to survive it.
