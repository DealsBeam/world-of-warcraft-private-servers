Blizzard's November 11 hotfix notes bring another class pass, bigger profession bags and a round of quest repairs. They also carry a reversal. The Auction House changes published in the November 10 update have been taken out and pushed to the November 17 weekly maintenance.

Kaivax posted the correction in the running hotfix thread shortly after the November 11 notes went up:

> We've temporarily removed Auction House changes from yesterday's update, as they will go live with scheduled weekly maintenance on November 17. They will appear again in the hotfixes update thereafter.

## What that reverses

Our [November 10 tuning brief](/news/forever-tuning-nov-10/) reported three Auction House lines as shipped: a minimum deposit cut to **1 copper** from 1 silver, a fix for unread mail reading as from the Stormwind Auction House, and a fix for mail that took about two minutes instead of 60 seconds. None of those lines appear in the current hotfix article. They are not live yet.

This is a correction to our earlier report, not a change in what Blizzard originally published. The November 10 notes did include those lines when we read them on November 11. Blizzard has since removed them and dated the delay. We have updated the earlier brief with a dated note rather than rewriting its original text.

## Item Restoration is offline

The Customer Support section of the November 11 notes is one line: **the Item Restoration service has been disabled while Blizzard investigates and addresses a critical bug.** No item type, timeframe or workaround is named.

That lands the day after Blizzard restored Shaman totems lost in Monday's crash. The two notices are separate, and the hotfix notes do not connect them. For now, do not count on Item Restoration to recover anything lost in the same window.

## Bags get bigger

Tailoring bags that were "unintentionally smaller than intended" are corrected, and each now costs one more bolt:

- **Red Mageweave Bag**: 14 slots, requires 5 bolts of mageweave (was 4), previously smaller than intended.
- **Green Silk Pack**: 12 slots, requires 5 bolts of silk (was 4).
- **Black Silk Pack**: 12 slots, requires 6 bolts of silk (was 5).
- **Green Woolen Bag**: 10 slots, requires 5 bolts of wool (was 4).
- **Red Woolen Bag**: 10 slots, requires 5 bolts of wool (was 4).

Blizzard does not say what the old slot counts were. The notes give the new sizes only, so we are not reconstructing the previous numbers.

## Other profession fixes

- **Enchanting:** Arcane Salvager now requires the **Runed Golden Rod** tool (was Runed Truesilver Rod) and correctly applies its disenchanting bonus to lower-level enchanters. Enchant Boots and Bracer **Lesser Spirit** now give their stated Spirit bonus instead of Resist All.
- **Blacksmithing:** **Coarse Grinding Stone** now provides skill-ups to **90** (was 75).
- **Cooking:** the **Journeyman Campfire Kit** now requires **Cooking 90** to place, matching its recipe requirement (was 140).
- **Engineering:** **Iron Grenades** vendor price falls to **1 silver 25 copper** (was 5 silver). **Swiftstrike Shot** and **Swiftfeather Arrow** are no longer Bind-on-Pickup; Blizzard notes they are not available until raids unlock and says the change may influence profession choice.
- **Alchemy:** **Minor Arcane Elixir** sells for **5 copper** (was 25 copper), and "Recipe: Elixir of Greater Firepower" is renamed **"Recipe: Elixir of Holy Power"**, with the aura renamed to match.

## Class changes that touch rotations

- **Paladin:** Judgement is now treated as a **ranged attack**. It no longer counts as melee for item and effect procs and uses the Paladin's ranged hit chance. Retribution's **Unsealed Fury** no longer generates echoes from Windfury Totem attacks, following the fix that let Windfury Totem activate from Holy Strike.
- **Hunter:** Volley no longer activates procs and stops channeling when the Hunter moves.
- **Druid:** Feral's **Rend and Tear** now works on Pounce Bleed.
- **Priest:** Gnome's Confounding Flash and the Holy **Twilight Focus** talent now work in Shadowform.
- **Shaman:** **Flametongue Totem Rank 2** now works.

## Riverglades and quest repairs

Riverglades gets three damage reductions. The Twilight Enforcer's **Smash** deals significantly less, **Executioner Morg** hits less hard, and the Twilight Forgemaster's **Mark of Flames** now increases damage by **50, down from 1000**.

The quest fixes cover the Western Plaguelands Command Tent for "Scarlet Diversions", the Witherbark Mallet for "The Giant in the Den" in Arathi Highlands, and the Cage Door reset for "Freedom for All Creatures" in Feralas. Mounting now works in named areas of Shen'dralas. Two item fixes also land: Bloody Claws of Attack correctly gives **+1 Fist/Unarmed Weapon skill**, and Earthen Silk Slippers no longer give more spell damage than intended.

## The build and the record

Our CDN watch still reads **1.60.1.70888** for the live `wowf` track, updated November 11. These are server-side hotfixes; no new client build is attached to them in our record. Blizzard reuses one article for the running list, and its slug has already changed from the November 10 version to the November 11 version at the same news ID. Older links to the November 10 slug still resolve to the current page.
