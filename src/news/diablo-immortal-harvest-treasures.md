Blizzard's November Diablo Immortal content update went live after regional maintenance. The first-party notes cover several dated events, a new Battle Pass item and class balance changes.

This sits under our **Diablo** news tag alongside the Diablo 4 and Diablo 2 stories.

## Maintenance timing

Maintenance ran **November 19 from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. PST** for Oceania, East Asia, Southeast Asia and Europe, and **November 18 from 12 a.m. to 2 a.m. PST** for Americas servers. Everything in the notes went live after that maintenance unless a later date is given.

## Harvest Treasures, to December 8

The new event runs from **November 18 at 2 a.m. PST to December 8 at 2:59 a.m. PST**. It uses **Abundant Treasure Cards** with a **3x3 grid**. In-game tasks, from logging in to defeating Helliquary bosses, earn a scratch of one grid square, which has a chance to grant a reward. Progress is tracked in the Abundance and Treasure tab of the Events UI.

## Phantom Gallery, to December 1

Phantom Gallery runs **November 18 at 2 a.m. PST to December 1 at 2 a.m. PST**. Spending **Litra**, bought with Eternal Orbs, draws one item at random from a pool of ten. Each item can be won once, and the cost rises with every draw. Ten draws guarantee the full pool. Extra rewards land on draws 2, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 10.

Blizzard published first-draw rates, including Bound Gem Fragment x5 at **74.08 percent**, Rare Crest x20 at **22.01 percent**, and Legendary Crest x10 at **1.37 percent**. Unspent Litra is removed when the event ends.

## The rest of the update

The notes also list:

- **Maiden's Chains** in the new Battle Pass;
- updates to **Cross-Server Bout of Realms**;
- **class balance changes**;
- quality of life changes and bug fixes;
- a **server merge for EU and Asia only**.

We are not detailing the class balance numbers here because the notes are long and the changes are specific to Immortal's classes. The first-party post is the place to read them before playing.
