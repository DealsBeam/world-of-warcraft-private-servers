Blizzard has published the reward list for **Talebound**, the limited-time mode arriving with **Midnight patch 12.1.7 on December 1**. Our earlier Talebound article covered the mode; this adds the currency and the prices.

## How the currency works

- **Bound Silver** is a **Warband-bound** currency. It is earned by completing quests, achievements and progress in the Tales of the Storyteller.
- Along the way you collect **Ducats**, which convert into the resources needed to progress through Talebound and into more Bound Silver.
- Once a tale is complete, return to **Quillgrave** and trade Ducats for Bound Silver and other rewards.
- Bound Silver buys transmog sets, mounts and other cosmetics from the Talebound store, reachable from the character selection screen, the Game Menu, or **Nib** in Quillgrave.

The Warband-bound part matters: progress is shared across characters rather than trapped on the one that ran the mode.
