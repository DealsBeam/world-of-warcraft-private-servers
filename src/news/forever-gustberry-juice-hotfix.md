ForeverChanges reports another undocumented hotfix in the WoW Forever client. The site first saw it in the caches the launch realms send at **03:29 UTC on November 22**, and dates the entry November 21. Blizzard has posted no notes for it.

The one observed change is small but concrete:

> Gustberry Juice now costs **1 silver 25 copper** at vendors, up from **25 copper**. It sells for **6 copper**, up from 5.

That is a fivefold increase in the vendor price of a consumable, with a much smaller increase in its sell value.

## Why we are reporting this separately

This is the third undocumented change cluster we have covered in four days. The pattern is consistent: data sites see changes in realm caches before Blizzard writes them down.

Our rule has not changed. We record what the data sites observed, we say when Blizzard has not confirmed it, and we do not fold the change into our hotfix history as shipped. The earlier report from November 19 now has one confirmed line and one still withheld; this one has no Blizzard line at all.

If you buy Gustberry Juice, check the vendor price in game before stocking up. We have not tested it ourselves, so treat the numbers as reported.
