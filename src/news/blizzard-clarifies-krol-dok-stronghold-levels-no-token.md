September 14, 2026 — Two items from BlizzCon got official answers after the show: Blizzard corrected the level range for the Krol'dok Stronghold dungeon in a forum post, and the WoW Team confirmed in an interview that Forever will not ship a WoW Token or paid boosts.

## Krol'dok Stronghold is level 40-45, not 40-55

In a blue post on the official WoW forums, Blizzard said the Krol'dok Stronghold dungeon was "listed alongside an incorrect level range" in material from BlizzCon. The corrected range is **levels 40-45**. The same post published the official brackets for all nine launch dungeons in Forever:

| Dungeon | Level range |
| --- | --- |
| The Hall of Thanes | 13-18 |
| Ruins of Lordaeron | 15-20 |
| Excavation Site | 24-29 |
| City of Dalaran | 28-33 |
| The Drowned City | 35-40 |
| Krol'dok Stronghold | 40-45 |
| Alcaz Prison | 48-53 |
| Blackmaw Hold | 55-60 |
| Shaper's Terrace | 58-60 |

Our [Forever hub](/classic-plus/) has been updated with the full bracket list. The roster matches our tracked nine dungeons with the exception that Blizzard's official list names the second dungeon "Excavation Site" — the Whelgar's Excavation reading came from the earlier panel recap phrasing.

## No WoW Token, no boosts

In a BlizzCon interview conducted by Destin for the Tailosive pod, lead designer Clay Stone said: "No plans for anything like a WoW Token or a boost." The wider sentiment, per the interview: "The Journey is the game. End game is amazing, but the journey is so important."

The interview also touched on the team behind Forever — executive producer Josh Greenfield, lead designer Clay Stone, and lead software engineer Nora Mills — and on why the Riverglades was chosen as a new zone: it was "a chunk of map that really hadn't been filled out yet," giving players somewhere to go besides spamming Scarlet Monastery.

The no-Token, no-boost line answers a question on our [Sep 17 Q&A list](/news/wow-forever-live-qa-questions/) — whether the "no plans right now" framing left the door open. Stone's wording was unqualified: no Token, no boost plans.
