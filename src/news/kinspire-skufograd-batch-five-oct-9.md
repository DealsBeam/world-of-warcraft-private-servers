Fifth nostalgic batch, smaller this time: two audited first-party, both banded `tiny` on the projects' own statements.

## Kinspire: two classes, one hero, single digits by design

Kinspire runs WotLK 3.3.5a on AzerothCore with a genuine dual-class system: keep a primary class, take a secondary, with abilities, resources, armor, weapons and talents from both, resolved server-side through a module plus custom addon and MPQ patch pipeline rather than GM-granted spells.

The combinations read like character-creation heresy: Mage/Warrior, Druid/Paladin, Rogue/Shaman, Paladin/Shaman, with the server handling even the awkward cases like two classes sharing a resource.

The FAQ states **population is measured in single digits by design**. A family-and-friends realm that says so on its front page is the opposite of the dead-servers-with-live-counters problem: a small number, owned, current.

## Skufograd: 19 players beside 255 labeled bots

Russian 3.3.5a realm in a Classic content phase with TBC/WotLK restrictions, draenei and blood elves unlocked, dated hotfixes as recent as September 5 2026, and published bot-behavior notes including periodic bot online/offline cycling.

The widget reads **19 online (15 Alliance, 4 Horde) with 255 bots alongside**, 0 unique players today and 16 for the month. Like EvolvedWotLK's labeled breakdown, the bots are counted separately rather than folded into the headline, and the tier follows the human figure.

One curiosity recorded rather than resolved: the published realmlist points at **logon.extazy-wow.ru**, not the site domain. Whether that is infrastructure sharing, a rename in progress, or something else is unconfirmed, and the entry says so.
