## World of Warcraft

**Classic Plus / Project Camelot.** The worst-kept secret in WoW. Build 69698 tracked September 6. Blizzard website code showed Classic Plus armory additions in August. Two "What's Next" panels on Day 1 — this is where the reveal happens if it's coming. Corden says codenames "Camelot" (ancient Azeroth prequel) and "Warcraft Chronicles" (RTS roots) are both in play. Realm First! notes neither panel names Camelot as a player-facing product yet.

**Midnight 12.1.5.** On PTR now. "The Promise of Tomorrow" campaign, single-boss raid "The Unbinding of Kith'ix," Labyrinths, Aqir Invasion. Likely gets a release date on stage.

**The Last Titan.** Full rundown expected. Xal'atath arc conclusion.

## StarCraft

**Two projects, not one.** Jason Schreier clarified that the Nexon-developed StarCraft game is **separate from** the Dan Hay-led Blizzard internal shooter. That means two concurrent StarCraft projects: (1) Blizzard's open-world co-op TPS led by Dan Hay (Far Cry), and (2) a Nexon deal that reportedly includes Overwatch mobile publishing rights.

**The shooter.** Corden saw test footage: Terran marines vs Zerg, open-world, cinematic campaign. Not Destiny-like MMO-lite (Schreier podcast). Crossplay with PlayStation confirmed in Corden's reporting. Hopes for co-op PvE like Helldivers 2 / Gears Siege.

**The ARG.** Day 3 (September 6-7) added sensor feed data — "6800_SOUTH MOTION DETECTED," 29 occupants registered. Story set in year 2575 on planet Roxara, an ancient Xel'Naga outpost at the Roxara Lunar Mining Complex. Confirmed created with AI, noted as less immersive than Project Blackstone 2013.

**Blackbird Interactive** rumored on classic RTS help. Single source (Corden), unconfirmed.

**Diablo 4 Season 15.** Launching around September 15. Features include Soul Splinters, Rebirth mechanics, Challenge Dungeons. Map leak shows new western continent (Kingsport, Doom Craig). **D2 Warlock DLC.** Rumored, not confirmed.

**Diablo Immortal x Spawn.** Image Comics' Spawn arrives September 29. Todd McFarlane appears at a Diablo Immortal panel on September 13.

**Diablo TV show.** Netflix or Amazon, reportedly in development. deadmau5 appearing at a Diablo panel may hint at transmedia announcements.

**30th anniversary** of the Diablo franchise in 2026.

## What to watch for

The biggest question heading into Saturday is whether Blizzard leads with Classic Plus or saves it for a dedicated panel. The opening ceremony typically sets the tone — if Camelot gets a cinematic reveal, it signals Blizzard is confident the product is ready for public scrutiny. If it's relegated to a dev update, the timeline is likely further out.

The StarCraft dual-project revelation changes the calculus. Two concurrent projects means Blizzard is investing seriously in the franchise for the first time since Legacy of the Void in 2015. The ARG escalation on the shooter side suggests a playable demo or closed test announcement is possible.

HotS is the wildcard. The Classic Cup match is confirmed, but whether it's a farewell tour or a revival launch depends entirely on what happens on stage. Console port rumors remain unconfirmed — treat them as speculation until Blizzard says otherwise.
