The November 19 WoW Forever class podcast covered more than Best-in-Slot gear. Warcraft Tavern's report of episode 8 also covers a defence of unusual loot and a plan for the two crate-turn-in factions. Both are worth separating from the BiS story, because they affect what drops you keep and what reputation you grind.

As with our [BiS article](/news/forever-podcast-class-design-nov-19/), the first-party post confirms the episode, its guests and its topic. The remarks below come from Tavern's reporting and are attributed as such.

## "Optimisation is the enemy of fun"

Senior Game Designer **Aidan Moon**, also known as Zirene, defended items with unusual stats and effects. Tavern places the discussion at about the **14:12** mark and quotes him saying:

> Optimisation, to some degree, is the enemy of fun with itemization.

His argument is that the developers already know which stats players want. They could fill every loot table with straightforward upgrades, but then nobody would look at those items again except to compare the next +1. Unusual stats and flavourful effects are how the team tells stories through gear.

He also teased an unreleased item that might be "master looter BiS", because it increases your stats when your bags are completely full. That is a joke with a serious point: an item whose value comes from a situation, not from a stat line.

## Merchant's Favor: grind it now

Principal Designer **Kris Zierhut** addressed the **Azeroth Commerce Authority** and **Durotar Supply and Logistics**, the two factions fed by Waylaid Crate turn-ins. Tavern reports that he acknowledged players find the different reputation methods cumbersome and confusing, and that the team will meet to discuss improvements. He also said they are looking into possible bugs with crate turn-ins.

The practical part is this:

> The developers plan to add more recipes to these factions in every future patch.

If that holds, the reputation is worth building now rather than later. The factions already gate patterns, and a new recipe each patch means the grind keeps paying out. Tavern notes that Zierhut described the factions as important to the economy and professions.
