Warcraft Tavern reports a Hunter bug in the current WoW Forever client: **Lone Wolf** is applying to pets when it should work the other way round.

The talent is meant to give the Hunter a **20 percent damage increase while no pet is active**. The report says it is instead boosting the pet itself, and that the interaction is being heavily used in both open-world play and battlegrounds.

We are publishing this as a community report. There is no Blizzard note naming Lone Wolf, no hotfix line, and we have not tested the talent in game.

## Why it matters

Hunter pets are already central to the class in Forever. A talent that should reward petless play and instead powers the pet is a large shift, particularly in PvP where pet damage is easy to see.

Our own earlier coverage recorded a related Lone Wolf change in the beta: a note that the talent no longer applied a negative damage effect while a pet was active. The current report is different, and we are not connecting the two without evidence.

## What we are waiting for

- a Blizzard hotfix line naming Lone Wolf, or
- a first-party acknowledgement in the Known Issues thread.

Until one of those appears, treat the bug as reported rather than confirmed. If you are planning PvP, check the talent's behaviour in game rather than assuming the fix has shipped.
