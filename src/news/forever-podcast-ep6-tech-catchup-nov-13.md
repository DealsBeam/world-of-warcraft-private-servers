Blizzard published **episode 6** of the World of Warcraft: Forever podcast on **October 29**, six days before launch. The episode is titled **"All About Tech"**.

The panel is host **Countdown To Classic** and special guest **Shobek**, with **Senior Game Designer Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield**, **Lead Software Engineer Ana Resendez** and **Principal Software Engineer Frank Williams**. Blizzard's article says they "talk tech" and that the episode offers "insights from the development team" about Forever arriving on November 4.

We did not publish a brief for this episode when it went out; our podcast coverage went from episode 5 on October 22 to episode 7 on November 12. This note fills that gap rather than leaving it silent. We have not reviewed the episode, so it records who appeared and how Blizzard describes it, not the technical claims made in the conversation.
