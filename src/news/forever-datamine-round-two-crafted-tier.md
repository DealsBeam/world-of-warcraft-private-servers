September 19, 2026 — the second datamine wave after [round one](/news/forever-beta-datamine-round-one/) is about crafted power. Icy Veins' Anshlun found **Artisan tier sets** in the beta files: crafted pieces (shoulders, boots, leggings, gloves per set, with entries for six slots) that grow with phases. The economy around them is the story.

- **Raid loot feeds crafting.** Each piece costs 3 **Malleable Essences of Nature**, obtained only by disenchanting items from the two launch raids, Hyjal Summit and Barrow Deeps. Raiders supply crafters by definition.
- **One piece at first, two later.** Only one crafted tier item is equippable at launch, so no set bonus is reachable on day one. The **Guardians of Hyjal** reputation raises the limit to two, unlocking 2-piece bonuses.
- **Bind on Equip.** The finished pieces are BoE, so the market, not the raid roster, decides who wears them (per community dataminer Stiven's screenshots).

The bonuses themselves read like Classic design with new hooks: shared templates across classes (Spirit, Defense, hit, haste 2-pieces; Barkskin, Rebirth-in-form, totem-radius 3-pieces) plus flavor like Hibernate castable in Cat Form and Life Tap generating 20% more mana. Full per-class tables live at Icy Veins; treat every number as beta-subject-to-change, same as round one.

What this means: Blizzard is building a real raid-to-crafting pipeline at launch, not a side hustle. Disenchanters become supply chain, Hyjal rep becomes a second progression axis, and the one-piece gate keeps crafted tier from replacing raid loot on day one.

SOURCES: Icy Veins Anshlun (crafted tier datamine, Sep 18); Stiven screenshots (BoE confirmation).
