September 14, 2026 — Diablo 4's **Season of Hell's Legacy (Season 15)** starts tomorrow, September 15. Roundup of the launch-eve picture, per Duskforge's BlizzCon-week coverage (fan site; Blizzard patch notes remain the authority where they differ).

## TL;DR

- **Launch:** tomorrow, Sep 15. PSN patch shipped a day early and is erroring for PlayStation players (per Duskforge)
- **Season Blessings:** Smoldering Ashes boost salvage materials through Glyph upgrades
- **Horadric Cube rework:** reroll affixes, protect Greater Affixes, convert a perfect Unique into a Mythic
- **Difficulties:** Expert and Penitent unlock without finishing the campaign; higher Torment tiers grant more XP
- **Mythic crafting:** Iconic Mythics stay Mythic-only after PTR feedback; new Blacksmith cache targets them directly
- **Rewards:** free bear pet via Season Ranks, plus Mythic caches, Sparks, 125 objectives
- **Theme:** Dark Wanderer transformation, nearly 20 classic bosses and heroes back, Deckard Cain returns, Prime Evils loose

- Whether the PSN early-patch errors are resolved before the season opens

## Bottom line

Hell's Legacy is a systems-heavy season (Cube, Blessings, difficulties, crafting) wrapped in a nostalgia theme. The mechanics affect every build; the Dark Wanderer wrapper is the hook. Blizzard also confirmed development continues past Season 15 — Season 16 and further seasons are already planned despite the Diablo 5 announcement (per BlizzardWatch).
