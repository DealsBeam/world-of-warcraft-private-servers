September 12, 2026 — Overwatch's BlizzCon slate centers on **Doctrine**, Hero 54: a vampire-inspired Support and Doomfist ally, playable in a trial through September 14 before joining the roster in Season 5.

## TL;DR

- **Doctrine** (Support): lifeforce manipulation via scepter, infusions, bat-like drones. Trial live through Sep 14.
- **Watchpoint: Grimsvötn**: new escort map, Icelandic volcano prison, key to the Reign of Talon finale.
- **Sombra** full rework, Damage → Support. **Roadhog** major update. **Hero 55** teased for 2027.
- **BlizzCon Mythic Gift**: play one game after ceremony, redeem one full Mythic bundle from Seasons 1–14 (valid through Oct 5).
- Season 5 launches **October 6**. Spotlight returns February 2027 with full-year roadmap.

## What it means

Support keeps absorbing reworks (Sombra's role swap is the headline for queue health), and the Reign of Talon arc closes with its two leads sharing a map. The Mythic Gift is the most generous OW drop in years — one free bundle of choice reads as a lapsed-player winback timed to Season 5.

Live side notes: LE SSERAFIM and YOASOBI performances plus the Matt Mercer QuestWatch TTRPG one-shot round out the weekend. Ceremony schedule in the [preview](/news/blizzcon-2026-preview/).
