Blizzard's **3.3.0 PTR notes** confirm that **Tyrael's Boons** will be available to all seasonal Diablo 4 players in Season 16, without owning the Lord of Hatred expansion. The line is explicit:

> All Seasonal players can access Tyrael's Boons; owning the Lord of Hatred expansion is not required.

That makes the new War Plans tree an exception to the normal system, which is tied to the expansion.

## What the tree gives you

Tyrael's Boons is a season-themed War Plans tree that tailors rewards and activities. Blizzard's notes say you can earn up to **132 points**, enough to choose **4 of 6 available selections within each of the four paths**. The Season 16 paths are:

| Path | What it does |
|---|---|
| Curious Addition | Changes the Purveyor of Curiosities for a second item, new item types, or higher quality. One option makes the Purveyor expensive but raises Ancestral odds. |
| Merchant of Mystery | Adds a new merchant at Torment difficulty selling caches for Forgotten Souls. Points increase Ancestral cache chances and can change the currency. |
| Scent of Gold | More gold and experience, more Horadric Cube materials and Gem Fragments, better monster gold and gem drops, higher Legendary chance. |
| Trash for Treasure | Cheaper Alchemist transmuting, more Forgotten Souls, more Obducite and Scrolls of Restoration, and the Greater Temper recipe. |

The tree does **not** replace Season Blessings. Those become static, with the same five on offer each time, and the two systems combine.

Respeccing costs **5,000,000 gold per point**, which Blizzard states in the notes.

## The new vendor, explained

Merchant of Mystery is the path that matters most for gearing. It unlocks a new vendor who sells caches for **Forgotten Souls** at Torment difficulty. Duskforge reports that the vendor is **Master Mercer**, found in **Temis and Gea Kul** once you reach Torment and spend a point in the path.

That name and location come from Duskforge's report, not the Blizzard notes. The first-party note only says "a new merchant at Torment difficulty selling caches for Forgotten Souls". Treat the specifics as reported.

The same report says cache slots cost **10, 20 or 40 Forgotten Souls** on the PTR, depending on type, and that a cache gives a focused set of possible rewards rather than a guaranteed item. Those are PTR numbers and may change on December 1.

Two upgrades shape the stock: **Blessed Antiquities** raises the chance of Ancestral Archetype Caches by 0.4 percent per rank up to 10 percent, and **Stuff of Legends** adds another cache slot per rank. Both come from Duskforge's reading of the PTR.

## The limits for base-game players

Access to the tree is not the same as access to every reward. Blizzard's own notes attach Lord of Hatred content to individual lines: some Purveyor options add Horadric Seals or Runes, both tied to the expansion.

The tree is also **seasonal only**. Duskforge reports it is not available on Eternal realms. We could not find that restriction stated in the 3.3.0 notes, so we are carrying it as a report rather than a confirmed fact.
