Blizzard published a fresh hotfix pass at **03:23 UTC on November 18**, which is the evening of November 17 in Pacific time. The running article was retitled **"WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 17, 2026"**, and Kaivax's matching forum post followed a minute later. This is the second pass in as many days, after [the November 16 set](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-16/).

The headline change is a reversal of an accidental nerf. The pass also gives Paladin tanks a new primary stat on their upgraded Dungeon Set, repairs a Legacy perk, and touches nearly every profession.

## Upper Blackrock Spire is harder again

Blizzard says it corrected an issue that made enemy health and damage in Upper Blackrock Spire **significantly lower than intended**:

> Corrected an issue causing enemy health and damage to be significantly lower than intended.
>
> Developers' notes: This should bring UBRS back in line with its intended difficulty, and it again requires a full complement of diligent players to complete.

That developer note is unusually direct. The low values were not a design choice, and the intended version expects a full, coordinated group. If you ran UBRS while the bug was live, that version is gone.

## Paladin tanks get Spell Power

The item section changes the stat on **Soulforge Armor**, described as the upgraded Dungeon Set for tanks, from **Strength to Spell Power**:

> Soulforge Armor (upgraded Dungeon Set for tanks) now has Spell Power (was Strength).
>
> Developers' notes: We are leaning more into Paladin tanks favoring Spell Power as their primary stat for dealing damage.

Two other item fixes are worth noting. **Whiteout Staff** and **Crackling Staff** now share the same stats: **25 Stamina, 25 Intellect and 74 Spell Power from weapon damage**. **Escape Plan** has correct range and damage values, and Shadow Priests can now hold a **Night Watchman's Torch**.

## The two world buffs behave properly

The PvP-facing lines from the pass are now in the notes: **Might of Stormwind** and **Warchief's Blessing** should always cancel on death, and they are no longer applied to non-players. Blizzard also fixed a bug that let players mount in the starting rooms of Warsong Gulch, and made the Battle for Alterac Valley quests require **level 58** to accept.

For context, we had flagged the rally-buff change as unverified in the morning brief. It is now confirmed by Blizzard's own post.

## Camping: the Cozy Sleeping Bag

The camping fixes are all about one object. **Mounted players can no longer sleep while sitting atop a Cozy Sleeping Bag**, the bag can now be placed in specific locations like hunter traps, and it provides a **Resting aura** showing when you are inside its effect.

A separate Shaman line may matter in groups: **Flurry no longer incorrectly loses stacks when Windfury totem attacks land**. That follows the Windfury cleanup in the November 16 pass.

## Legacy: Reagent Economy repaired

**Reagent Economy** should no longer randomly stop working. That perk removes the vendor reagent cost from class abilities and makes Tier 1 camp features free, so the bug had been quietly draining gold from characters that had spent a point on it.

## Professions

- **Waylaid Crate** conversions no longer reward an obsolete turn-in item. Players already affected can turn those crates in for the intended reward.
- The Blacksmithing quest **"The Old Ways"** now grants the **Orcish War Leggings** recipe through an item. Players who missed it or lost it can get a replacement from **Sumi** in the nearby blacksmithing hut after completing the quest.
- **Bronze Mace** is now **Bind on Equip** rather than unbound, which also allows it to be added to the Transmog Collection.
- **Craftsman's Writ: Golden Scale Gauntlets** now points at the correct completion NPC.
- Herbalism: **Cultivation** now works on Mageroyal, and wildflowers for **"Her World"** are no longer tracked by Find Herb unless you are on the quest.
- Skinning: skinners can no longer skin a few creatures that were not meant to be skinnable.
- Various **Paletusks** can no longer be pickpocketed.

## Quests and zones

A long set of quest repairs landed, concentrated in the new zones:

- **Shen'dralas:** repairing pylons for **"Powering the Pylons"** is shared with nearby party members; the **Depleted Crystal Heart** for **"Heart of Crystal"** now drops for every player in the party; **Rokren's** escape speed in **"Going Home"** is reduced; miners can track **Spectral Iron Ore** and its spawn rate is increased; Bristleback Skullcrushers and Decrepit Pylon Protectors cast less often; the necromancer summon of Undead Ravagers can be interrupted.
- **"Agamaggan's Blood"** is no longer flagged as a dungeon quest and can be completed in the elite area outside Razorfen Downs.
- **"Show Them How It's Done"** now appears on the map.
- Wetlands has more **Fen Creepers** for **"Blisters on the Land"**, and more **Gravelysnout Diggers and Surveyors** for **"A New Ore Sample"**.
- **"Step One: Rock"** rockpiles are tracked with Find Minerals instead of Find Treasure.
- Rogues can get the expected letter from **Mathias Shaw** in SI:7 for **"The Manor, Ravenholdt"**.
- Thousand Needles fixes stop **Pesterhide Snarlers, Needles Cougars, Gravelysnout Diggers and Surveyors** spawning or pathing inside rocks.

The UI section fixes ignore lists not persisting through logout and a bug with the Master Swordsmith, Master Axesmith and Master Hammersmith screens.

## What is still outstanding

Two items from earlier rounds are still not in the notes:

- **The Auction House changes.** Blizzard pulled the 1-copper minimum deposit, the unread-mail display fix and the mail-delivery timing fix from the November 11 notes and said they would go live with the **November 17** weekly maintenance. Some third-party sites now describe them as shipped. At our read on **November 18 after the new pass**, the running article still has no Auction House line, so we are not reporting them as live.
- **Item Restoration.** The November 6 disabling note is still the only entry. Nothing in the November 16 or November 17 passes restores it.

The live client remains **1.60.1.70959**. The CDN published a new manifest for that same version on November 18, so there is no new build to pair with these notes.
