September 20, 2026: the first player-made dungeon guide for WoW Forever is out. Warcraft Tavern's Val Hull [mapped the Hall of Thanes](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/how-to-find-the-hall-of-thanes-dungeon-quests/) on foot: the Alliance 13-18 dungeon (Ragefire Chasm's counterpart) sits **under Ironforge at 43.5/52.0**, through a spiderweb corridor left of Magni's throne room.

The details that matter for runners:

- **Split path:** upstairs leads to a quest giver, downstairs to the dungeon across lava that kills with no escape but resurrection. Bridge NPC plus entrance at the end.
- **Five quests:** Thom Filch (8 heirlooms), Afadra Dunwall (15 apparitions + 10 souls), a hidden chain starting with Earthseer Farsen in Dun Morogh (64.8/58.5) via a Dark Iron Map drop, Ghostly Attendant inside (kill Faldrim Anvilmar), and a Reliquary tablet that just wants a Magni turn-in.
- **Rewards are real loot**, not placeholders: Dwarven Tome, Tomb Robber's Gloves, Catacomb Cloak, Deepgrave Trousers, and a three-way weapon pick (gun, 2H mace, wand) from the Magni turn-in.

Why this is tracker-relevant: it is the first confirmation that Forever's new dungeons ship with full quest scaffolding (breadcrumb chains, hidden starters, in-dungeon pickups), not just boss rooms. The hub's [dungeon table](/classic-plus/#dungeons) carries brackets; this is the first feet-on-the-ground layer on top.
