Diablo 4 Season 16 gets its Developer Update livestream on **November 24 at 11:00 AM PT**. Ahead of it, Duskforge has collected the Season 15 secrets that are still open. Nothing below has been confirmed by Blizzard, and we are reporting it as a list of loose ends rather than as a preview of what the stream will say.

## The Chat Gem codes

The Chat Gem has been producing numeric codes in **21 Strongholds** across Sanctuary. Each message begins with **41** followed by six digits. Duskforge reports that investigators believe the numbers may point to pages, lines and words in a book, though a different cipher or something hidden inside the game are also possible.

The item itself is a **Mythic Gem** that can be socketed into jewelry to activate the Chat Gem. Its in-game description says its effect is "nearly imperceptible".

## The Standard of Heroes

Defeating the three Prime Evils in the hidden **Uber Tristram** encounter awards the Standard of Heroes. The open question is whether it is just a trophy or an input to something else.

## The Map of the Stars

The purpose of the Map of the Stars has not been established. It is a known item from the season with no confirmed use beyond what players have already found.

## The Cow Level may have another step

The secret Cow Level content is a recurrent part of Season 15. Duskforge suggests there may be another step that has not been found. No evidence for it is given beyond the pattern of previous secrets.

## "More whimsy"

The final loose end is Blizzard's own habit of adding small, strange discoveries. Duskforge lists it as a category rather than a specific clue.

## What to expect on November 24

Blizzard's first-party announcement confirms the stream will cover the new seasonal quest, a Seasonal Lair boss fight with two bosses instead of one, the new War Plans trees, Horadric Items, activity tree updates and class balance, followed by a Q&A. It does not mention any of the Season 15 secrets.

So the honest framing is that the stream is a place where answers *could* appear, not a promise that they will. We will update this page if any of the five are resolved on air.
