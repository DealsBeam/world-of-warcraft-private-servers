September 17, 2026 — a community item database for Project Ascension's dead Bronzebeard realm surfaced at **bronzebeard.bendo.lol**: 12,874 items including the realm's custom Worldforged and Bloodforged gear, 83 Mystic Scrolls, and a phase-3 snapshot (Blackwing Lair and Zul'Gurub open, Ahn'Qiraj next). The footer is explicit: "Unofficial community mirror · Not affiliated with Project Ascension or Blizzard Entertainment."

## What it is

A browsable item DB (12,874 total items, 1,633 Worldforged, 1,642 Bloodforged-eligible, 83 Mystic Scrolls) drawn from Ascension's **Bronzebeard (Warcraft Reborn)** realm — the OG-nine-classes rebuild with Mystic Enchantments that shut down with the rest of the network on [September 4](/news/ascension-shutdown-sep-4/). Custom items carry a "Custom" tag and the mirror shows the realm's phase state: "Classic · Phase 3 — Blackwing Lair & Zul'Gurub open · Next: Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj, Temple of Ahn'Qiraj."

## Where it fits

The preservation wave keeps growing. The [Ascension Archive](/news/ascension-archive-opens/) (our September 15 piece) collects WDB and addon data from former players with a browsable explorer at ascension-db.ascension-archive.workers.dev — still the primary data source. Bronzebeard DB is different in kind: it is a **curated mirror of realm items** — Worldforged/Bloodforged rolls, Mystic Scrolls, phase progression — the kind of snapshot that dies with the realm otherwise.

## Honest flags

- **Unofficial and self-reported.** Single-preserver community work on a personal domain; item counts, phase state and "Custom" labels are the mirror's own.
- **Snapshot, not live.** The realm is [dead](/news/ascension-shutdown-sep-4/); the DB preserves, it does not update.
- Worth checking before trusting any single entry: cross-reference against the Ascension Archive's recovered datasets and the [CoA AzerothCore port](/news/ascension-classes-azerothcore-port/) where items matter.
