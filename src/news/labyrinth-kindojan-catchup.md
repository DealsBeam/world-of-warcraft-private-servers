Blizzard's **November 20 WoW Weekly** adds a catch-up path for the Labyrinth of Kindo'jan, the retail WoW activity we first covered in October. The new detail is that **Wah'du** hands out skipped chamber quests in sequence.

> Have you missed a few weeks? No worries, Wah'du has you covered. He'll hand out each skipped quest, one after another, so you can wrap up several in a single visit and get right back on pace.

Each weekly chamber quest pays **1 Mythical Soul Fragment** and **2,000 Undercoin**. Clearing only the chambers Wah'du assigns, in his order, earns enough fragments to claim **any two pieces of Myth 2/6 gear** from Wah'du, Servant of de Loa.

## How to unlock the Labyrinth

Blizzard repeats the entry requirements in the same post:

- players who completed the campaign quests are already unlocked;
- otherwise, go north of **Amani Pass in Eversong Woods** and take **"The Long Way Around"** from **Liadrin**, or go to **Tokka's Landing** and accept **"The Labyrinth"** from **Kul'amara the Fierce**;
- complete the **Chamber of Rites** with **Valeera Sanguinar** and speak with **Kinduru** to unlock the full Labyrinth for the entire Warband.
