Jez Corden interviewed lead game designer **Tim Jones** and lead software engineer **Nora Valleta** at BlizzCon 2026 and published it September 22. It is the first long-form developer interview of the beta period, and it is the source that resolves or sharpens several claims we have been carrying as single-report or second-hand.

## Class viability, stated plainly

Jones: "**Every single one of our 27 talent trees is now viable for all content in WoW: Forever.** That's one big class design goal. That doesn't mean they're all the same ... or all equal. They have strengths and weaknesses. But they're all worthy to bring. None are so powerful that you'll stack your raid with them either."

This is the same claim the Kotaku and The Sun interviews carried in paraphrase. Jones stating it as a completed goal, in his own words, upgrades the grade. The qualifier matters as much as the promise: viable, not equal, and explicitly not stackable. That is a design intent statement, not a balance verification, so beta values still rule.

Valleta added the specific case the old trees failed: "**You can be a Ret Paladin in raids, you can Seal twist.**" Our [hub](/classic-plus/) lists seal twisting under the Paladin rework as a beta-era mechanic. This is the developer naming Retribution as a raid-viable spec, which historically it was not in Wrath.

## Raid delivery and difficulty

Jones: "**We're going to consistently deliver new 10 and 20 player content every patch. Onyxia will be our 40 man raid.**" And on the target: "Classic players enjoy a casual experience, we don't want people to do 100 attempts on a boss. If you've formed the group and done your homework, you should be on the road to success. Over-prepare and crush the content, that's what Classic players want to do."

Valleta on the intended feel: "**the world should be scary ... raids should be difficult. You'll need to prepare, craft potions, make sure you have class diversity and buffs. Get all the gear you can, do reputation grinds. You should need a good amount of research on what bosses do. But ... we're not going to stomp your face into the ground.**"

Two things to carry forward. The "every patch" phrasing is a third independent developer statement of the 10-and-20-per-tier structure, alongside the Kotaku and September 17 Q&A versions. And "Onyxia will be our 40 man raid" is unqualified on timing, which is a fourth data point pointing at launch rather than December 9, but it still does not name a date, so the [first-party conflict on the hub](/classic-plus/#raids) stays open.

## Mid-level zones are a stated plan

This is the most consequential new claim in the interview for anyone reasoning about the long-term shape of the game. Jones: "**We might add an update where we put in a new zone designed not for level 60, but for level 27 to 32 for example, with a new dungeon. The best way to experience that would be to make a new character. It's not all about level 60, we're going to serve across all levels, as well as awesome level 60 endgame content too.**"

Our hub currently describes four new regions shipping alongside the level 1-60 world. Jones is describing a different, later cadence: post-launch updates that add sub-cap zones and dungeons, with the explicit design intent that a fresh character is the intended way to play them. That is a direct answer to why the [Legacy system](/blog/forever-legacy-camping-systems-explained/) exists, and it is a roadmap item the official 2026-27 graphic does not spell out. "Might," "for example" and "we're going to" in the same answer means treat it as direction, not schedule.

## Player housing and idea borrowing

Jones on retail borrowing from Classic: "**Retail now has World Bosses inside of a Lair for example now, we borrow ideas from each other. I could see them borrowing the camping system from us very easily, and perhaps connect it to Player Housing?**" And on his own wish: "**I would love some day to maybe take Player Housing and bring it into the Classic world.**"

Housing is a Midnight 12.1.5 retail system. Jones naming it as a future Classic goal, and Camping as the most likely bridge, is a designer preference stated on the record. It is not a roadmap commitment and should not be written into one.

## Lore: adjacent timelines, and a name we had wrong

Valleta on continuity: "**This is a new continuity. We're starting from the same place. Think of adjacent timelines. Some things might be a bit different ... we're not going to do something that is super divergent, like, stopping Arthas from becoming the Lich King for example. Or that Jaina is secretly a Dreadlord ... but who knows?**"

On filling gaps in the existing map: "**The same philosophy applies to how we'll expand continents. The new zone River Glades for example, the landmass existed in the classic map ... but there was nothing there. It was an opportunity to fill in those spaces and complete those stories too.**" And on dead space in old zones: "**What was that weird gate in Azshara all that time, for example? That's now Timbermaw Hold, right? What's inside Old Ironforge?**"

That last quote matters for a naming conflict we have been carrying. Blizzard's own recap names nine dungeons and the furbolg city is **Blackmaw Hold** in Azshara in the beta data, while Inven Global's coverage called the same dungeon **Timbermaw Hold**. Jones naming Timbermaw Hold for the Azshara gate, in an interview, moves the conflict but does not settle it: the sentence is phrased as a question to the reporter, and no official dungeon list has been published. Both names stay visible on the [hub](/classic-plus/#dungeons) with the conflict flagged.

Valleta also confirmed the Skyborne's narrative job: the race tells the story of Eldre'Thalas and Dire Maul, ground Blizzard had not previously fleshed out.

## What is still open

None of this supplies a Server Slam date, a level-30 activation date, or a resolution to the Onyxia launch-versus-December-9 conflict. The Onyxia statement narrows the range of plausible readings without naming a date. The mid-level zone plan has no timing. Housing is a stated wish. The Timbermaw and Blackmaw naming needs an official list. All four stay on the [verification hit list](/news/forever-beta-verification-hit-list/).
