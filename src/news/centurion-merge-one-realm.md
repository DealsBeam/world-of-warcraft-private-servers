September 19, 2026: [Centurion](/servers/centurion/) is one realm now. Per the project's September 18 news posts, **Legionnaire+ and Barracks+ merged into a single realm called Centurion**, and existing characters came with them. The two-realms structure the tracker listed since September 1 is gone; the entry is merged to match.

The merge replaces the old PvP-realm/PvE-realm split with two character types on one account: **world characters** (level 1 to 60, the long run) and **tournament characters** (start at 60, straight to fighting). Three more systems landed in the same batch of posts:

- **Cooldowns wait outside.** Entering a battleground sets world cooldowns aside; you start the match clean, and they return on exit, win, loss, early leave or disconnect alike.
- **Tiered battleground AI.** The bots that backfill quiet lobbies are now sorted into three tiers instead of dealt at random, so thin hours stop producing formality-or-wall lobbies.
- **Gurubashi chest on a timer.** The arena chest spawns on its own clock, needs one real player present to appear, and despawns five minutes later whether taken or not.

The PvP ladder stays public, with the top names past 5,000 lifetime kills, consistent with a small but genuinely played realm. Our [Centurion guide](/blog/centurion-guide/) is updated for the merged structure.
