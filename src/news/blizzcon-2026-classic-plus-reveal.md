September 12, 2026 — Classic Plus is real, and it is called **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Blizzard closed the worst-kept secret in WoW at the BlizzCon opening ceremony: an alternate-timeline Vanilla branch, made by the Classic team, launching **November 4** with a beta starting **this Thursday**.

## TL;DR

- **Name:** World of Warcraft: Forever. Year-1 Vanilla setting, cap 60 "will always be" 60, content for all levels.
- **New:** 4 named regions (Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, Riverglades, Zephras Isle), overhauled zones filling blank vanilla map space, 1000+ new quests, 9 named dungeons, 2 named raids (20-person Hyjal Summit, 10-person Barrow Deeps), new 15v15 Darkspear Islands battleground.
- **New race:** Skyborne — Horde-aligned get Shaman, Alliance-aligned get Mage; shared Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid.
- **Systems:** Legacy alt progression, outdoor Camping (shared campfire buffs), opt-in Transmog, SD/HD character models, restored Classic animations, **official gamepad support**. No flying, no level scaling.
- **Roadmap:** launch November 4, first new raids December 9, then more raids, dungeons, PvP, world content, quests — and **Hardcore**. Beta Thursday.
- Playable on the show floor now. Live dev Q&A Thursday 10:00 AM PT.
- Our [Warcraft Forever name-trail post](/blog/warcraft-forever-triple-signal/) called the title; the [dwarf shaman clues post](/news/camelot-dwarf-shaman-clues/) called the combo.

## What was announced

Two design pillars: the world is a main character, and the journey matters more than the destination. "We are putting the war back in Warcraft: armies, battles, bloodshed, survival," said executive producer Holly Longdale. The What's Next panel (Ion Hazzikostas, Tim Jones, Nora Mills, Clay Stone) went deeper:

**The world.** Four new regions. Mount Hyjal (post-Archimonde recovery, Darkwhisper Gorge), Shen'dralas (a hidden zone between Mulgore and Desolace tied to the Shen'dralar, Eldre'Thalas, Dire Maul, and the centaur tribes), Riverglades (a mid-30s to mid-40s frontier with 150+ quests), and Zephras Isle (an elemental skybound 1-12 starting experience with Skywall-inspired architecture).

**Race split.** Skyborne choose a side at creation: Horde-aligned embrace elemental tradition and get Shaman; Alliance-aligned pursue arcane legacy and get Mage. Both can play Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, and Druid — with new Skyborne druid forms, racials, customizations, voices, and dances. Beta access and the race/Zephras start ship in the pre-purchase packs; launch access itself just needs a subscription.

**Dungeons and raids.** The nine dungeons: Hall of Thanes (under Ironforge), Ruins of Lordaeron, the Whelgar's Excavation site, City of Dalaran, Blackmaw Hold, Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, and Shaper's Terrace. Raids: **Hyjal Summit** (20-player) and **Barrow Deeps** (max-level 10-player), with new tier sets and a legendary reward teased. The Darkspear Islands PvP battleground is a 15v15 mix of Arathi Basin-style flags and control points off Kalimdor's coast.

**Systems.** Legacy progression for alt leveling, an outdoor Camping system (craft campfires, share buffs through professions), opt-in Transmog, HD/SD character models with restored Classic animations, and official gamepad support. Design constraints stated plainly: no flying mounts, no level scaling, with "thoughtful" convenience because QoL choices shape how players meet.

**The roadmap.** Launch November 4, 3:00 PM PST. **First new raids unlock December 9.** After that: more raids, dungeons, PvP updates, world content, additional level-60 quests, **Hardcore**, and a revamped iconic raid. "This is not a mode, a season, or a new version of Classic. It is a promise to keep building this world with the community."

Forever's tale begins after the Forsaken Kingdom campaign and before Molten Core — player choices in the RTS carry echo into the MMO, which opens with a neutral Forsaken Paladin hero unit.

## FAQ

**Is this a replacement for Era/Anniversary?**
No — a separate branch. Era stays museum, Anniversary stays progression, Forever diverges.

**How do beta and pricing work?**
Beta access requires pre-purchase of the Skyborne Epic Pack or Warcraft Forever Collection. Tiers per Blizzard: Skyborne Heroic (race + Zephras Isle start, name reservation, Cerulean Prideclaw mount, cosmetics, housing decor, 1 Invite-A-Friend code), Epic (adds beta, 30 days game time, pets, tabards, 3 codes), Collection (adds WC3 Reforged + Forsaken Kingdom + housing figurines, through Jan 11 2027). Physical Collector's Edition via Gear Store. At launch November 4 (3:00 PM PST), an active subscription alone grants access — cap 60 "will always be" 60. Live dev Q&A Thursday September 17, 10:00 AM PT — same day beta opens.

**Do I need to buy it?**
No — access is included with a WoW subscription or Game Time at launch (November 4, 3:00 PM PST). The packs are optional upgrades that add the Skyborne race, Zephras Isle start, and beta access.

**What about the demo?**
Playable on the BlizzCon show floor all weekend.
