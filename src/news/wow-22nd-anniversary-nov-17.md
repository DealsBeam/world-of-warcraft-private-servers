World of Warcraft's **22nd Anniversary** is running from **November 17 to December 8, 2026**, in the Caverns of Time in Tanaris. Blizzard's first-party announcement gives the dates, the buff stack and the reward structure.

## The buff, and how to reach 20 percent

The event grants an automatic **10 percent experience and reputation buff**. A repeatable daily quest, **"Celebrate Good Fun!"** from **Yllana** in Tanaris, adds another 10 percent, for a total of **20 percent**.

Blizzard says the anniversary buff stacks with other XP and reputation buffs, and gives these examples:

- **+20 percent** XP and reputation from the anniversary event;
- **+25 percent** XP from the Five Warband Mentors: Midnight achievement;
- **+15 percent** XP and gold from War Mode;
- **+10 percent** from the Draught of Ten Lands potion, for characters level 49 and lower.

Getting to the event is by portal from Orgrimmar or Stormwind, or by picking up the quest from any Holiday Celebrant.

## Rewards are tracked Warband-wide

Quests and activity completions are tracked **Warband-wide**, so they can be done on any character and count toward the weekly amount of Warband Transferable Tokens. Blizzard's note is explicit that there is no need to run the event on multiple characters each week.

The cosmetics include the **Loyal Watchman** ensemble, with helm, gauntlets, trousers, cuirass, spaulders, belt and boots. Currencies include **Timewarped Badges**, earned from Timewalking events.
