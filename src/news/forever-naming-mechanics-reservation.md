September 21, 2026: the policy ([beta bans](/news/forever-beta-name-enforcement/)) now has its mechanics. Warcraft Tavern's Luxrah [recaps Blizzard's naming post](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/naming-guidelines-early-name-reservation-in-wow-forever/) (official article 24304161):

- **Both names required**, but the secondary hides in the world via Nameplates options while staying visible in chat.
- **Filters evaluate both names together.** Splitting a restricted word across first and last names does not evade them.
- **Family and guild surnames work**: shared second names (Brightvale-style) as long as each full combination is region-unique.
- **Reservation: October 27 through November 3**, upgrades only, first-come first-served, random generator as fallback.

The design intent is explicit: two-part names solve mega-realm availability while Familiar Faces makes names worth remembering. Enforcement plus mechanics, same week, same system.

**Update September 25:** Blizzard's [official account-action guidance](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360739) now confirms forced name changes and beta-access removal for severe naming-policy violations. The development team says actions are individually reviewed, and Customer Service cannot assist with appeals for this enforcement wave.
