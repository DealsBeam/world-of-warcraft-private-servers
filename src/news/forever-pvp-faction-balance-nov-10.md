title: "Forever throttles character creation on the crowded PvP faction"
date: 2026-11-11

game: wow
summary: "Kaivax's November 10 post says the most lopsided region sits near 55/45, so character creation now tracks regional faction balance and makes the larger side harder to join, currently Horde on North American PvP and Alliance on EU Roleplay-PvP."
---

Blizzard is steering new characters away from the larger faction on Forever's PvP rulesets. In a November 10 forum post, Community Manager Kaivax said the character creation system now reads the overall state of faction balance in each region instead of what he called "snapshots of points in time."

The practical effect is that it becomes **increasingly difficult to create a character on the more populous faction** in a region. Blizzard names the current cases as **Horde on North American PvP rulesets** and **Alliance on EU Roleplay-PvP**. It also says those restrictions may flip to the other side as populations move.

The stated target is a **50/50 split**. Kaivax puts the most extreme region at roughly **55/45**, which he says is not close enough.

## What the change does and does not cover

The post is framed around PvP rulesets, and it describes an adjustment to the **character creation system**. It does not describe restrictions on PvE rulesets, and it does not say existing characters are affected.

Blizzard acknowledges the friction directly, calling the restriction frustrating for players who want a specific faction in that moment, and a "necessary trade-off" for the long-term health of the ruleset while guilds and rivalries are still forming.

## The thread is not convinced

The announcement drew 859 posts by our November 11 read. A recurring response disputes the official ratio: players say contested leveling zones feel far more lopsided than 55/45, and some allege that trial accounts or abandoned characters inflate the smaller faction's count. Others ask for incentives, free faction changes or layering changes instead of creation limits.

Blizzard answered two of those points in the thread. Kaivax said the team investigated the trial-account claim and found "no evidence that trial characters had any impact at all," and that the 55/45 figure counts the active logged-in population across all levels over a specific period. He also pointed to a congestion change made earlier on November 10, which lowered player population in crowded zones, with a bigger reduction on the PvP ruleset.

The remaining complaints are player claims, not Blizzard statements, and we are not treating the 55/45 figure as independently measured. What is first-party is the policy and Blizzard's definition of the ratio: creation on the larger faction is throttled by region, and the named cases are Horde on NA PvP and Alliance on EU Roleplay-PvP.

## What it means if you are starting now

If you are rolling on a PvP or RP-PvP ruleset, check faction availability before you commit to a server with friends. The post says the restriction can move, so a faction that is open today may not be tomorrow, and the reverse is also true. Blizzard's own framing is that the ruleset is healthiest when both sides can fill a fight.
