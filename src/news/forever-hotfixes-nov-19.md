Blizzard published a new hotfix pass at **04:00 UTC on November 20**, which is the evening of November 19 in Pacific time. The running article was retitled **"WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 19, 2026"**, and Kaivax's matching forum post followed a minute later.

The headline change is the return of the **Seed Hybridizer**, with conditions. The pass also resolves one of the two undocumented changes we reported yesterday, leaves the other unresolved, and fixes a long tail of dungeon, quest and racial bugs.

## Seed Hybridizer returns, with a failure chance

Three lines cover the system:

- **Seed Hybridizer has been re-enabled.**
- **Seed Hybridization now has adjusted values, as well as a chance of failure when combining seeds.**
- **Scarce Herb Seeds are no longer able to be used in the Seed Hybridizer.**

The failure chance is the important part. Blizzard did not publish the new rates, so we are not guessing at them. What is clear is that combining seeds is no longer guaranteed, and the scarce seeds that players may have been saving cannot be fed into the machine.

## The Paladin talent is now in the notes

Yesterday we reported that data sites had seen an undocumented change to **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** for Retribution Paladins. Blizzard has now documented it:

> Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now correctly increases all Holy Damage you deal while a two-handed weapon is equipped.

That closes half of our report. The other reported change, **Ruzzgot's Shipment** dropping for every party member on "The Big Payback", is still absent from Blizzard's notes. We continue to hold it.

For the full history, including why we separated the two, see [our November 19 report](/news/undocumented-forever-hotfixes-nov-19/).

## Dungeons and enemies

- **City of Dalaran:** Shade of the Archmage should no longer despawn immediately on entering combat.
- **The Shaper's Terrace:** Vitrix's Poison Shock now deals less damage.
- **Shimmering Flats:** a Silithid Hive Drone that could get stuck under the world near the Rustmaul Dig Site has been fixed.
- **Riverglades:** a bug preventing Mosh'gra from ever dropping rare loot has been fixed.

That last line matters for anyone farming the new zone, and it follows the earlier Riverglades fixes in the November 16 pass.

## Items

- **Motes of Unstable Magic** are now soulbound.
- **Squall-breaker's Potion** can now be deleted.

The second is a quality-of-life fix for anyone whose bag was stuck with an item they could not discard.

## Quests and races

- **Booty Bay:** Rix Xizzix should no longer hand out a strange tome after completing "Rift Away".
- **Alterac Mountains:** players can now get a new Ensorcelled Parchment during "Stormpike's Deciphering" if they destroyed the first one.
- **"Hunting for Ectoplasm":** the wrong creature type was being used to gather ectoplasm, now fixed.
- **Skyborne:** the 10 percent Honored reputation discount on Apprentice and Journeyman Riding from Genn Fairweather on Zephras Isle now applies.
- **Tauren:** Cultivation should succeed more reliably on herbs you have the Herbalism skill for, and now works correctly with Purple Lotus.

The Skyborne and Tauren lines are the first racial fixes in a hotfix pass since the November 16 notes.

## What is still outstanding

The Auction House changes promised for the November 17 maintenance and the return of Item Restoration are both still absent from the notes. The live client was **1.60.1.70959** at the time of this pass.

**Update November 21 2026:** a new hotfix pass was published at 00:18 UTC today and the live client moved to **1.60.1.71037**. The Auction House and Item Restoration are still absent. See [the November 20 brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-20/).
