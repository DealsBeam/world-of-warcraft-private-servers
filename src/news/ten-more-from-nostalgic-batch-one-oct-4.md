A second directory crawl, this time nostalgic.gg with **175 World of Warcraft listings** against our 164 entries. After exact and fuzzy matching plus manual review of the ambiguous cases, **94 were genuinely not ours.**

Ninety-four is not a batch, it is a backlog. So this is batch one of it: triaged by the directory's own Discord activity figures, audited first-party, ten added.

## Evaluated and skipped

**WoWMythic** renders a blank page even in a browser: HTTP 200 with nothing in it. There is no project to describe first-party, so there is no entry. **Shadoria** serves "this site is no longer available" in French: a takedown notice, not a server, and with no date it cannot enter as dead either. Both stay out.

Three domains from the triage do not resolve at all (wotlkplus, wowaddicts, nakama-wow). Those are leads for the re-probe pile, not entries.

## The method note that matters

WoWZull publishes **9,920 registered accounts and 20,803 created characters**, and it would be easy to band that as a population. It is not one. Cumulative registrations measure a project's history, and concurrent players measure its present, and a tracker that mixes them is lying by rounding. Same rule as ever: no concurrent figure, no band.

## What remains

Roughly eighty unreviewed slugs from the same directory, plus the re-probe pile. They are queued in the order of the directory's activity figures, highest first, and the next batch follows the same rule: first-party read or no entry.
