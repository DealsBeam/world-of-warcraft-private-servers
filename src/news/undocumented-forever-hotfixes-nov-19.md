A data site is reporting two changes to the live WoW Forever client that Blizzard has not documented. We are publishing the reports with their source and our checks, and we are **not** recording the changes as shipped.

## What is being reported

ForeverChanges says it first saw the changes in the caches the launch realms send, between **20:47 and 20:54 UTC on November 18** and again at **01:23 UTC on November 19**. Its patch notes page lists:

- **Ruzzgot's Shipment**, the quest item for **"The Big Payback"**, now drops for everyone in the party who has the quest.
- **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** for Retribution Paladins now applies to all Physical and Holy damage, and all threat, while a two-handed melee weapon is equipped, not only to the weapon's attacks. The site grades both as buffs.

The site states plainly that Blizzard has posted no notes for them.

## What we checked

- Blizzard's running hotfix article is still titled **"WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 17, 2026"**.
- The matching forum thread's highest post is still **post 12**, timestamped **03:24 UTC on November 18**. No later blue post has appeared.
- Neither "Ruzzgot" nor "Two-Handed Weapon Specialization" appears in Blizzard's article or in the forum thread data we read on November 19.

There is some independent context for the quest item. WarcraftMaps logged a **player** bug report on November 17: two players on "The Big Payback" killing the Venture Co. Agent together could not both loot the package, and the second had to wait about ten minutes for the next spawn. That is a player report, not a Blizzard confirmation, and it does not establish that a fix shipped.

**Update November 20 2026:** Blizzard's November 19 hotfix pass, published at 04:00 UTC today, now documents the Paladin change: "Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now correctly increases all Holy Damage you deal while a two-handed weapon is equipped." That half of the report is resolved. The Ruzzgot's Shipment party-drop change is still absent from Blizzard's notes, so we continue to hold it. See [the November 19 brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-19/).

## Why we are holding

This is the same rule we applied to the Auction House changes. A cache observation is evidence that something changed in the data a realm serves. It is not the same as a first-party note, and we have not tested either change in game.

So the record stays split:

- **Reported by ForeverChanges, November 18 and 19:** two undocumented changes, listed above.
- **Confirmed by Blizzard:** the November 17 hotfix pass, published November 18 at 03:23 UTC, which contains neither line.
- **Our position:** we are not adding the two reported changes to our hotfix history until Blizzard documents them or we verify them in game.

If they are real, they are useful fixes, especially the party-drop change for a quest that players were reporting as broken. That is exactly why it is worth separating what we know from what we have merely read.
