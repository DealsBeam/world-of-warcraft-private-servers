September 21, 2026: the Horde counterpart to yesterday's [Hall of Thanes mapping](/news/forever-hall-of-thanes-mapped/) is documented. Quissy's [Ruins of Lordaeron guide](https://quissy.tv/wow-forever-ruins-of-lordaeron-quests/) counts **10 quest entries** in beta: six Horde, four Alliance, including separate faction versions of Crest of Lordaeron.

Horde runners get full routes: Deathguard Kristof at the Brill camp (kill Rath'mael), Morbin Lightbane in the Royal Quarters (25 Intact Limbs), Tabitha Heartweaver at the Sepulcher (find Edward), Theodore Griffs in the Apothecarium (Highly Toxic Strain from Witherfang, with an unconfirmed prerequisite). Two start inside: Abominable Head loot and the Crest item for Oran Snakewrithe, whose spawn rules are explicitly unconfirmed.

The four Alliance entries (Abominable Creatures, Bloodied Insignia, Remember That I Love You, Alliance Crest) come from beta quest data with pickups unverified, and the guide says so. Objectives and rewards were checked against wowforevertalents.com beta data and Wowhead player reports; quest log levels read 21-22 against 15-16 minimums.

Two-dungeon pattern now visible: HoT had 5 quests, RoL has 10, both mixing outdoor breadcrumbs, in-dungeon starters and faction mirrors. The beta's dungeon quest scaffolding is deeper than the nine-dungeon count suggests.

SOURCES: Quissy RoL quest guide (Sep 2026, with beta-data caveats); wowforevertalents.com; Wowhead player reports.
