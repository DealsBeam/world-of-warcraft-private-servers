Blizzard has refunded the cost of Forever skill purchases that players lost during a server problem on **Monday, November 9**. Kaivax announced the refund in a forum post timestamped **01:36 UTC on November 14**, which is the evening of November 13 in Pacific time; the thread is titled "Lost Training Purchase Refunds - November 13".

## What the post says

> With in-game mail that has now gone out to all affected players, we have refunded the cost of skill purchases that were made on Monday, November 9 between 1:17 p.m. PST and 2:17 p.m. PST (8:17-9:17 a.m. on November 10 AEDT) when the WoW: Forever servers experienced issues that caused some players to lose training they purchased.

> If you were affected by this issue during that timeframe, check your in-game mail for gold equaling the cost of training that you purchased and lost.

The refund is specifically for **training**, meaning the skill purchases made in that window. Blizzard does not mention items, subscriptions, game time or any other loss.

## The window

The eligible window is one hour, and it is given in Pacific time with an Australian conversion:

- **1:17 p.m. to 2:17 p.m. PST on Monday, November 9.**
- **8:17 a.m. to 9:17 a.m. AEDT on Tuesday, November 10.**

PST is UTC-8, so the same window is **21:17 to 22:17 UTC on November 9**. We are adding that conversion for readers outside the two zones Blizzard named; the post itself does not give UTC.

## The same failure, a different remedy

The refund covers the same day as the server trouble that cost some Shaman their totems. Blizzard restored those totems on November 11, and the [totem brief](/news/forever-shaman-totem-restore-nov-11/) records what that fix did and did not cover. The training refund is a separate remedy for a separate loss: gold spent on skills, returned by mail rather than by restoring the skill.

The post does not say how many players were affected or how much gold was returned.

## One player says the mail has not arrived

The thread has a report from a player who says the refund did not reach them. They write that they had put in a ticket, were told to comment on the forum post, and had not received the in-game mail. They describe training spells in Undercity with 20 something gold, dropping to 15 gold, then losing the spells when the server came back.

That is a player's account, not a Blizzard statement. Blizzard's post says the mail went to "all affected players". We are recording the discrepancy because it is visible in the same thread, not because we can resolve it. If your mail is missing, the post gives no second step beyond checking it.

## What to do

Check the in-game mailbox on the character you were playing in that window. Blizzard says the gold equals the cost of the training you bought and lost. The post does not describe an appeal process, a deadline, or a way to claim the refund outside the mail.
