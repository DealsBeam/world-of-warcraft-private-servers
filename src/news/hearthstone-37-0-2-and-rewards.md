Blizzard's **Hearthstone 37.0.2** patch brings balance changes to Standard, Arena and Battlegrounds. A separate first-party post details the **Reign of the Black Empire** rewards track. Both are new to our news section, which has had only one Hearthstone item from BlizzCon.

## What 37.0.2 changes

Blizzard's dev comment says the Old Gods have shaken up the meta and that temporary bans are being replaced with permanent balance changes.

**C'Thun** was the strongest of the four Old Gods. Its power is being moved away from the cards that activate its abilities early and into C'Thun itself:

> C'Thun will now quadruple its abilities instead of tripling them, making it an even juicier late-game payoff.

The enablers were reduced. Wrath of C'Thun deals 6 damage instead of 7, Domain of C'Thun gives +2/+2 instead of +2/+3, and Servants of C'Thun summons 2/2 Fanatics instead of 2/3.

**Warlock** took several hits. M.O.T.H.E.R rose from 9 to **10 mana**. Spurious Shiv gives +2/+2 instead of +3/+3. Criminal Contract summons three 2-cost minions instead of 3-cost. Sentenced for Smuggling steals one card instead of two. Ritual of Summoning now summons two 2/4 C'thraxxi instead of 3/5.

**Priest** saw Reach Equilibrium's requirement increased and a previous buff to Egg of Khelos reverted, with several newer Priest cards strengthened to compensate.

**Unstable Prototype** was adjusted to reduce early board strength across classes, especially Handbuff Death Knight. Blizzard also buffed underperforming cards with a focus on the other Old Gods, and says new free event cards are on the way.

## The Reign of the Black Empire rewards

When the rewards track refreshes with the expansion, players automatically receive any unclaimed rewards from the current free track and Tavern Pass before the new track takes its place.

The **free track** includes **14 Golden cards** and the **Archaedas Legendary card**, plus a random Legendary, a random Epic, Tavern Tickets, Gold and Packs. The 14 Golden cards, two copies of each of seven cards, and Archaedas cannot be crafted or disenchanted.

The **Tavern Pass** grants an immediate **10 percent XP boost** for the rest of the expansion. Listed rewards include Rajh Diamond and Ammunae Diamond Legendaries, Golden M.O.T.H.E.R and Archaedas, Signature cards, a new coin and card back, **eight hero skins**, a Golden Standard Pack and 4 Reign of the Black Empire Packs. All Tavern Pass card grants are uncraftable and undisenchanted.
