September 20, 2026: after yesterday's [Hall of Thanes mapping](/news/forever-hall-of-thanes-mapped/), player documentation of Forever's new content continues at quest scale. Warcraft Tavern's Val Hull [solved Silver of the Waves](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/silver-of-the-waves-quest-solved/), a new quest hidden in old Loch Modan.

The chain: a Discarded Fishing Toolbox sits underwater at 45.6/43.4 (mind the over-leveled Young Threshadons), holding a Monogrammed Silver Hair Clip with only the initials K.D. as clue. Anglers will recognize Khara Deepwater, the Fishing Supplies vendor at 39.5/39.2; everyone else gets to play detective. Reward is experience plus a Repurposed Hair Band (cloth wrist, 2 Intellect/2 Spirit).

The pattern matters more than the wrist: Forever hides new quests inside familiar zones with diegetic clues rather than map markers. Expect dozens more like this as beta players sweep old Azeroth.
