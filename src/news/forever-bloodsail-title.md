A player screenshot shared by Duskforge shows that the **Bloodsail Admiral** title is now awarded by the "Avast Ye, Admiral!" quest in WoW Forever, alongside the usual **Bloodsail Admiral's Hat**.

That is a change from original Classic, where the quest gave only the hat. The title did not arrive until patch 3.0.2, shortly before Wrath of the Lich King, as an achievement reward.

This is a **community report based on a screenshot**, not a Blizzard note. We have not seen the turn-in ourselves.

## The route to the title

The chain depends on **Bloodsail Buccaneers** reputation in Stranglethorn Vale:

1. Reach **Friendly** with the Bloodsail Buccaneers while dropping **Booty Bay to Hated**. That means attacking the goblins and their guards, which also lowers your Steamwheedle Cartel standing.
2. Pick up **"Avast Ye, Scallywag"** from **"Pretty Boy" Duncan** in Booty Bay.
3. Speak with **Fleet Master Firallon** aboard the **Crimson Veil**.
4. Take **"Avast Ye, Admiral!"** from Firallon, kill **Fleet Master Seahorn** and **Baron Revilgaz** in Booty Bay, and return to Firallon.

The hat is a cosmetic item that also summons a Blood Parrot. The reported addition is the title on the same turn-in.

## Why it is worth recording

Reputation grinds in Forever are being carried over from Classic with small additions, and this is a concrete example. For players who already planned the Bloodsail grind for the hat, the title is a bonus; for players who skipped it because the hat was not worth the effort, it may now be.
