Two of our tracked servers have announced dated content for October. Both claims come from the servers themselves, and both carry a specific date and a specific content list, which makes them the kind of claim we can hold ourselves to later.

## uWoW: Mists of Pandaria x10 on October 16

uWoW announced this on **9 September 2026 at 10:45**, and the announcement is unambiguous: **16 October 2026 at 19:00 Moscow time**, a new Mists of Pandaria world at **x10 rates on the 5.4.8 client**.

uWoW is the operation that absorbed ArgusWoW after ArgusWoW closed in May 2022, and it currently runs three expansions. This would make four. Its own counter, read on September 29, showed **3,615 players online** in total, with **2,756 on Legion x100** and **335 on x5**, and it also runs a **WotLK x100 realm** that moved to its fourth progression stage on 24 July 2026.

The surrounding news gives the announcement context. On **2 September** uWoW posted season results. On **9 September**, the same day as the MoP announcement, it pushed a client update of roughly **300 MB** and warned that the first launch after it would take longer than usual, which reads as preparation. It also ran a **`DAYS3` promo code** giving three days of premium to level 110 accounts between **8 and 21 September**, once per account.

The pattern that matters: the client update, the level-110 giveaway, and the new-realm announcement all landed in the same window. That is what a launch preparation looks like from the outside, though we have no first-party statement saying so.

## UwowSA: Legion 7.2.0 on October 10

UwowSA's own news post announces **Legion 7.2.0 arriving 10 October 2026**, with **new weekly objectives**, **more reward options**, and **five dungeons from other expansions adapted to Legion**. Its homepage names the same date and adds the **Gran Cámara**.

UwowSA is a Spanish and Portuguese Legion 7.3.5 x5 realm that launched in June 2026 with a stated 3,100+ players. It also publishes a **hardcore mode** with one life and x1 levelling, Mythic+ routes, and custom bosses.

Its news archive shows a second dated item worth noting: **Prueba del Valor** opened on **10 July 2026**, closing the Emerald Nightmare phase and moving Legion x5 into its next progression stage. The post says so, in a slightly tangled sentence, but the date and the phase transition are both stated.

## Why these two are worth publishing and the rest are not

Our tracker has 24 entries in development, and 14 of them carry no date at all. Some are honest about that: `Hour of Twilight` says "Expected 2026", `Warsworn` says "Launch TBD", `Epsilon` says "Active Development", `Revelation WoW` says "Beta Stage". Several of those entries have **no URL on file at all**, which means we cannot verify them even if they published something.

The difference between a useful dated claim and an undated one is whether anyone can be held to it. A server that says "October 16, 19:00 Moscow, MoP x10 on 5.4.8" can be checked on October 16, and if it slips we will say so. A server that says "coming soon" cannot be checked by anyone, including us, and we have spent the last three days finding out what happens to our entries when nobody checks them.

That is the whole lesson of the ArgusWoW correction published earlier today, applied in the forward direction: **we hold dated claims and we do not hold vague ones.** We will revisit both of these on their stated dates and publish the result either way.

## One correction to our own data

Checking the Draevor Project for a related piece turned up an error. Our entry says its Public Test Realm is "coming soon, no date", but the site's own news list says **"Warlords of Draenor PTR Is Now Online!"** and gives a live realmlist at **`play.draevorproject.com`**, plus a second post about making testing faster with **instant flight paths, a 60-second hearthstone cooldown, and a PTR Helper NPC**.

The same page still carries "PTR coming soon" in its hero text and footer, so the site contradicts itself, and our entry appears to have taken the marketing copy rather than the news. Corrected today: the PTR is online and the entry now says so.
