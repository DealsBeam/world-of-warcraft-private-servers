Blizzard shared a set of first-two-week statistics for WoW Forever on November 18, and Executive Producer **Holly Longdale** said the game has brought World of Warcraft its **highest weekly player count in 15 years**. The numbers are Blizzard's own, posted by the official World of Warcraft account and reported by Warcraft Tavern.

Forever launched on November 4, so the figures cover exactly two weeks.

## The full set

| Statistic | First two weeks |
|---|---|
| Characters created | 15,160,147 |
| Quests completed | 1,480,343,357 |
| Enemies defeated | 9,786,925,806 |
| Guilds created | 140,764 |
| Camping items used | 28,696,386 |
| Parties formed | 71,309,899 |
| PvP kills | 28,938,569 |
| Player deaths | 196,851,761 |
| Deadliest boss | Archmage Arugal, Shadowfang Keep |

The official post's accompanying message was short: "The hype train continues. You're breaking records by the day!"

Longdale's claim was separate and broader. She wrote that Forever "has more people playing weekly than we've seen in World of Warcraft in 15 years". That is a statement about Blizzard's whole MMO, not just Forever, and it is the strongest claim in the set.

## What the numbers do and do not tell us

These are **activity counters**, not a population estimate. A character is not a player, and 15.2 million characters created does not mean 15.2 million people logged in. The same applies to parties formed and enemies defeated.

That distinction matters for us because our tracker assigns population bands from server ladders, counters, telemetry and Discord presence, each with its own grade. We cannot turn Blizzard's global counters into a band for any private realm, and we will not try.

What the numbers do show is the scale of the launch. Nearly 10 billion enemies died in a fortnight, and 28.7 million camping items were used. The camping figure is the one that connects most directly to the design: Blizzard built campfires as a social system, and players used them tens of millions of times.

## Arugal is the deadliest boss

Blizzard named **Archmage Arugal**, the final boss of Shadowfang Keep, as the deadliest boss in Forever. That is a player-kill count, not a difficulty ranking, but it fits what we have heard about early dungeon routing. Shadowfang Keep sits in the level band where a large share of those 15 million characters spent time.
