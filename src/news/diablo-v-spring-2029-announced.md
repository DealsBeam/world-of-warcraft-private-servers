September 12, 2026 — Diablo has won. Blizzard officially revealed **Diablo V** at the BlizzCon 2026 Opening Ceremony, in active development and targeting **Spring 2029**.

## TL;DR

- Setting: a Sanctuary where Diablo triumphed and the traditional heroes are defeated — first in series history.
- Central question: *what must you become when the world has already fallen?*
- Deep dive today: **Diablo V: Forging the Next Era** panel, 1:45 PM PT.
- Updates: Diablo5.Blizzard.com. Full Diablo coverage in the [ceremony roundup](/news/diablo-blizzcon-2026-season-d5/).

## What we know

Only the frame so far: fallen world, defeated heroes, tonal shift for the franchise. World-building, classes, and creative vision land in today's panel. No platforms, pricing, or beta windows announced.

## What it means

A 2029 window gives Diablo IV a full runway — 30th season Monday, Amazon class 2027 — with no awkward overlap. Blizzard is spacing its tentpoles: V in 2029 puts roughly six years between mainline entries, the "shorter gaps" cadence in action. The fallen-world premise is the boldest narrative swing since Reaper of Souls' harder tone — judge it when the panel shows gameplay, not CG.

## FAQ

**Is Diablo V replacing Diablo IV?**
No. D4 gets a season Monday and a class pack in 2027. Parallel tracks.

**When do we learn more?**
Today 1:45 PM PT panel, then likely silence until next year.
