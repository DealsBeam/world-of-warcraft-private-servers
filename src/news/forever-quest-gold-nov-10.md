Players who reached level 60 in Forever and found their quest turn-ins paying no extra gold were not imagining it. In a November 10 reply in the "Gold at 60 for quests" thread, Community Manager Kaivax confirmed that Forever **does not include the XP-to-gold conversion** at maximum level.

## What was removed, and why

Blizzard added that conversion to original WoW in the weeks before The Burning Crusade, and Kaivax says it stayed in Classic Era in 2019. For Forever, the team disabled it on purpose.

The reason is quest hoarding. With conversion on, the most efficient way to level was to finish a full quest log and then hand everything in after hitting 60, or to grind creatures for experience and skip quest turn-ins entirely. Kaivax says that is not the design Blizzard wants to keep.

## The part that went too far

Removing the conversion had a side effect Blizzard calls "slightly more negative than intended." In original WoW, only about **25 percent of quests** actually pay raw gold, so players approaching 60 lost a meaningful source of income for spells, mounts and training.

The fix is the line that appears in the **November 10 hotfix notes** published on November 11: "Most quests level 48 and higher now additionally reward gold."

Kaivax's post, written before the notes went live, said the hotfix should arrive within about 24 hours. The published notes confirm it shipped, and they add detail the forum post did not: the change also adds gold to quests above level 48 that never paid gold before.

## Which quests are excluded

The hotfix notes name three groups that are not affected:

- quests that already reward valuable items
- repeatable quests
- quests that do not give significant rewards because they are quick, easy or trivial

Blizzard's stated goal is for players to feel rewarded for doing quests at the appropriate level, both on the way to 60 and after.

## What this means in practice

Do not bank completed quests for level 60 in Forever. There is no conversion waiting there, and the new gold is attached to quests at level 48 and above, so turning them in as you level is the intended path.
