Blizzard has dated the next **Diablo 4 Developer Update livestream** for **November 24, 2026 at 11:00 AM PT**. The stream will go into the content and features coming with the next update, and it includes a Q&A at the end.

This is the event that will settle several questions our Season 16 coverage has had to leave open. We published the War Plans Tactics and Tyrael's Boons guides from PTR information; this is Blizzard's first chance to confirm the live values.

## What Blizzard says is on the stream

From the first-party announcement:

- the seasonal quest and a **revamped Seasonal Lair boss fight with two bosses instead of one**;
- two new War Plans trees, **War Plans Tactics** and **Tyrael's Boons**;
- **Horadric Items**, a new Legendary type with Horadric Affixes that work with the Talisman system;
- updates to existing War Plans activity trees;
- **class balance updates**;
- a Q&A at the end.

Blizzard also confirmed there is no new purchase requirement for the associated Twitch drop: you do not need own Diablo 4 or Lord of Hatred to earn it.

## The drop, and how to get it

Watching any Diablo IV category channel with drops enabled for **30 minutes** while the livestream is on earns the **Noxoiect dagger cosmetic**. The deadline is **November 25, 2026 at 11:00 AM PT**.

The FAQ in the announcement is worth reading before you plan a watch:

- progress comes from any eligible channel, and you can switch channels without losing it;
- watching several channels at once does not speed it up;
- background tabs and inactive streams do not count;
- the drop is not automatic, you must claim it on the channel or in the Twitch Drops Inventory;
- earned drops expire seven days after being claimed if no Battle.net account is linked;
- console, smart TV and TV app versions of Twitch do not support drops. Desktop browsers and the Android and iOS apps do.

## Why this matters for our coverage

We flagged the Season 16 start date of December 1 from Duskforge and did not promote it to a Blizzard-confirmed date. This livestream is the next first-party opportunity to settle it. When it happens, we will update the Season 16 guides with confirmed numbers and keep a dated note where PTR values change.

Two specific things to listen for: whether the **12 Tactics points** and **132 Boon points** survive, and whether the new Horadric Items drop or are crafted. Our Horadric Items guide records that Blizzard had not said which.
