## TL;DR

OctoWoW under sustained DDoS, ninth round. Forum consensus suspects Ravencraft (timing + launch rhetoric). Counter-theories: gold sellers, third-party mafia framing, infrastructure. Staff won't name names. No verified attribution exists.
