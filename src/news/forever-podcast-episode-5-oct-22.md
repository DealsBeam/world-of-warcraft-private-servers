Blizzard published its own note today: **"Dive into the End of Beta Special!"** for episode 5 of the WoW: Forever Podcast. It is the fifth episode of the official series, and the follow up to the episode we covered on October 15, which was about PvP.

## Who sits on this episode

Per the official article: podcast host **Countdown To Classic** is joined by special guest **Meeix**, and the developer seats are **Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield** (Senior Game Designer), **Clay Stone** (Associate Production Director), **Ana Resendez** (Lead Software Engineer), and **Michael Nuthals** (Lead Encounter Designer).

The video is [published on the official channel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uOlxa3xvNq4), and Blizzard's article frames it as insights "from the development team about the beta and World of Warcraft: Forever arriving globally on November 4".

## The connection to today's rule changes

Two of the biggest facts in Forever's cycle broke their trail through this podcast series: Aggrend's [RP-PvP reversal](/news/forever-rp-pvp-ruleset-oct-22/) today explicitly cites "a recent episode of the WoW Forever Podcast" as the place the team discussed its concerns, and one of the decisions came out of that chain rather than a panel. This episode is the anchor point for that thread of the conversation from the developer side, and any claim about mid-beta design reversals should be checked against it.

## What we can confirm without the transcript

The video upload is dated today and the article's participant list is official, so the episode roster above is first party. The content of the conversation itself is video and we cannot yet transcribe it; claims about what was said in it stay out of this piece until we can verify against the video itself or a reliable transcript.
