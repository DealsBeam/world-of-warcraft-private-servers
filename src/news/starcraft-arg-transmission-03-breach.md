Transmission 03 is here and the tone flipped from mystery to emergency. Doors breached, miners down, an insider warning to question everything.

## TL;DR

11 logs recovered: three medical patients with O₂ saturation above 100%, D. Alvarez ("THEY'RE ONTO ME. QUESTION EVERYTHING"), mission survey naming operatives, population counter going haywire (92 → 391 → 570 → 1610 → ERROR → CAP EXCEEDED), vent horror maintenance log, and Keri Parker's emergency transmission from Drift 4600-South ending in "DOORS BREACHED. DOZENS OF THEM." Plus a site takeover screenshot. Four days to ceremony.

## What changed

- **Scale jump:** 02 was fragments, 03 is an 11-log incident report with named miners (Huxley, Nguyen down), timestamps, vitals on three patients.
- **Patient anomaly:** All three patients (T. W███████, S. K███████, E. B████) show O₂ saturation of 105%, 126%, and 107% — above the 100% physical ceiling. Elevated BP, heart rate, respiratory rate, and temperature across the board. Whatever they're exposed to, it's not normal.
- **Population counter breakdown:** Field report shows civilian population climbing (92 → 391 → 570 → 1610) before hitting ERROR and CAP EXCEEDED. The counter is either malfunctioning or something is multiplying.
- **First insider voice:** D. Alvarez warns the reader directly. Prior transmissions were logs and manifests.
- **Vent horror:** Maintenance request from Shipping and Receiving — "something is in the vents, it's taking out our wiring and comms." Three people missing but still appearing on comms. Crates exploded. Scratching, screeching, banging.
- **Keri Parker emergency:** 30 miners trapped at Drift 4600-South. Doors breached. "HUXLEY AND NGUYEN ARE DOWN. THEY'RE COMING FOR ALL OF US." Signal lost.
- **First site takeover:** User-supplied screenshot of the live site taken over.
- **Transmission 04:** Listed as "SIGNAL UNRESOLVED — AWAITING TRANSMISSION."

## What it means

Escalation cadence: 01 (setup) → 02 (channel move) → 03 (incident) across five days. Scripted runway into Sep 12. Horror framing now explicit — extraction-shooter and Ghost-revival theories both gain. The O₂ anomaly and population counter suggest something biological or Xel'Naga-related, not just military action.
