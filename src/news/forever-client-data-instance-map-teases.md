September 17, 2026 — captures from the Forever client (via Icy Veins, September 16) surface **instance maps Blizzard has not announced**, alongside the [nine announced dungeons](/classic-plus/#dungeons): a second Dalaran map, Hyjal Crater, Warsong Gulch (Winter), Battle for Gilneas, "Half-Pint Tavern", "Manor Mistmantle" (sic) and "Eastern Kingdoms Preserved" — plus older cut content in **Azshara Crater** and **Karazhan Crypts**. The captures carry their own warning: presence in game data does not mean the map ships.

## Separating announced from unannounced

The announced set all appear in the data — Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Whelgar's Excavation (as "Excavation Site: Wetlands"), City of Dalaran, Blackmaw Hold, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison and Shaper's Terrace — matching the [hub's table](/classic-plus/#dungeons) with its level brackets. The five announced zones (Mount Hyjal, Zephras Isle, Darkspear Islands, Riverglades, Shen'dralas) show up too, with no sixth zone surprise.

Everything else is signal, not confirmation:

- **Dalaran City** — a second, separately-named Dalaran instance next to the announced City of Dalaran dungeon.
- **Hyjal Crater** — distinct from the Hyjal Summit raid map.
- **Warsong Gulch (Winter)** — a winter variant of the classic battleground.
- **Battle for Gilneas** — the Cataclysm-era battleground asset, which ties into the tracker's long [pre-reveal speculation history](/news/blizzcon-2026-classic-plus-reveal/) naming Gilneas as a wanted zone.
- **Azshara Crater** — the battleground concept Blizzard famously cut in 2007; **Karazhan Crypts** — the dungeon planned for the original Karazhan and never shipped.
- **Event-cluster maps** — Scarab Dais, Searing Basin, Tainted Scar (Silithus/AQ-adjacent), Shadow Hold, Storm Cliffs, Starfall Barrow Den, The Crystal Vale.

## The SoD caveat

Season of Discovery lineage is all over the files — Demon Fall Canyon, Scarlet Enclave, Nightmare Grove, an Emerald Dream minimap, even a "Development Land" map. The captures state explicitly that classic and SoD maps being present "doesn't mean they will be used." Treat the unannounced list the same way: interesting teases, zero shipping guarantee, and none BlizzCon-announced.

## Why it matters now

The **beta client is capturable**, which means the [verification hit list](/news/forever-beta-verification-hit-list/) can finally start being answered from data — see the [beta-live post](/news/forever-beta-live-sep-17/) for today's status. One honest wrinkle: Forever's own [loot-surprise design](/news/forever-beta-live-sep-17/) hides item stats until first drop, so the client answers questions about *content* while deliberately staying silent about *gear*. Maps in data ≠ maps in the patch schedule — expect the first real confirmation when the December 9 raid window approaches.
