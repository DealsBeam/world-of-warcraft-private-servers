**World of Warcraft: Forever Podcast, Episode 2** was published on **1 October 2026**, titled "Speedrunning Classes", hosted by **Countdown To Classic** with Senior Game Designers **Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield** and **Aidan Moon**, Principal Game Designer **Kris Zierhut**, and content creator **Sodapoppin**.

The stated subject is class design philosophy: "explore the strategies, challenges, and choices around Class design philosophy in WoW: Forever".

## Why a video post is worth a news item

Because of what it is not. The class deep dives published on **30 September** were four pages of specific numbers: level gates, baseline abilities, a new 16-point talent milestone. This episode went up a day later on the same subject and is **a video with no written notes at all**, 483 characters of page text in total, and **zero comments**.

That contrast is the useful part for a reader trying to follow this beta. Blizzard is publishing its class work in two formats at once, and the written one is where the checkable claims live. Anyone trying to pin down what a class does in Forever should be reading the deep dives and treating the podcast as the reasoning behind them, not the other way round.

Episode 1, published in the week of 21 September, had Aggrend, **Lead Classic Designer Tim Jones**, and creators Countdown To Classic and Xaryu. The progression from episode 1 to episode 2 is a shift from "what is this project" to "how were the classes designed", which matches the deep-dive rollout landing in the same window.

## What we cannot tell you

We have not watched the episode, so we cannot summarise what was actually said, and we are not going to infer its contents from the title. A speedrunning guest on a class-design episode suggests the conversation includes how the class changes interact with speedrun categories, but that is our inference from a title and it is exactly the kind of claim this coverage refuses to make.

If you have watched it and the discussion contradicts anything in our reporting, that is worth telling us.
