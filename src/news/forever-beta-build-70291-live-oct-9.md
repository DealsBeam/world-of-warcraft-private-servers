The beta track moved to **1.60.1.70291** on October 8 at 20:27 UTC, up from 70245.

## Single push, new binary

| Sequence | Time (UTC) | Build | build_config |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4066960 | Oct 8, 20:27 | **1.60.1.70291** | `e8dd824c` |
| 4063876 | Oct 7, 18:53 | 1.60.1.70245 | `0bf14126` |

One sequence, one config change. Genuinely new build, not a re-point. Product config and keyring stable, regions in parity.

## The coincidence, stated carefully

Kaivax's development notes, new Dalaran dungeon, 16 free Legacy points, dungeon XP increase, landed the same day. The notes name **no build number**.

It would be easy to write that 70291 contains the Dalaran dungeon. That is plausible and unverified: the notes could equally describe server-side content independent of any client, and the manifest says nothing about dungeons either way. So this post records the coincidence with the date shared and stops there.

If a fan diff later compares 70291 against 70245 record by record, that comparison will settle it. Until then the honest position is two facts side by side.

## Fan coverage lags one build

Neither reference that diffs builds has published 70291 yet. Their latest remains the 70245-versus-70235 comparison at zero changes. The gap between our manifest reading and their written record, which closed briefly this week, is open again by one build.
