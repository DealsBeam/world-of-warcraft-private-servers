Blizzard used a November 11 post to put Forever's optional upgrade editions back in front of players a week after launch. The post, "Purchase World of Warcraft: Forever Upgrades and Begin Your Next Journey in Azeroth", does not introduce a new edition. It re-lists the same three that were sold before launch:

- **Skyborne Heroic Pack**: access to the Skyborne race and the Zephras Isle starting experience, the Cerulean Prideclaw ground mount, the Shen'dorei Skyseer's Garb, the Veteran Adventurer's Rucksack, the Shen'dorei Windwell toy, and Skyborne housing decor for modern World of Warcraft.
- **Skyborne Epic Pack**: everything in Heroic, plus 30 days of game time, additional Veteran Adventurer cosmetics, tabards, and pets.
- **Warcraft Forever Collection**: everything in Epic, plus Warcraft III: Reforged, the Forsaken Kingdom campaign, and Forsaken and Human figurine decor.

Two details in the post matter more than the list. The Collection is available **through January 11, 2027**, and the Forsaken Kingdom campaign is also sold separately, either on its own or in a combo with Reforged.

## What the post does not say

Blizzard repeats that Forever itself is included with a World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time, and that the packs are optional. It does **not print prices** for any edition, so the only storefront figure we can still attribute is the Heroic price reported at launch, not an official number in this post.

The post also does not change the access model. Skyborne race access appears as a Heroic Pack benefit, while the subscription covers the base game.
