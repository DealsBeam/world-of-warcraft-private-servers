TBC5Legends has published dates for Tier 6. Both gates open at 18:00 CEST on a Saturday, one week apart:

- **Mount Hyjal: Saturday 17 October 2026, 18:00 CEST.**
- **Black Temple: Saturday 24 October 2026, 18:00 CEST.**

The site runs both countdowns in the visitor's own timezone. This is the first dated Tier 6 schedule the project has given, and it puts the top of Outland two and a half months after the realm went live on July 18.

## The two notable changes in the same window

The September 21 and 22 updates also landed the fourth Shaman specialization, **Way of Earth**, a tank spec backported from Season of Discovery, plus 14 new Ancestral Runes.

The Shaman tank change is worth a specific note. The Way of Earth rune originally required a shield and Rockbiter Weapon, and because Rockbiter is a temporary weapon enchant, expiring it dropped the whole package, plate proficiency included. Players were being stripped of half their gear mid-fight. The requirement was removed the same day, so armor no longer depends on a buff that runs out.

World bosses were also switched off in the same update: **The Tempest** (Tier 5) and **Champion of the Old World** (Tier 4) no longer spawn. The project states that nothing was deleted, their loot, models and history are untouched, and they can be switched back on without a restart.
