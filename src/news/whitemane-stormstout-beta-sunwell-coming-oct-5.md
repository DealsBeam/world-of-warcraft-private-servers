Whitemane's front page currently lists **six realms in three states**: Frostmourne, Gilneas and Maelstrom Online, **Stormstout in Beta Online**, and **Sunwell Coming Soon**.

## Stormstout: MoP in beta

Stormstout is the network's **Mists of Pandaria** realm, and its status reads **Beta Online**. That is a playable beta rather than an announcement, though we have not created an account and cannot say what state the content is in.

A third-party hub describes it as a fresh blizzlike MoP realm with quality-of-life changes carried over from the network's other realms. That description is theirs, not verified first-party, and the only first-party fact we hold is the Beta Online status.

## Sunwell: TBC coming soon

Sunwell is the **Burning Crusade** realm, status **Coming Soon**. No date is published on the front page. A coming-soon without a date is the category this tracker records as unverifiable rather than pending, and Sunwell sits in it until a date appears.

## Frostmourne did 8,500

The network's own news states its **Wrath Rebuffed realm opened September 19 with over 8,500 players online across the globe**. That is a first-party launch figure, self-reported like all such numbers, and it makes Frostmourne one of the largest single-realm launches of the year on our tracker.

Our existing Frostmourne entry carries an older reading and will be refreshed on the next pass.

## Why this matters at 30 days to Forever

Whitemane is expanding in two directions at once while Blizzard's launch approaches: backward into TBC and sideways into MoP, with a rebuilt Wrath realm already live. A network running Cataclysm Classic, Cataclysm, Wrath Rebuffed, MoP beta and TBC soon, simultaneously, is operating at a scale where any one realm's problems are cushioned by the others.

That cuts both ways for a reader choosing a realm. Depth of operation is evidence of competence and evidence of divided attention, and no front page tells you which one you are getting.
