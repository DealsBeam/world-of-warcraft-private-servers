Blizzard's September 24 update says additional English versions of the **World of Warcraft: Forever Collector's Edition** are available for pre-purchase in North America, the United Kingdom, Europe, Australia, and New Zealand. Exact availability varies by retailer. Orders from the restock are expected to ship and deliver in **February 2027**, after Forever's November 4 launch, and the Blizzard Gear Store limits purchases to one per household.

The physical set includes a 13-inch Dwarf and Bear statue, Zephras Isle mousepad, art prints, collectible pins, a companion journal, and a digital code for the Warcraft Forever Collection. That code includes the Skyborne Epic Pack, Warcraft III: Reforged, and Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom.

Blizzard says beta benefits tied to the code expire after the September 17 to October 21 access period. Qualifying purchases can also receive one or more Invite-A-Friend launch codes by email beginning October 20, valid during the November 4 to 11 launch week. Separate digital purchases may qualify for Battle.net Balance credit when the Collector code is redeemed by December 31, 2027, subject to Blizzard's support terms.

This is a restock and fulfillment update, not a new gameplay feature. The original digital edition details remain in our [pre-purchase breakdown](/news/forever-pre-purchase-editions-beta/).
