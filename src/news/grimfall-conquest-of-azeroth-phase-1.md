[GrimfallWoW](/servers/grimfallwow/) launched **Conquest of Azeroth: Phase 1** on October 16 2026, a second product line beside its existing Classless realm.

The project's own announcement page reads "Phase 1 · Live Now" and states the release is **progressive and phased with an initial level cap of 20**, with later phases raising the cap. At the level 20 cap it offers **scaled Heroic and Mythic dungeons** and other end-game content, which is unusual for a level 20 ceiling.

## What Phase 1 ships

- **21 new custom classes**, each with unique abilities, expanded talent trees and specializations. The page names the Necromancer, Chronomancer and Runemaster among them.
- **More than 10 additional playable races**, with racials, forms, faction flavor and visuals.
- **Prestige**, which resets a character back to level 1 for cosmetics, heirlooms, mounts, pets and Chrono Fragments.
- **Scaled TBC and WotLK dungeons and quests**, plus two Cataclysm dungeons adapted to 3.3.5a: **The Stonecore** and **Lost City of the Tol'vir**.
- **Tol Barad** as a daily quest zone, and rebuilt **Kezan** and **Gilneas** starting experiences.

GrimfallWoW's Classless realm released September 5 2026 after an open beta from March 27, and remains live. The two products share a site and a download page, and the Conquest of Azeroth phase is presented as a separate realm rather than a replacement.

## The name, and why it needs a warning

**Conquest** is now the most overloaded name in this scene, and we track several unrelated projects under it:

- **Conquest of Azeroth** was the Ascension network realm, shut down September 4 2026 after a Blizzard cease-and-desist. That entry is `dead`.
- **Conquest of AzerothCore** is an open-source continuation on the AzerothCore core, running 22 classes at a public bug-test realm. We updated it today.
- **Conquest Reborn** is an independent, non-commercial WotLK realm in closed testing.
- **GrimfallWoW's Conquest of Azeroth** is this release.
- Separately, **[ArdenWoW](/servers/ardenwow/)** lists **21 custom classes** in its closed alpha, with a roster of class names that overlaps heavily with what GrimfallWoW is now shipping, including the same three examples.

We are **not** claiming those projects share code, staff, or a source. We are saying the numbers and names line up closely enough that a reader should not assume any two of them are the same thing, and that we will say so if we can verify a link. Each entry on [the tracker](/) now states which Conquest it is.
