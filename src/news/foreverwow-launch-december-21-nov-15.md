ForeverWoW, an English-language private server for World of Warcraft: Forever, has postponed its launch. The project's own site published the notice on **November 15, 2026**, moving the date from November 22 to **December 21, 2026**.

## What the project said

The post is direct about the reason:

> After we announced the date, lots of new players joined and you started sending far more bug reports, thank you for that. But they also showed us something we had underestimated from the inside: there are more core problems than we expected. We don't want to open the server in this state. We clearly rushed the November 22 date, and we apologize to everyone who was already getting ready for launch.

The new plan has two dates. **December 21, 2026** is the target if the core problems are fixed by then. **January 20, 2027** is the fallback if they are not, and the post calls that date final. The team says it will confirm one to two weeks before December 21 whether it is on track.

## What does not change

The post is careful to separate the launch delay from the beta:

- Nothing shuts down on November 22.
- There is no wipe on November 22. The wipe happens right before launch.
- The beta continues as usual.
- Accounts stay.
- Beta rewards still stand, and points keep adding up while the test server runs.

That matters for anyone who was treating November 22 as the end of the beta. The test server keeps running, and the characters made there still end at the pre-launch wipe.

## What ForeverWoW is

ForeverWoW runs its own core, which it calls **ForeverCore**, and says the core reads world data directly from the Forever client. The project is English-language, with a Russian-language sister project at nvord.com run by the same team. Its open beta has run since **October 1, 2026**, open to everyone, with a wipe before launch. At our read on November 16, the site's homepage counter showed **59 players online** and the project page listed rates and ruleset as coming in the news.

## Why it is on the tracker

We added ForeverWoW to the [server tracker](/servers/foreverwow/) on November 16 with status **In-Development**, a release date of **December 21, 2026**, and a dated note on the postponement. It qualifies on first-party evidence: the project's own site carries the beta, the core description and the launch notice. The tracker records that the site exists but does not link it.

The date also sits after Blizzard's **December 9** raid gate for Forever itself, so a ForeverWoW launch on December 21 would arrive after the first Forever raid tier has opened.

## What we do not know

- The project has not published its rates or ruleset.
- We have not tested the core or verified its behavior beyond the project's own description.
- The 59-player reading is a beta counter, not a launch population.
- The December 21 date is conditional on the core fixes. The project's own fallback is January 20, 2027.
