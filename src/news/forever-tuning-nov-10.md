Blizzard published its **November 10** hotfix notes on November 11, and the class pass it had trailed since November 6 has now shipped, though the Shaman portion arrived a day later. The notes also carry a November 9 section, so the dungeon experience increase Kaivax announced on November 8 appears in the same compiled article.

## The promised changes

The November 6 preview named Hunter pets and Voidwalkers, Affliction's Wracks, and Enhancement Shaman. The shipped notes go further in places.

- **Hunter pets:** Blizzard says pets erroneously had **150 percent of normal health** and corrected them to **115 percent**. Arcane Shot now costs **11 percent of base mana at every rank**, and Beast Mastery's Summon Hawk uses the same 11 percent while the Hawk's melee damage rises by about **80 percent**.
- **Warlock pets:** Imp, Voidwalker, Incubus and Succubus base health all rise by about **15 percent**, and the first four ranks of Imp Firebolt gain base damage.
- **Affliction:** **Wrack damage is cut by 30 percent**, it no longer increases the Warlock's periodic damage, and while channeled it makes Corruption, Siphon Life and Bane of Agony tick **100 percent faster**. Soul Siphon now gives **4/8/12 percent per active effect, up to 36 percent**.
- **Enhancement:** **Stormstrike falls to 100 percent weapon damage (was 150 percent)** and its cooldown rises to **8 seconds (was 6)**. Mental Dexterity's Intellect-to-Attack-Power conversion drops to **33/66/100 percent (was 50/100/150)**. Maelstrom Weapon is reworked to **20 percent reduced cast time and mana per application at all ranks**, with activations scaling from **6 to 10 per minute** instead of a flat 20.

Blizzard's developer note says the Enhancement changes reverse beta adjustments that pushed the spec toward Spell Power and Intellect, and that it wanted the fix in before the December 9 raids so players would not chase gear that a later change would devalue.

**Correction November 12 2026:** The Shaman changes in this pass did not go live with the rest of the notes. Kaivax said an "unexpected delay in deployment" meant the weapon imbue and weapon totem change plus the three Enhancement changes went live to all regions at **10:45 a.m. PST** on **November 12** (18:45 UTC). The delay notice names only the Shaman changes. The Hunter, Warlock, Druid and other class changes above were not listed in it.

## Beyond the class pass

The same notes cut the Auction House **minimum deposit to 1 copper (was 1 silver)**, fixed unread mail reading as from the Stormwind Auction House, and fixed some mail taking about two minutes instead of the intended 60. Most quests **level 48 and higher now also reward gold**, a change Blizzard says avoids pushing players to bank quests until 60. Racial vendor mounts are now **Bind-on-Pickup**.

**Correction November 11 2026:** Blizzard removed all three Auction House lines from the article and delayed the changes to the **November 17** weekly maintenance. The minimum deposit, unread mail and mail delay fixes are **not live**. We originally reported them as shipped because they were in the notes when we read the article on November 11. The level 48 quest gold change and the class changes above remain in the notes. [Full November 11 hotfix brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-11/).

The November 9 section repeats the **50 percent dungeon kill experience increase** and adds fixes across Alcaz Prison, Blackfathom Deeps, Blackrock Depths, The Drowned City, The Shaper's Terrace and Stratholme.
