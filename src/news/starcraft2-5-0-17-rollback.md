Blizzard's **StarCraft II 5.0.17 patch** rolls back most of the balance changes introduced in 5.0.16. The developer note says the team wanted to try something new with workers, then let the meta develop before acting on feedback.

> Based on that feedback, we have rolled back most balance changes to the live release of StarCraft II from patch 5.0.16 to 5.0.15.

This is a first-party patch note. We are covering it under our **StarCraft** news tag, which has been thin since BlizzCon.

## The economy changes that stayed

Some economy values did not go back to the old numbers:

| Value | 5.0.17 |
|---|---|
| Starting workers | **12**, up from 8 |
| Large mineral patch | **1,800**, up from 1,600 |
| Small mineral patch | **900**, down from 1,100 |
| Vespene geyser | **2,250**, up from 2,000 |
| Total default gas per base | **4,500**, up from 4,000 |

So the worker experiment is partly retained while the wider balance work is reverted.

## Terran, Protoss and Zerg highlights

A selection from the patch notes:

**Terran**
- Command Center cost rose from 300 to **400**, and supplies provided from 13 to **15**.
- Planetary cost fell from 250/150 to **150/150**.
- Widow Mine attack launch delay rose from 0.85 to **1.07 seconds**.
- Ghost health rose from 100 to **125**, attack damage changed from 15 to 10 with +10 versus Light, attack range fell from 7 to 6, and movement speed rose from 3.85 to 3.94.
- Steady Targeting damage fell from 170 to **130**, energy cost from 75 to 50, and it now cancels on taking damage.

**Protoss**
- Nexus supplies provided rose from 13 to **15**.
- Warpgate research no longer speeds up Gateway production. Warp-in time is split into **11 seconds in slow power fields** and **3.6 in fast ones**.
- Several warp-in cooldowns changed: Zealot and Adept 22 to 20, Stalker and Sentry 22 to 23, High Templar and Dark Templar 35 to 32.
- Transform to Warpgate now costs **0/0**, down from 25/25.
- Shield Battery starts at **100 energy** near a Nexus and 50 otherwise.
- Forcefields now take **1 bile** to destroy, down from 2.
- Colossus damage fell from 12(+3) to **10(+5)**.

**Zerg**
The patch notes continue beyond the lines we read directly. We are not listing Zerg changes from memory; the first-party post is the place to read them.

## Why this is worth our attention

The 5.0.17 PTR note described the same rollback while keeping the updated map pool and bug fixes. That makes this a deliberate reversal, not a hotfix, and it is the clearest esports-side story we have carried under the StarCraft tag in months.
