ForeverChanges has recorded a batch of item and recipe changes found **in the game**, not in Blizzard's notes. The largest group is ten crafted Tier 1 armor pieces covering Shaman and Druid sets, first seen at **00:37 UTC on November 21**.

We are publishing these as in-game observations from a data site. Blizzard's November 20 hotfix article contains none of the item names below, so they are not confirmed changes in our record.

## The class sets

Four **Spiritcaller's Rage** pieces, the Shaman set, now carry **Spell Power** where they previously had Agility, Critical Strike or Expertise:

| Piece | New notable stats | Was |
|---|---|---|
| Spiritcaller Treads | +13 Spell Power, +12 Intellect | +9 Hit Rating, +11 Agility |
| Spiritcaller Spaulders | +11 Spell Power | +21 Strength, +11 Intellect |
| Spiritcaller Grips | +12 Spell Power, +13 Intellect | +14 Critical Strike Rating |
| Spiritcaller Leggings | +11 Spell Power, +14 Agility | +12 Expertise Rating |

That is the clearest direction in the batch: Spell Power is moving into Shaman tank and melee pieces, which fits Blizzard's stated plan for Paladin tanks favouring Spell Power in the November 17 notes. We are not claiming the two are connected.

Six **Grovekeeper** pieces for Druids also changed. The reported pattern is more Stamina and Strength and less Expertise, Hit and Dodge. For example, Grovekeeper Gauntlets now read +18 Stamina and +15 Strength where they had +14 Stamina and +21 Strength, and Grovekeeper Leggings dropped +15 Expertise Rating for +15 Agility.

## Other item and recipe changes

- **Feralheart Raiment** 4-piece: when the bonus returns energy, it now restores energy over **5 seconds**. The site says it restored mana before.
- **Filcher's Cowl** no longer requires level 50.
- **Cooking:** Boiled Clams, Clam Chowder, Undermine Clam Chowder and Clam Linguine now apply Well Fed after 10 seconds of eating, for +1, +3, +15 and +20 Stamina over 15 minutes. Murloc Fin Soup now restores as much health as Clam Chowder and gives +3 Stamina, up from +1. Goblin Deviled Clams now give +5 Stamina and no longer give +6 Spirit.
- **Leatherworking:** Centaur Hoofwarmers is now a Leatherworking recipe.
- **Engineering:** learning Goblin Mortar now also teaches The Mortar: Reloaded, which reloads an empty Goblin Mortar.

## The source discrepancy worth noting

ForeverChanges labels two class lines as coming from Blizzard's notes: **Chastise can now be cast in Shadowform** for Priests, and **Intensity now also covers Incinerate** for Destruction Warlocks.

We checked Blizzard's running article at the time of writing. Neither "Chastise" nor "Intensity" appears in it. We are therefore not treating those two lines as Blizzard-confirmed. Either the data site has a source we cannot see, or the label is wrong, and we will not guess which.

## What this means for gearing

If these stats are live, anyone planning a Shaman or Druid Tier 1 set should re-read the items in game before spending materials. Crafted pieces cannot be returned once made, and ten pieces changed. The same applies to the Cooking recipes, where the Well Fed values moved.
