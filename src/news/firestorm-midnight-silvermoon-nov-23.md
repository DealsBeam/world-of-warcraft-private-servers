Firestorm releases **Midnight** on **Monday, November 23, 2026**, and it is not opening a second realm to do it. In a post on its own news page, the network says the **Dornogal** realm becomes the Midnight realm, **Silvermoon**, on that date. The War Within stops being available.

## What happens to characters

The migration is automatic and requires no action from players:

- Characters move from Dornogal to Silvermoon with the realm.
- Gold, items and guild bank contents carry over.
- Auction house listings end and return to the mailbox.

That last point matters for anyone holding auctions through the weekend. Listings do not transfer to the new realm; they come back as mail.

## The client and the patch

Firestorm is explicit that the version numbers do not match in the usual way:

> The client will actually run on version 12.0.7, but the content will be from patch 12.0.2, and class balance will reflect 12.0.7.

Players can download the Midnight client through the launcher or update an existing The War Within client. The new level cap is **90**.

## Season 1 and the launch week

**Midnight Season 1** arrives on **December 7, 2026**, two weeks after the expansion. Firestorm lists PvP, Mythic, Delves, raids, the Hard and Nightmare difficulties of the Prey system, and world bosses in that Season 1 package.

For the first week, **November 23 through November 30**, logging in grants a unique reward, and **Boris** offers a **level 80 boost** to one character per account. Firestorm says more detail is in the post.

## Why the realm is being replaced

Firestorm frames the change as a deliberate retirement of old realms:

> Following our recent changes to legacy realms, we don't want to keep accumulating old realms. Instead, we want everyone to move forward and join the new content in Midnight realm.

The network already runs BFA: Reforged alongside its older MoP and Legion realms. Folding the TWW realm into Midnight keeps the expansion count from growing, at the cost of removing The War Within entirely.

## Tracker note

[Firestorm's tracker entry](/servers/firestorm/) moves from the **TWW** tag to **Multi** on November 17, because after November 23 no TWW realm remains on the network. Status stays playable; the release is a realm replacement, not a new server. The entry records the November 23 date and the December 7 Season 1 date as read at Firestorm's own site.

## What we have not verified

- We have not tested the Midnight client or the migration.
- Firestorm says almost every part of the patch has been scripted, but we have not checked specific systems against the retail client.
- The post does not state how long the realm is offline on November 23, or whether the move happens at a set hour. It gives the date only.
