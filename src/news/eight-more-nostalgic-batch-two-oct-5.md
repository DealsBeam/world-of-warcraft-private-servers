## WoW Tales opened two days ago

The most time-sensitive find: **WoW Tales officially opened on October 2 2026**, running TBC Phase 1 content on the 3.3.5a client with its own launcher, and announced a hardcore level-70 event on September 30.

A two-day-old realm is the noisiest possible moment to measure, which is exactly why the tier is `unknown` rather than read from its 248 accounts and 381 characters. Those are registrations, and registrations on day two measure marketing.

## Luminary's October 31 goes on the calendar

Luminary's news, posted September 23, dates **patch 1.7 opening Zul'Gurub to October 31 2026**. That joins Nozdor's DUNE (Oct 16), uWoW's MoP realm (Oct 16), UwowSA's 7.2.0 (Oct 10) and Stonetavern's gate (Oct 23) as the fifth dated private-server claim we hold. All five get re-checked on their dates.

## Four zeroes, four different meanings

Kaelara, WoW-Vicious, Aetheria and EvoWoW all read 0 or near it, and the entries distinguish what kind of zero each is. Kaelara's is a community project idling. WoW-Vicious is a **fresh install still showing the template post**, which is a beginning rather than an ending. Aetheria's sits beside 2 days of uptime. EvoWoW's is 1, which rounds to a story about smallness rather than emptiness.

A zero on a live widget is a reading like any other, and it bands `tiny`. What it does not do is distinguish dying from starting, and the entries say which where the evidence exists.
