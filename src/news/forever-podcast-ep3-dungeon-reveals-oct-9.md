Episode 3 of the official Forever podcast covers dungeons, and two timestamped segments moved our verification list.

## Blackmaw Hold, settled at 39:07

The Azshara dungeon is **Blackmaw Hold**, located at the unused Timbermaw Hold entrance. Bigger footprint than Blackrock Depths, vertical and non-linear, full of secrets, occupied by corrupted furbolgs and demons, comfortable at level 60 with earlier entry possible, tighter level range so it clears in one long session.

It will **not be available during the beta**.

That resolves our Timbermaw-versus-Blackmaw naming item outright. A developer's question in September left both names visible; a direct statement in October settles it. The gate was Timbermaw's. The dungeon is Blackmaw's.

## Onyxia attunement stays, at 53:33

Players must still complete the **lengthy Classic attunement chain** for Onyxia's Lair, and it will **not be account-wide, at least not at launch**.

Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit get **no attunement chains**, but the developers hinted at unspecified extra steps for the full experience.

This constrains but does not resolve the launch-versus-December-9 date conflict. An attunement players grind argues for launch-day relevance: you do not preserve a weeks-long chain for a raid opening five weeks later without saying so. But it is an argument, not evidence, and the conflict stays open.
