The schedule held. The Brazilian Vanilla project's own front page today still reads the two lines it has carried since early access: **08/10: acesso antecipado, nível máximo 10** and **22/10: lançamento oficial, nível máximo 20**.

## What is live now

- Official launch, October 22, level cap raised to **20** (early access opened October 8 capped at 10)
- Client: Vanilla **1.12.1 build 5875**, downloaded from the project's own Google Drive link
- XP **1x with an optional 2x toggle**, improved drops, accelerated rest, partybot assistance
- Progression: the "Marcha para o 60" panel describes **seven content phases** and **45 days of preparation before the first raid**
- Playable status in our [tracker entry](/servers/anachronos/), population unknown: the site publishes no player counter, so on launch day, and unlike a launch page, we cannot measure the crowd

## What to watch

The two dates we can now check against the clock are the phase announcements and the first raid's date, both of which should appear in the project's own news archive ("Ver o arquivo de notícias" on the front page carries dated entries). If the 45-day line holds, the first raid window opens in early December, which is the same month Forever's raids unlock.

One caveat, stated plainly: launch-day readiness is not launch-day reality. The schedule panel is the operator's own commitment and today's read confirms it was kept to the level-cap line; the realm's actual population and stability are unverifiable from first-party data until the project publishes its own counters.
