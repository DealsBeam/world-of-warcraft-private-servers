In four days, Blizzard presents **an in-depth look at one of the new dungeons being added in World of Warcraft: Forever**, on the PAX Aus stage at **01:00 UTC on October 9**. We covered the announcement; this is what the segment itself needs to answer to be worth anything.

## The question

**Is it a new layout with Classic mechanics, or new mechanics in a Classic layout?**

Those are different claims with different consequences:

- **New layout, Classic mechanics** means Forever's PvE is a content drop: fresh geography running on systems players already understand. The interesting follow-ups are size, boss count, and where it sits in progression.
- **New mechanics in a Classic layout** means Forever's PvE is a systems change: familiar geography running on rules players have to learn. The interesting follow-ups are what the mechanics are and whether they appear elsewhere.

The announcement says neither. It promises "an in-depth look" plus design philosophy, which could be either or both.

## Why it matters more than a dungeon preview normally would

Dungeons are the least-documented part of Forever. Classes have deep dives with numbers. Progression has the Legacy System with a cap and a table. Instanced content has had almost nothing specific said about it in public.

So this segment is not one preview among many. It is the **first specific information about Forever's PvE endgame structure**, a month before launch, and whatever it establishes becomes the baseline every later dungeon announcement gets read against.

## What to watch besides the dungeon

**Hands-on reports.** The booth runs the beta all weekend, and attendees write things. First-hand game-feel reporting from non-Blizzard players is the most valuable possible output of this event, more than the stage segment, because everything in our coverage currently comes from Blizzard posts or CDN metadata.

**Whether the mechanics shown match the datamined record.** The fan references read game files; the stage shows design intent. If those disagree on anything, the disagreement is the story.

**Whether a date is attached.** Announcements without dates are the category this tracker treats as unverifiable. A dungeon with a launch-day or December 9 attachment is a claim; without one it is a demo.
