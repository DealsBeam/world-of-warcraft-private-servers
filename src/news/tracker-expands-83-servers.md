The tracker grew from 71 to 83 servers this week after a scene-wide gap sweep — and for the first time it covers the **Legion** and **Cataclysm** private server eras, which had no entries at all despite hosting some of the scene's largest communities.

## The headline adds

The biggest untracked server in the scene was [uWoW](/servers/uwow/), a Legion 7.3.5 network running continuously since 2009 with roughly 3,300 Discord members online — now tracked at medium population tier alongside [Atlantiss](/servers/atlantiss/), the Cataclysm 4.3.4 flagship that relaunched under the Tauri/Atlantiss network banner in February 2026 (with MoP 5.4.8 and TBC 2.4.3 realms also live). Rounding out the veterans is [WoW-Mania](/servers/wow-mania/), a blizzlike WotLK 3.3.5a realm operating since 2015.

## Era gaps filled

- **Legion**: uWoW
- **Cataclysm**: Atlantiss
- **TBC**: [Moonwell](/servers/moonwell/), an enhanced x100 XP veteran since 2011 whose fresh November 2025 realm peaked above 3,000 players

The WotLK and Vanilla rosters deepened too: blizzlike PvE mainstay [TrueWoW](/servers/truewow/) (since 2010), purist Vanilla launch [Stonetavern](/servers/stonetavern/), custom modernization project [Valanior](/servers/valanior/), instant-60 funserver [AmberWoW](/servers/amberwow/), Playerbot-AI realm [Stormgarde](/servers/stormgarde/), long-running funserver [ThoriumWoW](/servers/thoriumwow/), Spanish/LATAM PvP beta [ThunderWoW](/servers/thunderwow/) with its in-client AI NPC assistant, and AI-companion Vanilla realm [NycterMoon](/servers/nyctermoon/).

## How the gaps were found

The sweep cross-referenced scene directories ranked by community size against the tracked list, then verified each candidate's site, era and population signal before entry. Population tiers were assigned conservatively from Discord-based evidence rather than raw member counts. Two directory listings fell below the notability bar and were skipped.
