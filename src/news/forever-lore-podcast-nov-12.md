Blizzard published "The Lore Episode" of its World of Warcraft: Forever podcast on November 12. The forum thread for the video labels it **Episode 7** and titles it **"Turbo Nerds Lore-ing Out"**.

Host **Countdown To Classic** is joined by creators **PlatinumWoW** and **Nobbel** for a conversation with **Senior Game Designer Josh "Aggrend" Greenfield** and **Lore Historian Lead Sean Copeland**. Blizzard's article says the group dives "deeper into the lore and story of World of Warcraft: Forever."

The post is a video with a short description. We have not reviewed the episode itself, so this brief records who appeared and how Blizzard frames it, not the lore claims made in the conversation. Earlier episodes covered speedrunning classes, dungeon reveals, PvP and the technology behind the game; the tech episode is recorded in [our catch-up brief](/news/forever-podcast-ep6-tech-catchup-nov-13/).
