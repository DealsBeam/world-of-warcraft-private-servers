Stonetavern's Barrens, the progressive realm climbing toward Wrath as one community, merged into **Elwynn** on **October 23, 2026** at the published gate time. The project had long said the roads would diverge this way, and today it happened.

## What changes

- **Elwynn is now the single realm.** The museum realm remains faithful Vanilla 1.12.1, x1 rates, no shop, no layering, cross-faction chat/groups/dungeons as the one deliberate exception, and the raids staged by readiness: Molten Core and Onyxia open **2 weeks after 80 accounts reach level 60**, then Dire Maul, Blackwing Lair, Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj and Naxxramas over a ~15-month roadmap counted from MC-open day. After Naxxramas, the realm stops at "full Vanilla, permanently."
- **TBC comes next, on a new realm.** After Naxxramas, **The Burning Crusade** will begin on a **new realm**, with **every character copied 1:1** to it and the same login. The Vanilla character stays on Elwynn. Wrath will follow TBC down the same path. That is a cleaner split than trying to carry a 15-year character base through an era change on the same world.
- **The project stays true to its posture.** No shop, no purchasable advantage, changes go to a vote and land in a public changelog, AI-assisted detection with human appealable bans, daily rehearsed backups. The realm count went from two to one, but the "the game, kept" pitch is now sharper: one world to freeze, another to climb from a snapshot.

## Why it matters

Stonetavern occupies a narrow niche: a small, EU-hosted Vanilla realm that is deliberately not trying to be the biggest one on the list. Merging the experiment into Elwynn and promising a proper 1:1 copy to TBC is unusually transparent for a private server, and the readiness-gated raid ladder (with a published community threshold) matches the "no invented calendar to rush MC" posture the team has held since day one. For readers looking for a long-term Vanilla home rather than a seasonal ladder, the post-merge state is easier to explain than the two-realm split was.
