September 23, 2026: one addition from today's community-list reconciliation. **Freedom WoW** joins the tracker as a playable WotLK realm, verified against its own live site rather than a toplist.

The numbers first, because they are the reason it clears the bar. The site shows **531 online** at check time, a **~7,500-member Discord** (per Nostalgic.gg), and a patch note dated August 28. That is a populated realm with an active development loop, not a directory entry.

What makes it different from the other 53 WotLK servers we track: **smart cross-faction**. Groups, trade, auction house and chat are cross-faction, but healing and buffs are **instance-only**, and portals or summons into hostile capitals are blocked. The stated design goal is factions playing together without letting world PvE be abused. That is a different answer to the cross-faction question than the usual blanket yes or no, and it is the most interesting thing on the page.

The PvP side is unusually specified for a volunteer project: **arena season 8** with a wider matchmaking range, arena location hidden during rated matches to stop sniping, anti-multibox moderation for rated play, an arena spectator with anti-dodging rules, cross-faction battlegrounds and arena teams, and **Battle for Wintergrasp at up to 120 players per team**. Balance is explicitly original 3.3, with quality-of-life only: no custom stat items, no spec rewrites.

Filed as **medium** population, not large. 531 concurrent is comfortably populated with regular dungeon and raid formation, which is the medium definition, and we do not promote a server into large on a toplist's word.

One caveat: the "biggest Ukrainian private server" claim comes from Warperia, and this is the largest Ukrainian realm we have verified with a live site, so the claim holds by our own book. The Amdir and other Ukrainian servers Warperia lists we could not verify to a first-party site, so they stay off the tracker.

SOURCES: Freedom WoW official site (freedom-wow.in.ua, site check Sep 23, patch note Aug 28); Nostalgic.gg (population and Discord, April 2026); Warperia server list (reconciliation source).
