Blizzard published the latest WoW Forever podcast on **November 19**, with host Countdown To Classic speaking to Senior Game Designer **Aidan Moon** and Principal Game Designer **Kris Zierhut** about class design. The first-party post is short and confirms the guests and the topic. The detail comes from Warcraft Tavern's report of the episode, which is what this article draws on and attributes.

The through-line is gear: what players wear while leveling, what they wear at 60, and how much of the game should be about chasing a perfect list.

## Spellpower gear is behind, and patches will add more

Zierhut acknowledged that spellpower gear is not keeping pace with physical damage gear during leveling. Tavern reports that he said the team plans to add more of it in future patches so caster specialisations are more competitive while leveling.

That is an admission, not a fix. There is no patch number or date attached, so we are recording the intent only.

## The healer shortage has no immediate answer

Aidan Moon, known as Zirene, addressed the difficulty of finding healers for dungeons. The problem as described is structural: a player needs a dedicated healing spec and a healing set for higher-level dungeons, but also needs a damage setup for questing. Carrying both is inconvenient.

Tavern reports that no immediate solution was promised. The developers are weighing ways to incentivise healing, or at least make it less costly to switch.

## The BiS comment is the big one

The headline quote, as Tavern reports it, is Moon saying:

> I essentially want to get rid of BiS.

The reasoning is not that gear should stop mattering. It is that a single best item in every slot makes most drops irrelevant. The proposed answer is more gear with **unique effects against certain creature types or in certain biomes**.

Raids are the first target, because different parts of a raid can have different biomes and different kinds of bosses. The intention is that a player might prioritise completely different equipment for each boss rather than one fixed set for the whole tier.

## More bags are part of the plan

Carrying gear for every boss and every biome means carrying more items. Tavern reports that the developers plan to expand bags and improve inventory and item sorting to make that practical.

That connects directly to the existing bag-slot fixes in the November 11 hotfix pass and to the wider inventory work Blizzard has already described. It also sits oddly next to the transmog and collection systems, since unique-effect gear is only useful if you keep it.
