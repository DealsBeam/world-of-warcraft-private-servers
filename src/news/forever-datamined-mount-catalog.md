September 23, 2026: the mount picture fills in from the files. Warcraft Tavern's Luxrah published a pre-beta datamine catalog ([Tavern, Sep 16](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/mount-names-datamined-in-wow-forever/)) naming every mount string in the client. Verdict-relevant entries first.

- **Forsaken Charger named.** The spell string "Summon Forsaken Charger" is in the files, which settles the undead-paladin mount's name off the [verification hit list](/news/forever-beta-verification-hit-list/). The model itself stays open: a name is not a horse.
- **Galestriders confirmed as the Skyborne racial line.** Eight variants: Empyrean, Regal, Stormy and Umber plus Swift versions of each. The hub's guess is now a file read.
- **New families with unknown acquisition:** five Devilsaur colors, four Warden Sabers, four druid moose colors, extra racial recolors (Ochre Skeletal Warhorse, Caravan/Lavender/Pack/White Kodos, Black Skeletal Horse).
- **Corroboration, not news:** the Spectral Bear sits in the list, matching the Legacy collection track; the Cerulean Prideclaw and Veteran Adventurer's Loyal Companion match the paid packs. Three `[PH]` Prideclaw placeholders read as unimplemented.

Limits stated plainly: names in data, no acquisition, no models, pre-beta client. Anything here can be renamed, cut, or priced before November.

SOURCES: Warcraft Tavern Luxrah (Sep 16, pre-beta client datamine).
