Diablo II: Resurrected **Ladder Season 16** began on **November 13, 2026 at 5:00 PM PST**, with regional starts on November 14 for Europe and Asia. The first-party post also details patch 3.4 item changes, a character conversion cutoff and the stash withdrawal rule.

## Timing and modes

| Region | Start |
|---|---|
| North America | November 13, 5:00 PM PST |
| Europe | November 14, 1:00 AM GMT / 2:00 AM CET |
| Asia | November 14, 9:00 AM CST / 10:00 AM KST |

Season 15 ended November 9 at 11:00 AM PST, with the patch rolling out the same day. Four ladder modes are available: Pre-Expansion Ladder, Pre-Expansion Hardcore, Ladder with the Lord of Destruction content, and Hardcore Ladder. The goal is level 99.

## The stash rule people lose items to

When a season ends, ladder characters move to their non-ladder group and the shared stash goes into **Withdraw Only** tabs, marked with a checked "Past" box. Those tabs are overwritten at the next season transition.

Blizzard's example is blunt: items from Season 14 in the Withdraw Only tabs were lost when Season 15 ended. All of Season 16 is the window to pull out anything you want to keep from Season 15.

## Patch 3.4 item changes

Ten previously Ladder Only items become available in online non-ladder for **Reign of the Warlock**: Angelic Raiment, Bane Ash, Blinkbat's Form, Bloodletter, Gravenspine, Manald Heal, Pluckeye, Rogue's Bow, The Battlebranch and The Ward.

Blizzard states that these change **only** for Reign of the Warlock. Classic and Lord of Destruction are unaffected.

Separate stat changes apply to online Ladder and offline, also RotW only. One named example is **Dimoak's Hew**, moving from +100% Enhanced Damage to +100-150% and from +15 Dexterity to +20 Strength.

## The conversion cutoff

Starting **November 16**, players can no longer convert **Expansion (Lord of Destruction) Online** characters, ladder or non-ladder, to Reign of the Warlock. Offline conversions and Classic to Expansion conversions still work.

That window closed three days after the season started, so anyone who wanted an online character in the new expansion had to act before November 16.
