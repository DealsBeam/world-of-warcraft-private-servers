September 21, 2026: the GDKP ban from the [Q&A](/news/forever-qna-answers-sep-17/) now has teeth attached. In a livestream ([FRVR, Lewis White, today](https://frvr.com/blog/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-will-very-aggressively-punish-gold-buyers-after-being-too-lenient-as-the-team-threatens-permanent-account-bans/), via PCGamesN), community manager Randy Jordan and senior designer Josh Greenfield escalated from policy to punishment.

- **Permanent account bans** are on the table. Greenfield, invoking his own 22-year-old account: "It's not worth losing it permanently. I promise."
- **"Too lenient" admission.** "Being frank, we've probably been too lenient on gold buyers," with a hint the harder stance could extend to other WoW versions.
- **Tunable economy.** Behind-the-scenes knobs let developers push resources directionally if the balance feels wrong, plus replayability levers.
- **Already happening.** A [forum thread](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/gold-buying-already/2354266) documents gold buying inside the wiped beta: buyers paying real money for currency that dies October 21.

The throughline with the Q&A position: GDKP laundering, buyer/seller/bot actioning, SoD as the clean experiment. This is the enforcement chapter of the same policy.

SOURCES: FRVR (Sep 21, first-party livestream quotes); Blizzard forum gold-buying thread.
