September 22, 2026: Blizzard article [24304075](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24304075) (via [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/all-new-race-and-class-combos-and-racial-abilities-in-wow-forever-official/)) publishes the official race/class matrix and full racial tables, and closes three items off the [verification hit list](/news/forever-beta-verification-hit-list/) at once.

- **Skyborne subtypes, official.** Horde-aligned **Windshapers**, Alliance-aligned **High Order**, each with a backstory paragraph (elemental seekers vs Eldre'Thalas scholars). The fan-transcription era is over.
- **Gnome Engineering, resolved for reliability.** "More reliable engineering devices," not +10 skill.
- **Night Elf Quickness, resolved 1%/2%.** "1% increased Dodge chance and 2% increased run speed," not 2% for both.

The tables corroborate the hub's verified entries throughout with no contradictions found. Remaining opens (Onyxia date, close-date split, Spearing Strike, Heroic price, Forsaken mount, Legendary teaser, 2027 fine print) stand.
