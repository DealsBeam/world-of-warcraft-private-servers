On November 25 2026 we read two Blizzard notes and six server projects at their own pages. Private Server Hub supplied the server leads and Duskforge flagged the Blizzard items. Neither source became a fact here: every date, status and feature below comes from Blizzard's own article, Blizzard's running hotfix page or the project's own site. Three projects are now playable tracker entries, three are development entries, and every population band stays **unknown** because no project published a usable concurrent player count.

## Blizzard confirms Diablo 4 Season of Tyrael's Gambit

Blizzard's launch article now gives the season a firm start: **December 1 2026 at 9:30 a.m. PT**. Patch 3.3.0 becomes available for pre-download on **November 30 at 10 a.m. PT** on Battle.net, Xbox and PlayStation. This settles the December 1 date that [our November 24 Developer Update brief](/news/diablo4-developer-update-nov-24/) had to leave as a fan-site lead.

The seasonal story pits players against the Corrupted Reaper and the Fractured Reaper. Blizzard says the two powers combine rather than fight as separate encounters, first in the seasonal questline and then as a Lair Boss pair in Zarbinzet. The pair is named as a source of Mythic Uniques on the highest Torment levels.

The article also confirms the two new War Plans trees. **War Plans Tactics** changes the odds of preferred activities and rewards appearing, requires Lord of Hatred, and works in both Eternal and Seasonal content. **Tyrael's Boons** is seasonal only and does not require the expansion. Blizzard says both trees continue beyond Season 16.

Two post-PTR changes matter for our earlier Diablo 4 guides:

- War Plans Tactics and Reward node chances went from 25/50/75 percent on the PTR to **200/400/600 percent** in the launch article.
- The Aberrant Cinders cap went from 3,000 on the PTR to **5,000**, and Cinders no longer reset when a Helltide ends. One Helltide stays active in the original zones for one hour while another appears in Nahantu or Skovos on a 30 minute offset.

Blizzard also lists carryovers from Season of Hell's Legacy, including the Prime Evil Council in Uber Tristram, Capstone Dungeons at Torment 10 and above, Legacy Uniques, the Annihilus Seal, the Hellfire Torch Charm, Deckard Cain's books in Tristram and Cow Island. **Dated note, November 25 2026:** these are the final article values. Where our earlier guides used PTR numbers, the launch article is now the source of record.

## Blizzard's November 23 Forever hotfixes are documented

Blizzard retitled its running hotfix article **"WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 23, 2026"**, and Kaivax posted the matching list on the official forums at 2:06 a.m. on November 24. This is a separate pass from the [November 19](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-19/) and [November 20](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-20/) lists we have already covered.

The headline fix is for World Buffs. Blizzard says they no longer fall off when changing areas, but still fall off when entering raid instances. The same spell section removes unintended extra power from Fengus' Ferocity and Darkmoon Faire buffs, both of which had been granting double their intended effect.

The rest of the pass is a broad cleanup:

- Druid's 4-piece Feralheart set now grants the stated 4 Energy per second for 5 seconds.
- Mind Vision no longer procs effects, Chastise works in Shadowform, and Destruction Warlocks get a working Intensity line on Incinerate.
- Warriors can no longer use Mortal Strike without spending the talent point, though the ability remains visible in the spellbook.
- The Obsidian Sentinel in Uldaman drops loot as intended, and the Filcher's Cowl no longer has a level requirement.
- Six cooking recipes get the right Well Fed bonuses, Murloc Fin Soup moves up a food tier, one engineering recipe and one leatherworking pattern become learnable, two engineering gift items stop showing as broken question marks, and four Wetlands and Riverglades quest rewards or escort steps are repaired.
- Wisp Form no longer applies to living players, and a few spells no longer cause extra weapon procs.

No client build number is tied to this hotfix list, so we are not pairing it with a build from our CDN watch. The remaining undocumented Ruzzgot's Shipment cache observation from [our November 19 report](/news/undocumented-forever-hotfixes-nov-19/) is still a reported observation, not a Blizzard-confirmed line.

## Six server projects checked at the source

### Alpaca WoW is a dated WotLK Classic launch, not a live realm yet

**[Alpaca WoW](/servers/alpaca-wow/)** describes a Wrath of the Lich King Classic 3.4.5 PvP realm named Rasganorte. Its own page sets the opening for **November 28 2026 at 14:00 Lima time**, with x3 experience, x2 gold and professions, phased raid unlocks from Naxxramas through the Ruby Sanctum, and a no pay to win promise. The site already offers account creation and a preconfigured 3.4.5 client ZIP. That is enough for a **dev** entry with a dated release. There is no live realm count, so population is **unknown**.

### FrostEvil runs WotLK and TBC in the browser

**[FrostEvil](/servers/frostevil/)** lists two live realms on one account: WotLK 3.3.5a at x5 experience and TBC 2.4.3 at x10 experience. Both play in the browser, with a realmlist option for players using their own WotLK client. Its news dates the TBC realm live on November 21 and a WotLK quality of life update the same day. The home and live pages show the online field as a dash rather than a number, so population is **unknown** even though the realms are marked Online. Status is **playable**.

### World of Arthemis has a separate WotLK branch

**[World of Arthemis WotLK](/servers/world-of-arthemis-wotlk/)** is its own branch of the project, not the existing Pandaria side. The site gives WotLK 3.3.5a build 12340, a prepared 17.9 GB client, separate accounts and login port 3725. A November 17 post describes new level 60 characters and x5 experience; a November 22 post lists the WotLK VIP catalogue. The registration page is open and the connection guide publishes the exact realmlist line. No concurrent counter is shown, so population is **unknown**. Status is **playable**.

### Zone WoW is a sandbox with totals, not a population count

**[Zone WoW](/servers/zone-wow/)** calls itself a WotLK 3.3.5a persistent sandbox built around gathering, crafting, housing, objects and trade. Its status page, stamped November 25 at 18:26:18, reads Online with 1 day 13 hours 20 minutes of current uptime. The same page totals 107 accounts, 122 characters and 7 guilds; the home page adds 1 active rental, 4 placed objects, 4 placed NPCs and 2 builders online. Recent changelogs are dated November 19, 15 and 12. Those are totals and category figures, not a full realm count, so population is **unknown**. Status is **playable**.

### Project Eclipse is still a placeholder with a test status panel

**[Project Eclipse](/servers/project-eclipse/)** calls itself an upcoming 3.3.5a roguelite and says the site is a placeholder for accounts ahead of tests. Its only news post is dated June 29 2026. The status panel at our read showed Realm Eclipse Online, Players 0 and 3 days 3 hours 38 minutes uptime, with a published connect address. An online test realm with zero players is still a **dev** project by the site's own description. Population is **unknown** rather than tiny, because zero is a test-panel value, not a measured public crowd.

### PsychoFun is in maintenance and GM test mode

**[PsychoFun](/servers/psychofun/)** shows a system maintenance banner with no estimated end time, locked registration and a realm locked for GM and test mode. The status block showed 0 players online, 1 day 23 hours 27 minutes of uptime, and both realmd and world server online. Its newest news is dated October 6 2026, and the roadmap still has V19.1 and V19.3 in development. That supports a **dev** status, not playable. Population is **unknown**.

## What we did not carry over

We did not use Private Server Hub's dates, review scores or population labels for any entry. We did not turn account totals, characters, guilds or builders into a player count. We did not add Alterworld - Ashbound, whose own site still advertises a 2026 Q2 Cataclysm release while showing its main realm offline and its PTR at 0 players.
