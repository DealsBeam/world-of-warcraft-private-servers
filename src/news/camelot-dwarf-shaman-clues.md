---
title: "Project Camelot hits Patch 1.60.1 — Dwarf Shaman and Shen'dorei clues surface"
date: 2026-08-16
---
Project Camelot's encrypted branches advanced to Patch 1.60.1 this week, with both test environments updating in parallel — WoW Dev Two reached 1.60.1.69328 and WoW Dev Five hit 1.60.0.69322. Dataminer Stiven suggests the separate Dev Five build may be stabilized for a playable BlizzCon 2026 demo while development continues on 1.60.1.

Separately, encrypted promotional rewards surfaced with a Dwarf Bear mount, a four-color Skyborne Cat mount, two Shen'dorei-themed transmog sets and housing decor. The Dwarf theme hints at Wildhammer-style Dwarf Shamans for the Alliance, though the assets could also be an unrelated Retail promotion. None of this is confirmed — Blizzard has made no announcement, and a Classic+ reveal at BlizzCon (September 12-13) remains widely expected but unofficial.
