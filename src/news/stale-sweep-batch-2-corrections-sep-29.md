Four entries were wrong or unverifiable in this batch, and the pattern is the same as yesterday's: **a field that recorded a plan or a label instead of a reading.**

~~Pop: small~~ **Pop: tiny** on Sunwell.pl. ~~Pop: small~~ **Pop: medium** on Moonwell. A flag on Apollo II. A dead address noted on Helios. A missing store on WOW-COLOMBIA.

## Sunwell.pl: four players, and a pivot away from running a realm

Our entry said `small`. The site's own status widgets, read this morning, say **Frosthold: 4 players online, Voltarus: 0 players.** Four in total, across both realms.

The other half of the story is in the dates. The newest item on the news feed is **five to six years old** — Arena Season 6, Secrets of Ulduar, an Easter post, a realm birthday. Nothing since. On a server that once had a full six-season arena ladder and a crossrealm feature matrix, a five-year silence is not a quiet month.

And the third piece is the one that matters most for what the project is now. The site offers, in plain text: **"Closed 3.3.5a cross-server tests are available for other pserver projects,"** with a Discord contact for server owners.

That is not a player-server pitch. That is a technology vendor looking for customers. The cross-server stack listed on the page is a working design: crossrealm auction house, crossrealm random dungeon finder, crossrealm battlegrounds, crossrealm arena, with per-feature enablement.

The honest reading is that Sunwell.pl is **zombie-live**. It is up, you can log in, and there are four people in it. The project appears to have redirected its effort toward licensing the core to other operators. Whether that is a failure or a rational pivot depends on your values, but our tier should have reflected what a reader would actually find on arrival, and `small` overstated it.

## Moonwell: our entry had the two realms backwards

This is the opposite error and the larger number. Moonwell has run **TBC 2.4.3 since January 2011**, which makes it one of the oldest realms in this scene, and it currently runs two of them at wildly different sizes:

- **Moonwell x5: 854 players online** this morning
- **Moonwell x100: 5 players online**

Our entry described the x100 realm as the headline one and rated the whole thing `small`. The x100 realm is the quiet one. The x5 realm is where the population has been all along, and the all-time records run from 1,656 in February to 2,347 in April to **2,987 in July 2026**.

854 concurrent moves it to `medium` on our own bands, and it is the largest confirmed TBC population on the tracker right now.

The rest of the entry is worth correcting too, because it is a well-run project with dated content. Content is phased and has reached **Phase Five since July 22, 2026**. Zul'Aman went out as a **10-player** raid. A **new core has been in testing since August 25**, running in parallel on a separate test realm, described as consolidating work from other cores into "a completely different level of performance." There is a **Fast Start** boost service, plus a Fast Start++ that skips straight to the final content phase, and the stated reasoning is demographic rather than technical: *"The Burning Crusade audience today mostly consists of adults: they have jobs, families, their own responsibilities, and no longer have a student's amount of free time."*

It also accepts account exchanges **from Warmane, Chromie, Project Epoch and Project Ascension** with special conditions for guilds, which is a notable detail given how many of those projects are gone or in upheaval.

## Apollo II: flagged, not corrected

Our Apollo II entry points at `twinstar-wow.com` and describes a Cataclysm realm. That page, read this morning, lists **four realms and Apollo II is not one of them**: Persés (MoP 5.0, launched 29 August), Kronos V (Vanilla 1.12.1, 9 May), Proudmoore (Mythic+ Cataclysm) and Helios (MoP 5.4.8).

Our description matches no current TwinStar listing except Proudmoore, which we already track separately. So this entry is either a duplicate or carrying a stale address.

We are flagging it rather than changing it, because we could not find a first-party Apollo II source to confirm either the realm or the right address. Guessing a replacement would be the same class of error we corrected yesterday. It needs a human decision or a real source.

## Helios: the address is a stub, the realm is not in doubt

Helios is a real MoP 5.4.8 realm running Siege of Orgrimmar and Timeless Isle, operated by TwinStar. The problem is only that `helios-wow.com` serves **a single line of page title and nothing else**, so the reachable first-party source is TwinStar's realm page. Same structural issue as Perseus yesterday: a realm inside a network whose own domain carries nothing.

## WOW-COLOMBIA: we missed that the store sells progress

Running since 2007, Spanish, Latin America, and doing something we had not recorded: **it runs two client versions simultaneously**, Cataclysm 4.3.4 build 15595 and Vanilla 1.12.1 build 5875, both on one realmlist.

It is also active right now, with an **Alamuerte** world-boss event that chains up to four cities a night and is currently targeting **Darnassus on October 5**, plus a Beer Festival running 25 September to 11 October.

And the store: **x3 experience, loot and gold for 200 credits**, sold as 24 hours of play per account and stackable to **168 hours**. It stacks with in-game rate events, so an event x3 plus a purchased x3 is x6. Our entry said nothing about the store, which for a tracker entry is the single most important thing a reader wants to know.
