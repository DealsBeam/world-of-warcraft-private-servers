Some Forever Shamans lost a totem item in the server problems that hit the game on Monday, November 9, and Blizzard has now restored it.

A player report in the official bug forum describes logging back in after the unplanned emergency maintenance and getting an internal bag error, then noticing that a Fire Totem item had disappeared. The report says the normal lost item support path could not recover it because the item had not been vendored.

## The fix

Kaivax replied on November 11 at 01:10 UTC:

> We have just deployed a fix for this to all regions. Shamans who lost a totem due to the crash should find it restored to their bag. Shamans who experienced the disappearance, who are currently in-game, will need to make sure you have bag space, and then logout and back in to receive them.

So the recovery is not automatic while you are logged in. Free a bag slot first, then relog.

## What is not covered

The Blizzard reply addresses totems only. Later in the same thread, players ask about training gold, purchased recipes, materials and profession skill points lost in the same incident. Those questions had no Blizzard answer in the thread at our read, and we are not treating the losses as confirmed or as covered by this fix.

If your totem is still missing after a relog with free bag space, the bug report thread is the first-party place to add your case: us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2409772.
