On November 25 2026 we crawled the two user named subreddits through Arctic Shift and the working Redlib mirror, then checked every candidate at its own page or an official Blizzard page. Reddit and Redlib supplied leads only. Duskforge flagged the Diablo 4 item only. Eight items survived that boundary: two Blizzard notes, three new server entries and three tools found in the community posts. All three servers have **unknown** population because none published a concurrent player count.

## Blizzard gives Diablo 4 patch 3.3.0 a build and a date

Blizzard's running [Diablo IV patch notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24316980/diablo-iv-patch-notes) now carries **3.3.0 build 74415 for all platforms, dated December 1 2026**. This is the notes page behind the season launch already covered in [our launch brief](/news/two-blizzard-confirmations-and-six-server-checks-nov-25/), and it adds the concrete build number and the final cleanup list.

For Season of Tyrael's Gambit, the notes say Helltide Stormcaller and Portal Invasion events now spawn the intended Ullo Sar cultist enemies. Reaper bosses no longer spawn a second time after a player dies, can no longer be defeated by ranged attacks from upper platforms, and no longer trigger enemy waves on the upper platform when traps sit near the arena edge. Legacy Mother's Cultists no longer appear in seasonal activities in place of the Ullo Sar cultists.

The Horadric item section raises the drop rate and adds a new seasonal crafting recipe to the Horadric Cube. The Barbarian block includes:

- Frenzy's Throwing variant raises Iron Shrapnel chance from **30% to 50%**.
- Raging variant raises the fiery explosion Lucky Hit chance from **75% to 100%** and moves the explosion to the target.
- Double Swing damage goes from **75% to 150% per swing**, with a larger swing area.
- Flurry and Twister variants go from **75% to 113%**.
- Slice and Dice loses its cast speed penalty, but its area drops from **200% to 100%**.
- War Cry gains diminishing returns per rank, up to **15%**.
- Battering Ram's burning damage falls from **252% to 126%**.

**Dated note, November 25 2026:** these are the values in Blizzard's patch-notes article. Where an earlier guide used PTR numbers, this article is the source of record.

## Blizzard lists the rest of Midnight: Echoes of the Worldcore

Blizzard's official [Echoes of the Worldcore article](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24317679/midnight-echoes-of-the-worldcore-arrives-on-december-1) says the update **arrives December 1 with regional maintenance**. We already covered Talebound's mode and its Bound Silver prices in [our Talebound guide](/news/wow-talebound-game-mode-midnight/). This article supplies the surrounding update list, so this brief records what we had not yet extracted from it.

The new story campaign, **The Return to Dornogal**, starts from the Worldcore breach by Xal'atath and the damaged Coreway. Arator, Brinthe, Dagran and Brann guide the attempt to reach the Worldcore. Li Li Stormstout offers four level 10 plus Lorewalking storylines about Jaina, Thrall, the Titans and Tyr from Stormwind, Orgrimmar or Silvermoon City. The update also brings back Turbulent Timeways and expands the Omnium Folio.

**Dated note, November 25 2026:** this is retail Midnight content. It is relevant to this tracker as a Blizzard cadence signal, not as a Forever or private-server feature. The Talebound guide linked above remains the detailed source for that mode and its rewards.

## Three new server entries from their own pages

### Everglow starts every character at 255

**[Everglow](/servers/everglow/)** is a WotLK 3.3.5a realm whose own site says every class, including Death Knights, begins at level 255. Darnassus is the realm hub. First-party pages read November 25 show registration with email verification open, a Windows launcher at version 1.26, a published realmlist line, and a full client download of about 37.5 GB for an empty folder. That is enough for **playable**. No page shows a concurrent counter, so population is **unknown**.

### World of Immortality restores the original realm

**[World of Immortality](/servers/world-of-immortality/)** runs two WotLK 3.3.5a realms under one account. Its About page describes Immortality as 10x experience, 10x gold and 5x honor, and Fury as 3x experience, gold and honor. Two posts dated November 23 2026 on the project's own news page say the original realm is back with old accounts and characters restored and new registration open. The connect guide publishes the exact realmlist line and says it needs a 3.3.5a build 12340 client. Status is **playable**. No concurrent counter appears, so population is **unknown**.

### Midgard's beta page conflicts on registration

**[Midgard](/servers/midgard/)** is a Spanish first progressive WotLK 3.3.5a project that starts everyone at level 1 with x1 experience. Its own page describes 14 phases and an open beta, with an official launch advertised for **December 19 2026 at 12:00 Peru time** and a full wipe before that launch. In translation, the page says both "create your account and enter now" and "registration opens very soon", so we do not restate either account availability claim as settled. Status is **dev** with the site's own launch date, and population is **unknown**.

## Three tools checked at their own repositories

### An unofficial Apple Silicon launcher for Firestorm Midnight

A community developer published a native macOS launcher for the Firestorm Midnight and Silvermoon client. Version **1.1.5** was published on November 25 2026. Its repository says it needs Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later, is pinned to game build **12.0.7.68974**, and supports pause and resume installs, language choice, verification and repair. The release ships a prepared game application and is **ad hoc signed but not notarized by Apple**. The original source and documentation are WTFPL v2; the README excludes game files, third-party material and trademarks from that grant. We are adding it as a links item, not as an official Firestorm tool and not as a recommendation to run ad hoc signed software.

### AndersCraft Studio v0.9

A Windows desktop editor for AzerothCore 3.3.5a published version **0.9** on November 20 2026. Its release notes describe a 3D model and spell effect viewer, a database admin console, Keira3 and DBC editing, and a staged review, backup, apply and rollback pipeline. The project's own LICENSE file is **MIT**, with a third-party notices section appended, and the README also says MIT. GitHub's metadata returns NOASSERTION for the repository. It is a host side tool, not a public realm.

### DialogueUI backported to 3.3.5a

The DialogueUI repository describes a port of the retail Dialogue UI to WotLK 3.3.5a. It replaces quest and gossip windows, adds keyboard selection, copies dialogue text with NPC and item IDs, opens readable objects in their own window, and has hooks for Pawn and transmog modules. The repository was created November 17 and pushed November 25 2026, with four stars at our read. It lists **no licence and no release**, so the links entry records it as a source repository rather than a packaged download.
