RestedXP Guides released version 4.11.9 with expanded support for World of Warcraft: Forever and its Skyborne starting zone. The release allows `#classic` routing on Forever and restructures quest paths throughout the new starting experience.

The update also improves class training and profession instructions. RestedXP added crafting, racial-ability, travel, and deathskip macros, then refreshed waypoints, quest conditions, spell IDs, and guide text.

This is a first-party project release, not a Blizzard announcement. It matters because a dedicated leveling-guide addon now treats Forever as a supported destination rather than a generic Classic branch. Routes and spell IDs can still change while the beta cap and zones remain in flux.

SOURCES: [RestedXP Guides v4.11.9 on GitHub](https://github.com/RestedXP/RXPGuides/releases/tag/v4.11.9), released September 22, 2026, and the matching [CurseForge file](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/restedxp-guide/files/8944921).
