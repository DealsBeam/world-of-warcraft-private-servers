Our October calendar committed to a final check on UwowSA's **Legion 7.2.0, dated October 10**: Gran Cámara, weekly objectives, and five cross-expansion dungeons adapted to Legion. This result should have published on the 10th. It publishes now, October 12, because the check did not happen on the day. That lapse is recorded here rather than smoothed over.

## What first-party sources show now

Read October 12, 00:50 UTC, from uwowsa.com:

- **The realm is online.** The status widget reads **"Reino de juego Online x5, 602 Online"**, self-reported as all such counters are.
- **The announcement is unchanged.** The September 9 post still stands as the newest published news, holding the October 10 date, Gran Cámara with 6 raid bosses and 12 world quests feeding 3-of-9 reward picks, and the five adapted dungeons.
- **No post-launch confirmation exists.** The news list holds eight posts, newest dated September 9. No patch notes, no "7.2.0 live" post, no changelog, nothing published after the date the patch was claimed to arrive.

## The verdict

**The dated claim is unconfirmed, not disproven.** The realm operates and its own widget reports 602 online, but no first-party statement published after October 10 confirms 7.2.0 actually landed. Gran Cámara and the five dungeons are not verifiable from outside the game client, so this tracker records them as open. Nothing gets struck through, because nothing has been shown false; the deficiency is on our side (a late check) and on theirs (no launch confirmation published).

The claim stays open and gets re-checked the next time UwowSA publishes anything. The one correction this post makes is to our own calendar: the promise said same-day, and this was not.
