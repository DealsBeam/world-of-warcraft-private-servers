October 13, 2026: Blizzard posted its [Temporary Economy Holds](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/temporary-economy-holds-for-rmt/2381433) notice on the Forever forums (community manager Kaivax, 23:58 UTC). It is the first enforcement tool that acts on suspected gold before it changes hands rather than after.

What a hold does, in Blizzard's own wording:

- **Restricted while held:** "restricted use of trade, mail, guild banks and the Auction House." [verbatim]
- **Trigger:** an account "suspected of being involved in RMT, either the selling or receiving of illicit gains."
- **Purpose:** "aggressively shut down the flow of suspicious gold before it has been transferred or put into circulation into the economy, while providing us the time needed to investigate more thoroughly."
- **How it ends:** "automatically removed when the actions that triggered it have been investigated and found false," or account action if the investigation finds RMT.
- **No permanent mark from the hold itself:** "merely having a Temporary Economy Hold will leave no permanent strikes against your account unless also later proven to be RMT motivated."
- **Scope:** "all of these systems will also be deployed to all versions of World of Warcraft if proven successful."
- **False positives:** Blizzard concedes the system is new and that "there may be false positives while things are fine tuned."

## Where this sits in the arc

This is the fourth step of the same policy, each one narrowing the gap:

1. September 17 Q&A: GDKP banned, gold buyers to be punished hard ([our report](/news/forever-qna-answers-sep-17/)).
2. September 21: Greenfield called Blizzard "too lenient" on gold buyers and put permanent bans on the table ([the enforcement chapter](/news/forever-gold-buyer-crackdown/)).
3. October 1: [Actions Taken Against Botting and RMT](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/actions-taken-against-botting-and-rmt/2368705) restated the stance and added a trade warning: "Be wary of accepting gold from unknown or suspicious sources." [verbatim]
4. October 13: the Temporary Economy Hold, the first tool that freezes value mid-transfer.

One regional asterisk carries over from October 9: the GDKP ban applies to North American and European realms only, per a 4Gamers Taiwan interview with Clay Stone.

## What it means for players

- A hold on your account is not a ban. It is a throttle, and Blizzard says it lifts on its own if you are cleared.
- Being on the receiving end counts. Blizzard names "the receiving of illicit gains," so a gift or a one-sided trade from a stranger is the exact pattern the system watches.
- Do not buy gold on the beta expecting the wipe to be a clean slate. Blizzard says action will continue "in both the Beta and the live game after the launch of WoW: Forever."

Reaction on the thread is split, and the objection is specific: a new automated system that can flag recipients leaves ordinary players guessing where a stranger's gold came from. Blizzard is accepting that cost with the line about false positives while the system is tuned.
