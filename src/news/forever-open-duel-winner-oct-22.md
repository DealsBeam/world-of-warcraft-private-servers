The [tournament we covered on October 21](/news/forever-duel-tournament-oct-21/) has a winner. The Forever Open, the community run 1v1 duelling event inside the Forever beta, concluded on **October 18** with a Warlock grand final.

## The final, and the road to it

**Warlockguy**, an Undead Demonology Warlock and the **twentieth seed**, took the title from upper-bracket winner **Neepzen**, also a Warlock, **3-1** in the best-of-five final. The path as relayed by the bracket: Neepzen won the upper final 2-1, Warlockguy dropped to the lower bracket, beat the Shaman finalist 2-0 in the best-of-three lower final, then won the rematch and with it the **$50,000** first prize and the **"Death's Duelist"** title. In a best-of-five without a bracket reset, one final was all that stood between the twentieth seed and the money.

## The class prizes

From the results page as relayed, the **$5,555** class prizes went to: Druid **Minpojke**, Hunter **Fvp**, Paladin **Azzu**, Priest **Nieze**, Rogue **Justwoo**, Shaman **Cdew**, Warrior **Mag**, Warlock **Neepzen** (runner up overall), and a **four-way Mage tie** among Allyana, Teleporter, Venruk and Yonezka.

## Sourcing caveat, stated plainly

Blizzard did not publish an announcement for a community event, so this item rests on the tournament's own **Raider.IO** results page as read and relayed by two independent fan sites, Warcraft Tavern and timesaver.gg, whose numbers agree with each other. The prize totals match the October 21 report's expectation of a pot doubled by a streamer donation, and the runner up tally matches the class prize list, so the numbers cross check, but the primary document is the event's own bracket, and we link none of it directly from the tracker pages.

One player fact, clearly labelled as reported and not verified by us: Warlockguy is reported to be Nientonsoh, a former League of Legends professional player, which contributed to the reaction the result generated in the classic community.

## Why the Warlock 1-2 matters for launch

Both finalists are Warlocks, in a format capped at level 30 where consumables and engineering items were reportedly in scope. One tournament bracket is not class balance proof, but it is the clearest beta format evidence yet of which class communities arrive at November 4 loudest. The beta's own send-off events are still live until tomorrow morning.
