BlizzCon 2026's schedule, venue and viewership guide locked Sep 1 on Blizzard's news hub and on the Camelot Chronicle's BlizzCon 2026 Guide. Two days, one venue, one free stream that matters for Classic.

## Dates and venue

- **Event:** BlizzCon 2026, Anaheim Convention Center (800 W Katella Ave, Anaheim, CA 92802)
- **Dates:** Saturday, September 12 — Sunday, September 13, 2026
- **Badge pickup & on-site registration:** Starts Friday, September 11, 2026
- **Official hub:** the BlizzCon portal (search BlizzCon 2026) — same place the programming schedule, viewership guide and floor map were posted Sep 1

## Free stream — what you can watch without a badge

The **Opening Ceremony** and the main-stage developer panels will be available via **free official livestreams** on Blizzard's channels (YouTube and Twitch). The ceremony traditionally opens around 11 AM Pacific on Saturday. The full programming schedule, viewership guide and floor map dropped Sep 1 alongside the venue confirm — use the viewership guide if you are picking a co-stream for Classic react with chat, or the floor map if you are on site.

For Classic, the watch guide we published earlier this week still holds: the Classic future reveal — the [Project Camelot](/classic-plus/) thread we track at build 69638 on Sep 1 — is expected on the main stage, not in a hallway. If you are not in Anaheim, the free stream is the only stream that matters.

## What is on the floor and on the stage

Blizzard's Sep 1 posts list the same lineup the Chronicle's guide does:

- **World of Warcraft:** deep dives into current content and community discussions around the future of Classic servers. This is the Classic slot.
- **Diablo IV & Diablo Immortal:** upcoming season previews and expansion showcases.
- **Overwatch & Hearthstone:** developer panels and community tournaments.
- **Esports:** WoW Arena World Championship (AWC) and Mythic Dungeon International (MDI) world finals, **Blizzard Classic Cup** (classic gameplay competition), Overwatch World Cup, Hearthstone Masters Tour.
- **Community:** Community Night and **Darkmoon Faire** floor experiences — cosplay contest, talent competition, interactive Faire — listed as core highlights.

The Classic Cup is the tell. It is the only esports slot that is explicitly Classic gameplay. Its placement on the schedule is a proxy for how much stage time Classic gets after Midnight's story beat (The Venomous Abyss eight-boss raid and Season 2 already have their slots).

## Drop window

The Sep 1 "Get the Drop on New Rewards During BlizzCon 2026" post sets the window: watch any eligible World of Warcraft stream on Twitch or on the official World of Warcraft YouTube channel from **September 12 at 9:30 AM PDT until September 13 at 7:00 PM PDT** to claim a bucket of rewards. Sep 12 09:30 PDT is 16:30 UTC, Sep 13 19:00 PDT is Sep 14 02:00 UTC. Historically that means a pet, a toy, a transmog set and a title spread across watch-time tiers, sometimes a mount. Link Battle.net to Twitch and to YouTube before the window opens; drops do not retro grant for time watched while unlinked. Starter Edition is enough for most buckets.
