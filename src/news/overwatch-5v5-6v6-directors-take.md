Game Director **Aaron Keller** has published a Director's Take titled **"5v5 and 6v6, Better Together"**. It is the first-party source behind reports we have been carrying from third-party sites, and it settles the format question for Season 6.

## Three Competitive modes on day one

Season 6 launches with three Competitive options:

1. **5v5**, the standard mode and still the main format;
2. **Open Queue 6v6**, which stays available and gets a mode-specific balance patch;
3. **Role Queue 6v6**, a **2-2-2** test running for the **first four weeks** of the season.

The Role Queue mini-season starts on day one and is additive. Keller says adding it rather than replacing an existing queue should be better overall, and that matchmaking changes shipping with Season 6 are intended to keep queue times manageable.

## Why Blizzard chose both

Keller's history is that Overwatch launched with six-player teams and moved to five primarily to improve queue times and address gameplay issues. Open Queue was changed to six players and has stayed that way for nearly two years. It is now the **second most-played mode in Season 5**, with Mystery Madness close behind.

Two tests earlier this year with different queueing methods did not deliver what players wanted from Role Queue, and the surveys after them were evenly split. Keller gives the number: **32 percent of respondents stood fully behind their chosen mode** on each side.

> We're now starting to use an amusement park analogy for the game. This way, you get to shape your experience.

The practical conclusion is that there will be no single winner. Blizzard says it has no current intention of moving the main mode to 6v6, but is starting to think of it as "a second among equals".

## The balance patch is the real change

Keller says the biggest request from 6v6 players was dedicated balance work. Until now, 6v6 inherited the standard balance changes each season. That mostly worked, but not for everyone: he names **D.Mon as more effective in 6v6** and **Domina as less effective**.

The mini-season gets an "impactful, specific balance patch for 6v6", applied to both Role Queue and Open Queue 6v6. Running both at once is also meant to produce data on whether players are there for the role structure or the team size.

## What else was in the post

Keller confirmed Season 6 is close and brings the next Hero. His identity is not named in the post, but the OWCS Finals Broadcast will show the full case file. He also confirmed **Mystery Madness returns in Season 1 2027** with a new theme, new powers and other changes.

We have not published the exact Season 6 start date because Blizzard has not given us one in a source we can quote. The post says only that it is "just around the corner".
