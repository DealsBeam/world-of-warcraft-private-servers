September 21, 2026: the beta keeps paying out for explorers. Warcraft Tavern's Sponge [confirmed](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/news/collect-books-for-powerful-rewards-in-wow-forever/) that Season of Discovery book-collection quests function in Forever: turn 10 books for Friend of the Library (neck pick: Scholarly Pendant or Erudite's Amulet), 20 for Greater Friend (ring pick: Field Researcher's Loop or Philanthropist's Ring). Beyond 20 is uncharted.

The bigger find is gated behind the same books for one class. Mages who finish the collection learn **Research**, a new profession: Study consumes Light Feathers to craft spell scrolls on a 1-hour cooldown. And it is already broken in the way beta things break: multiple mages report the profession vanishing after relog, author-confirmed in the comments.

Pattern note: this is the second straight day of old-world systems resurfacing with new Forever wiring (after the Loch Modan detective quest). The beta rewards players who check whether familiar content does something new.

SOURCES: Warcraft Tavern book guide (Sponge, Sep 21, with screenshots); comment-section bug reports.
