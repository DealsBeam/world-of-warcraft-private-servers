September 23, 2026: the first beta patch week opens with a client build, not a blog post. Blizzard pushed **WoW Forever 1.60.1 build 69977** to testers (US and EU blue posts, Sep 22-23), with a three-item fix list ([full blue, via Icy Veins Starym](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/a-new-beta-client-build-arrives-for-wow-forever/)): **Mac display and stability issues**, **controller navigation in the character-selection screen** (when launching with the gamepad active), and **gamepad interaction with items that grant quests**. The build also carries untestable fixes, and the next full weekly update is due September 24.

The Mac line matters because it was the one platform thread with no workaround ([workarounds](/news/forever-beta-player-workarounds/)): macOS players crashed on launch with nothing to try. That entry is now officially addressed, test-and-report rather than troubled-and-waiting. A companion dev reply on a detailed 8-bug Mac graphics thread says multiple analyses and potential fixes are underway, crediting player repro steps. The controller fixes land one week after the [BlizzCon hands-on](/news/forever-controller-support-hands-on/) flagged pad support as alpha, and the quest-item interaction line matters for the Zephras start, where pickup quests are the first thing a pad-only player touches.

Separately hotfixed (Sep 22): the quest **A Firm Response** no longer strands **Windshaper Skyborne on Zephras Isle** at Belathaan Brightwish near the Shrine of Akir. Two more threads (duplicate-name errors, login-stuck characters) are in info-gathering, not fixed.

Still open and conspicuous: the suspected **memory leak** behind hour-long FPS decay has no acknowledgment anywhere in this round. Fixes are landing same-week; that one is not among them yet.

SOURCES: Blizzard blue post US 2358655 (full text via Icy Veins Starym, Sep 23) and Tavern blue tracker RSS (EU 630815, US 2358655, EU 630568, US 2353697, US 2354050; Sep 22-23).
