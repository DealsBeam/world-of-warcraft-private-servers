Kaivax published full development notes after extended maintenance on October 8, and the headline items are a new dungeon and free testing currency.

## City of Dalaran, level 30

A new dungeon **west of the Alterac Mountains**, available for testing now. The community has already found its secret: the **Tome of Dalaran**, obtained from a Black Tome behind Arugal in Shadowfang Keep transformed at the Ambermill Leyline Focus, summons an extra boss, **Lyn the Ignored**, past Atrexis the Grave Knight.

A launch-window dungeon shipping with a secret summon boss found by players within days is the beta working as designed.

## 16 free Legacy points

Testers get **16 free points** for trying the Legacy System. That number is not arbitrary: 16 is the per-character spend cap from Blizzard's own Legacy post. Everyone tests with a full budget.

## Systems fixes

- **Dungeon kill XP up approximately 20 percent.**
- **Classic looting restored**: creatures with no loot can still be interacted with.
- **Weapon swapping within one GCD fixed**, including two-hander to sword-and-board macros.
- **Party-level XP penalty fixed**: reduced XP from grouping with higher-level players killing lower-level enemies.
- Visual flicker on large creatures and long-distance pull behavior fixed.

The notes do not name a build number. They coincide with the beta track at 70245 and dev at 70249, and either could carry them. Server-side changes would not appear in either manifest regardless.
