October 15, 2026: a Wrath of the Lich King realm called WoW Sulvus opens at 16:00 UTC (18:00 CEST), and the name is a trap. It is not the [WowSulvus already in our tracker](/servers/wowsulvus/), a custom instant-80 realm (Adarea) at wowsulvus.com with roughly 155 people in its Discord. The realm opening today is at **wowsulvus.es**, and the split is worth stating plainly because the two are unrelated.

The new realm, which brands itself "WoW Sulvus: Wrath Reforged," says it comes from the team behind the original Sulvus, a Spanish instant-80 project from around 2010 whose Erenia realm ran arena seasons in 2016 and 2017. Its site says this version is rebuilt on a new core and engine of its own rather than a repack.

What its own site says it ships on day one:

- **PvP first:** Solo Queue Shuffle (queue alone, six rated rounds, teammates rotate), plus rated 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 with class-aware matchmaking in the 1v1 bracket.
- **Imports:** battlegrounds from Mists of Pandaria and Legion, starting with Temple of Kotmogu, and playable Demon Hunter and Monk rebuilt for the 3.3.5a client.
- **Client:** 3.3.5a, with cross-faction queues, spell batching, and diminishing returns and scaling described as matched to Blizzard values.
- **Convenience:** a template NPC that hands out full starter gear, talents, gems and glyphs in under 10 seconds, plus a duel zone and a killstreak world PvP zone.
- **Events:** arena chests in four tiers and tournaments with cash prizes, and the site markets the realm as no pay-to-win.

Anyone who joined its Discord before today gets a Founding Member tag, and a closed arena and battleground test ran with the early community before launch.

We added it to the tracker as [Sulvus Reforged](/servers/sulvus-reforged/) so it cannot collide with the WowSulvus entry. Its population is recorded as unknown: no counter is published, so no band is claimed.
