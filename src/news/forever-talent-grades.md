ForeverChanges' talent calculator now attaches a source grade to each talent: **New**, **Changed**, **Moved** or **Same as Classic**. The values are read from the Forever client and show the first rank, with further ranks available in the calculator. The site header reads build **1.60.1.71037**.

This is useful because the old question about Forever's trees was not "what is in them" but "what is different from Classic". The labels answer that directly.

## What the Warrior section shows

The page I could read is the Warrior tree, starting with **Arms (17 talents)**. Across that tree the page counts **11 New**, **35 Changed**, **2 Moved** and **5 Same as Classic** labels. Those counts cover the Warrior page as a whole, not just Arms, so read them as a scale indicator rather than an Arms-only tally.

Three examples of talents labelled **New** in Arms:

- **Spearing Strike**, one rank: a brutal attack dealing 75 percent weapon damage, with another 75 percent against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets, which are dismounted.
- **Bloodthrill**, two ranks: main-hand attacks against enemies afflicted by your Rend have a 10 percent chance to allow an activation of Overpower.
- **Weaponmaster**, five ranks: melee weapons grant a benefit by type. Axe and Polearm give 1 percent critical strike chance, Mace and Staff ignore 3 percent of target armour, and Sword grants an unspecified benefit in the row I could read.

Several familiar talents are labelled **Changed**, including Improved Heroic Strike, Improved Rend, Anger Management and Two-Handed Weapon Specialization. **Improved Overpower** is labelled **Moved**. Sweeping Strikes is marked the same as Classic.

## Why this is worth linking

Our own [Classic+ reference page](/classic-plus/) records the broad class changes from Blizzard's panels and our reporting. A graded, client-read talent list is a different layer: it tells you which entries are actually different in the files. The two sources answer different questions and do not conflict.

For readers planning a build before the December 9 raids, the calculator is a useful cross-check. We are not reproducing all nine class tables here because the page renders its class data on the client side and I could only read the Warrior section reliably.

## A caveat on the build

The site header shows 1.60.1.71037, the current live build. We have not compared every talent value against our own client read, and we are presenting the labels as the site's grading rather than as our own verification. If a number matters to a build decision, confirm it in game.
