September 23, 2026: the last profession catalog falls into place. Blizzard Watch's Nick Marino published the full new-enchant list ([Blizzard Watch, Sep 21](https://blizzardwatch.com/2026/09/21/every-new-enchant-world-warcraft-forever/)), closing the gap beside the leatherworking, blacksmithing, tailoring and engineering catalogs. Three structural findings matter more than any single value.

- **New slot: necklaces.** Five necklace enchants (Agility, Deflection, Healing Power, Spell Power, Strength) open a slot Classic never enchanted. Values are small (+5s, +11 healing) and beta-graded.
- **Campfire exclusivity extends to Enchanting.** The Enchanted Lute camp object grants +28 Armor exclusive with Mark of the Wild, matching the camp-buff exclusivity pattern; Arcane Salvager and Arcane Forge add camp crafting. Enchanters become camp contributors, not just buff vendors.
- **Biome doubling reaches crafted gear.** The Heart of the Mountain trinkets double their effect in matching terrain (Volcanic areas for Attack Power, Snowy for Frost/Shadow). This is the biome-specific gear layer in crafted form, and more portable proof than any raid set.

Also notable: class-relic coverage (druid idols, shaman totems, paladin librams with Holy Shock synergies), gathering gloves (Lotus Claw pulls Death Lotus in Hyjal), and SoD plus Classic enchants carrying over untouched. Marino flags the standing caveat himself: beta values, hidden-from-datamine gaps possible, list kept updated.

Single secondary source with Wowhead Forever spell links embedded per entry. Treat values as beta reads, structure as the story.
