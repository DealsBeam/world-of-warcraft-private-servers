Two dated additions to the scene, both read at source today.

## Project Reforged puts a date on its Classic+ launch: November 6

[Project Reforged](/servers/project-reforged/) now runs a **launch countdown on its own front page** reading "**Starts 06.11.2026 18:00 CET**". That converts to **17:00 UTC** on November 6 2026, since European clocks are back on standard time by then.

The countdown sits beside a realm widget reading **Bronzebeard Online** on a **Low-Rate x1** realm, and the project's own realm statistics page, read today, shows the realm up with **32 characters created, 6 Horde and 25 Alliance**, and **0 players online** at the snapshot, on a reported uptime of **0 days, 17 hours, 30 minutes**. Its online-players page reads "There are no players online."

That combination is worth stating plainly rather than smoothing over: the realm is reachable and joinable, but it is empty at our read, and the operator is still advertising a November 6 launch. We have recorded the realm as reachable since **September 11**, so read the countdown as the project's own launch or fresh-start date rather than the first day the realm answers.

The project's October 3 news posts frame it as an expanded classic experience rather than a different game: "new and reworked zones, additional quests, challenging dungeons and raids, dynamic events" that "feel like a natural part of Azeroth". A separate post that day is a **recruiting call** for developers, Discord moderators and in-game staff. A community post on r/wowservers dated today describes the project as Classic+ with **8 additional playable races**; that race count comes from the community post, not from the site pages we read, so we record it as an unconfirmed detail.

## Evergreen HC opens a one-life vanilla realm

A second project published its site this weekend: [Evergreen HC](/servers/evergreen-hc/), a **permanent Vanilla 1.12.1 hardcore server** at `evergreen-hc.com`. You supply your own **unmodified 1.12.1 client** (no launcher, no custom patches), set `set realmlist evergreen-hc.com`, and register on the site. It describes itself as **blizzlike, free, with zero pay-to-win**, permanently on **patch 1.12.1** with normal experience rates and **no seasons and no wipes**.

The hardcore ruleset is the whole product, and it is unusually specific:

- **One life.** On death the character is flagged and **cannot be revived**. Resurrection abilities still cost mana and play their animation, but do nothing, and the release-spirit resurrection window is gone.
- **Server-wide death announcements.** Every death is announced to everyone with the character's **level and cause**, including falling, drowning and pet-caused deaths, with a themed sound. Reaching **level 60** is announced to the whole realm.
- **Rebuilt leashing** that the site says follows **official hardcore server rules**. Leash timers are tracked **per player** rather than one shared monster timer, monsters work a threat-ordered leash queue, and the initial no-break radius is cut from **50 to 31 yards**, with a **1,200-yard cap** for open-world elites and bosses. Leash duration runs **11 seconds at levels 1 to 29** up to **15 seconds at 60**. Linked packs share one timer, and the assist window is reduced from **1,500 to 500 milliseconds**.

The clearest limitation is the one the site states itself: "A small, new project. Our population is still growing." No player counter is published, so we record the population band as **unknown** rather than guess at it.
