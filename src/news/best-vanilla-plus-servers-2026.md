"Vanilla+" — the original 2004 world with new content built on top — has become the most competitive niche in the private server scene. Blizzard's silence on official Classic+ left a gap, and after Turtle WoW's shutdown flooded communities with displaced players, the surviving projects are fighting hard for them.

We track [66 servers](/) and update statuses as they change. These are the strongest Vanilla+ / Classic+ options playable right now, in rough order of who they suit best.

## Capybara WoW — the biggest community

Run by ex-Turtle SEA admins on the Turtle 1.18.1 core, two realms, around 10k online at peak. If you want the closest thing to the old Turtle experience with a large population behind it, this is it. → [Capybara WoW](/servers/capybara-wow/)

## Octo WoW — the structured one

Restored the 1.18.1 core in July 2026 with three realms — N'Zoth PvE, C'thun Hardcore, Y'Shaarj PvP — and a phased raid schedule running through 2027. Best for players who want a roadmap, not surprises. → [Octo WoW](/servers/octo-wow/)

## RavenCraft — the direct continuation

Built by former Turtle project contributors on the original 1.18.1 core; launches August 22. The heir apparent for Turtle loyalists — new enough that everyone starts fresh together. → [RavenCraft](/servers/ravencraft/)

## Caverns of Time — already live

Turtle+ project launched August 15 with an active population from day one. A solid pick if you missed RavenCraft's launch window and want a fresh-ish economy now. → [Caverns of Time](/servers/caverns-of-time/)

## Project Legacy — independent and feature-rich

Independent Vanilla+ on VMaNGOS: 150+ new quests, cross-faction play, dual spec, plus Prestige and Lorewalker modes. Not a Turtle fork — its own take on the genre, which matters for longevity. → [Project Legacy](/servers/project-legacy/)

## SandWorlds — the Brazilian powerhouse

Turtle-derived Vanilla+ hosted in Brazil: 13 custom zones, class reworks making all specs viable, cross-faction. The low-latency pick for South America, and one of the most content-dense servers anywhere. → [SandWorlds](/servers/sandworlds/)

## Classic Plus - Ashbringer — the Season-of-Discovery style

SoD-style gameplay on a stable 3.3.5a base: level 25 cap, a custom map called Mythia, and 277 runes reshaping every class. For players who want Vanilla reinvented rather than extended. → [Classic Plus - Ashbringer](/servers/classic-plus-ashbringer/)

## Conquest of Azeroth — the wild card

Ascension's custom-class project: fully classless builds on a Vanilla foundation. Legally contested — Blizzard sued Ascension in June — but still online and still the most radical buildcrafting in the scene. → [Conquest of Azeroth](/servers/conquest-of-azeroth/)

## How to choose

Population first: a Vanilla+ server lives or dies on its community, so prefer the largest that fits your region and playstyle. Freshness second — RavenCraft and Caverns of Time have young economies where gear gaps are small. And check monetization before investing time: every major server shutdown this year involved donation shops, while purchase-free projects carry materially less risk.

If Project Camelot becomes an official Classic+ at BlizzCon next month, this list will look different by winter. Until then, these eight are where the genre's energy actually is.
