September 14, 2026 — Blizzard's live WoW Q&A airs **Thursday, September 17** on the official Warcraft Twitch and YouTube (VOD after), in **two segments**: modern WoW starts at **10:00 a.m. PT** and the **WoW Forever segment follows at 10:30 a.m. PT** (18:30 BST / 17:30 UTC) — per Blizzard's official [watch post](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302544/watch-the-world-of-warcraft-live-q-a-september-17). Questions run through the [NA](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/submit-your-question-for-the-live-wow-qa-on-september-17/2347251) and [EU](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/world-of-warcraft-live-qa-september-17/628062) forum threads; Retail and Forever questions both welcome. Our [beta verification hit list](/news/forever-beta-verification-hit-list/) covers what datamining can answer — these are the ten that need a developer voice.

## The questions

1. **Does Onyxia's Lair open December 9** with Hyjal Summit and the Barrow Deeps, or later?
2. **Beta close: October 21 or 22?** Purchase terms and roadmap disagree by a day.
3. **Hardcore ruleset: what are the rules?** Death = transfer out is confirmed; Appleblerone restrictions, trading limits, and the "next year" date are not.
4. **Token and boosts: "right now" or never?** Clay Stone's wording leaves the door open — is the door actually open?
5. **Skyborne subtype names.** Are Windshaper/High Order the real faction names?
6. **Legacy catch-up.** A player starting in 2027 faces 65+ points of challenges — any catch-up, or is the gap the point?
7. **Realmless load.** One ruleset per region means launch-day concurrency on shared infrastructure — what are the caps and queues plan?
8. **Transmog endgame.** Epic-raid-appearances-to-recipient is confirmed; are there plans for appearance sources beyond dungeons/raids?
9. **WotLK Anniversary.** TBC Anniversary marches on with no Wrath announcement — is one coming, and does Forever change the plan?
10. **Era's future.** With Forever as the third experience, what happens to Era and seasonal experiments long-term?
