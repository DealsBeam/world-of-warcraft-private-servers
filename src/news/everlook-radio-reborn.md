Turtle WoW's in-game radio outlived the server that carried it.

Everlook Broadcasting Co. — tuned via a button on Turtle WoW's minimap — ran full community operation with DJs like Zappo Zapblast, the live show *Living in the Past*, voice-acted skits and RP ads, plus a *WoW Radio* app on Google Play and a YouTube channel. When Turtle WoW shut down on [May 15, 2026](/news/turtle-wow-shutdown/) after Blizzard's binding injunction, the minimap tuner died with the server. The signal did not.

The team rebranded as **Out of Bounds Entertainment** — "Your Gaming Radio Station. From the old Everlook Broadcasting Co. team" — and kept streaming around the clock. The station is still live at [outofbounds.live](https://www.outofbounds.live/) and [radio.outofbounds.live](https://radio.outofbounds.live/public/out_of_bounds), with direct [radio.mp3](https://radio.outofbounds.live/listen/out_of_bounds/radio.mp3), `.pls` and `.m3u` feeds.

Listen now: [Tune in on our Radio page](/radio/) (one-click play, now-playing + Live indicator) or [direct stream](https://radio.outofbounds.live/listen/out_of_bounds/radio.mp3). The in-game button is gone; the community soundtrack survived.
