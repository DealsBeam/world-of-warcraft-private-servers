---
title: "Everlook Radio lives on as Out of Bounds Entertainment — the station that outlived its server"
date: 2026-05-20
---

Turtle WoW's in-game radio outlived the server that carried it.

Everlook Broadcasting Co. — tuned via a button on Turtle WoW's minimap — ran full community operation with DJs like Zappo Zapblast, the live show *Living in the Past*, voice-acted skits and RP ads, plus a *WoW Radio* app on Google Play and a YouTube channel. When Turtle WoW shut down on [May 15, 2026](/news/turtle-wow-shutdown/) after Blizzard's binding injunction, the minimap tuner died with the server. The signal did not.

The team rebranded as **Out of Bounds Entertainment** — "Your Gaming Radio Station. From the old Everlook Broadcasting Co. team" — and kept streaming around the clock. The station is still live at outofbounds.live and radio.outofbounds.live, with direct radio.mp3, `.pls` and `.m3u` feeds.

Listen now: [Tune in on our Radio page](/radio/) (one-click play, now-playing + Live indicator) or direct stream. The in-game button is gone; the community soundtrack survived.
