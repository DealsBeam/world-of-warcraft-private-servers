Blizzard's **World of Warcraft: Forever Headlines a Packed WoW Weekly**, dated **25 September**, is not a news post in substance. It is an **index of six Forever explainer articles plus podcast episode 1**, wrapped in the usual Trading Post, Brewfest and Portal Room items.

Its value to us was as a completeness check, and it found gaps.

## What it listed

| Linked item | Did we have it covered? |
| --- | --- |
| Podcast episode 1 | yes |
| Choose your ruleset | **no** |
| Create a name of your own | partially, under naming mechanics |
| Create the hero you want to be | **no**, under race and class combinations |
| The new Skyborne race | yes |
| The Legacy System | **no** |
| October Trading Post | not covered |
| Brewfest | not covered |

Three of the six Forever explainers had no dedicated post on this site at the time: **rulesets**, **race and class combinations**, and the **Legacy System**. The Legacy System gap closed on 2 October with [our breakdown](/news/forever-legacy-system-breakdown-sep-23/), which turned out to be the most mechanically dense thing Blizzard has published about Forever outside the class deep dives.

## The two-name thing, in Blizzard's words

The character-naming post is described as giving names "more room to breathe through a new **two-name structure** designed for a larger, **realmless** Azeroth".

Two phrases in there are worth holding onto. **Two-name** means characters can carry a first and last name rather than a single string, which is a visible departure from Classic's naming. **Realmless** means the sharding question we have written about has an answer, and the answer is that the default experience is not realm-scoped.

That single sentence explains more about how Forever is structured than most of the marketing around it, and it is a quote rather than an inference.

## Why a content index is worth reading

Because it is Blizzard telling us what it considers the explainers a new player needs, in priority order, three weeks out. The list is rulesets, naming, hero creation, Skyborne, Legacy. **Rulesets first**, which is the choice that determines what kind of world you are in, and **Legacy** in the list at all, which means the account-wide system is a launch feature rather than a post-launch addition.

Our own beta coverage had been bottom-up: datamined mounts, quest text, addon tooling, build numbers. This is top-down and it is a different picture. Both are worth having and neither substitutes for the other.
