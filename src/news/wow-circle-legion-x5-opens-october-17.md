WoW Circle is opening a fresh **Legion 7.3.5 x5** realm today. The network's own news pages date the opening to **October 17 2026 at 19:00 Moscow (GMT+3), which is 16:00 UTC**, and it is the first of a planned run of three.

## What the project's own pages say

- **Realm:** WoW Circle Legion **7.3.5 x5**, listed as a **PVP** realm. Rates are **x5 experience and x2 for everything else**.
- **Content opens in stages, beginning with patch 7.0**, in what the project describes as a faster rollout than its usual pace.
- **Custom additions** named in the announcement: a PvE spectator, an arena spectator, and a RePlay arena for reviewing matches. A VIP account is also listed, which the post says will not be available at launch.
- Every "first on the server" achievement title will be handed out.
- At our read the new world was still in **open beta on the existing x4 realm** ("Текущий статус: ОБТ в рамках x4"), with registration and a personal account page already live.

## A series of fresh realms, not one

The Legion opening is the first of three. A September 30 announcement describes a "new format" of consecutive fresh realms:

- **Legion x5:** October 17 2026.
- **Battle for Azeroth x5:** planned for around December 2026.
- **Shadowlands x5:** planned for around February 2027.

All three carry the same x5 experience and x2 other rates, and each opens its content in stages. The project says it is not forcing merges, and that the new realms are meant to become long-term homes for older characters once their content is complete.
