**Anachronos opened early access today, October 8 2026**, capped at level 10.

## The march to 60, dated

The project's own schedule, published on its front page:

- **08/10: early access, level cap 10.** Today.
- **22/10: official launch, level cap 20.**
- Progression continues to **60**, with **45 days of preparation before the first raid** and **seven content phases**.

That is about as specific as a launch plan gets in this scene: two dates, two caps, a prep window and a phase count, all published before opening rather than announced after.

## The realm

Brazilian Vanilla, XP **1x with 2x optional**, improved drops, accelerated rest, **partybot assistance**. A full game guide and workshop section with server rates, talents, professions and raid documentation already live.

The combination of classic pace with optional accelerants is the now-standard answer to the aging-player problem, and Anachronos states both halves plainly rather than hiding the boosts.

## Uldaris, alongside

Also added today: **Uldaris**, a WotLK project describing its experience as **Offylike** with working raids and quests at 1x rates, custom events offered as optional rather than default, and a launcher download. No player count published; tier `unknown` like Anachronos.

Both entries carry no population band, because neither site publishes a figure and launch-day crowds are the noisiest possible measurement.
