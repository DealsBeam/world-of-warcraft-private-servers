Quissy published the first episode of a weekly Forever roundup on **October 7**, covering the first week of October in a 14-minute video with chapter timestamps and a written highlights page. It is third-party reporting that points at Blizzard's own October 1 notes reply, and the specifics are worth recording with that attribution.

## The XP clarification that matters most

Dungeon quest XP was cut **50 percent**, and the cut applies to the **bonus XP above normal quest values, not the whole reward**.

Quissy's illustrative example, flagged as example rather than a specific quest: a 1,000 base plus 2,000 bonus reward becomes 2,000 total instead of 3,000. The base survives; the bonus halves.

That distinction changes the argument about the cut entirely. A halved total would be a different levelling curve. A halved bonus is a smaller nerf aimed at dungeon-repeat efficiency, and Quissy's open question is the right one: after your own quests are done, does helping a friend still feel worthwhile?

## Class and system changes listed

| Area | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Feral Druid | Tiger's Fury removed; Shifting Power converts Mana to Energy |
| Warrior | Fury talents, Rage and off-hand mechanics adjusted |
| Hunter, Warlock | Aggressive Mode returns for pets |
| PvP | Most gear and token Honor costs up ~50%; cap raised 15,000 to 25,000 |
| Skinning | Creatures skinnable only once |
| Enchanting | Crafted common and uncommon wands vendor for one copper |

Plus: **level 30 cap** with the **Excavation Site** dungeon in the Wetlands for 26 to 31, **ray-traced shadows temporarily removed**, SD portrait icons matching the character toggle, Ghost Wolf appearance work, and Stitches back.
