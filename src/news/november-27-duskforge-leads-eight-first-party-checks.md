On November 27 2026 we crawled Duskforge for new blog and article leads and checked every candidate at an official Blizzard page. Duskforge is a fan site and supplied titles only. No Duskforge wording became a fact here. Seven items come from Blizzard's Diablo IV season article and running patch notes, and one comes from Blizzard's World of Warcraft: Forever news post. We did not add tracker entries or population bands because this batch has no private server candidates.

## Horadric affixes split and class bonuses change

Blizzard's [Season of Tyrael's Gambit article](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24316979/advance-the-war-against-death-in-season-of-tyraels-gambit) says Horadric affixes have been split into Weapon and Armor categories since the PTR, to encourage choice and limit the strongest effects. Jewelry affixes raise the relevant Talisman set bonus count by 1. Unlike Seal of the Diamond Mind, one charm from a set is enough to activate that Horadric affix's two piece bonus. Weapon and Armor affixes trigger when the character has at least a two piece bonus from the named set, with effects such as skill ranks, bonus damage and defensive perks.

The [3.3.0 patch notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24316980/diablo-iv-patch-notes) list class by class affix changes. Druid's Rampage duration affix was replaced by "of Stoicism", which grants damage reduction per stack. The Necromancer Horadric Bone Skill Damage tooltip now labels its bonus multiplicative, and Paladin's Cathan's Dauntless Faith affix now grants multiplicative damage to Weakened enemies. Rogue's Critical Strike and Close Enemy affixes were made multiplicative, Sorcerer's Habacalva's Cauldron affix now grants multiplicative damage over time, and Warlock's Demonform and Shadowform bonuses were made multiplicative.

**Dated note, November 27 2026:** these affix details are new. The Barbarian values from the same patch notes were covered on November 25. Blizzard's launch article and build notes are the source of record where earlier guides used PTR values.

## Reliquary rewards get named themes

Blizzard's season article lists the Battle Pass Reliquary available to all. Its named rewards include Doorway of the Kami Portal, Ki-Rin Mount, Red Bridle of the Ki-rin Mount Armor, Tooth of the Yokai Dagger and Koi Lantern Offhand. The Deluxe Battle Pass costs 2,800 Platinum and adds Sashimono of the Emperor Back Trophy, Aka-oni Companion Pet and Kuwagata Emperor Armor Bundle. These are Blizzard's own reward names. We are not adopting a theme label of our own.

## Seasonal Blessings add a reputation option

Within Season Rank, Smoldering Ashes are earned from Seasonal Objectives and Chapter Rewards. Blizzard lists five Blessings that can be bought with them. Urn of Tyrael's Blessing boosts reputation from all sources. The others boost rare materials from salvage, Ancestral Cache odds from Whispers, extra Obducite drops and extra glyph upgrades. That is the list in Blizzard's article, not an estimate from a fan site.

## Seasonal Ranks include the Stamper Stag pet

Blizzard says there are nine Seasonal ranks. The listed rewards include up to 11 Skill Points, up to 42 Paragon Points, up to 13 Resplendent Sparks and up to five Mythic Unique Journey Caches. Rank 5 grants the Mail Time! Stag emblem, and Rank 6 grants the Stamper (Stag) Pet. Titles arrive at Ranks 1 and 8, the Standard of Justice Mount Trophy at Rank 3, and Seasonal Title Laurels at Ranks 3, 7, 8 and 9. We are not calling the pet free, because the first-party article only states it as a Seasonal Rank reward.

## Helltide chests open without a channel

The running patch notes add a detail the November 25 Helltide brief did not record: overall loot quality from Helltide chests has been improved, and chests can now be opened instantly instead of requiring a channel. **Dated note, November 27 2026:** this is the chest change only. The Cinders cap, reset behavior and Helltide timing were covered in [our earlier launch brief](/news/two-blizzard-confirmations-and-six-server-checks-nov-25/) and are not restated as new here.

## Narrow carryover details, grouped as one item

This item groups two narrow details about features we have already covered.

First, the patch notes say the Horadric Cube 3-to-1 recycle recipe now preserves the Crafted designation when any input item is Crafted, preventing players from bypassing Crafted Mythic equipment limits.

Second, the season article adds that the Prime Evil Council, already covered as returning content, works like a traditional Lair Boss for summoning and claiming rewards. No materials are needed to fight it, but each player needs their own Wirt's Lucky Coin to open the Council's Hoard chest.

**Dated note, November 27 2026:** these narrow details supplement the earlier carryover brief. They are not a new announcement that the Council returns.

## Activity tree nodes get their own pass

Blizzard's article says Lair Boss, The Pit, Whispers and Undercity activity trees received new nodes, new connections, position swaps and better loot. The patch notes give examples: Cursed Machines in The Pit treat Glyph Upgrade Success Chance as five tiers higher, Whispers now offer Ancestral Caches or Junk instead of Greater Caches, and Undercity's Trials and Tributes chests are now free to open. This is separate from the two new War Plans trees covered on November 25.

## Forever announces the Al'Aketh Rising motion comic

Blizzard's [November 24 Forever post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24317680/dont-miss-the-world-of-warcraft-forever-comic-alaketh-rising) announces a motion comic named Al'Aketh Rising. It follows Constable Aonda of the Skyborne into a remote settlement that appears to be under strict control. The post credits Sam Slade reading as Constable Aonda, Robert Brooks as writer, Stefano Porcu for art and cover, Mauro Gulma for colors and AndWorld Design for letters. A downloadable PDF is linked from the post. No release date beyond the November 24 article date is stated there, so we are not assigning one.
