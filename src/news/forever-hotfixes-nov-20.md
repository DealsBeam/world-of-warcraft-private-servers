Blizzard published a new hotfix pass at **00:18 UTC on November 21**, which is the evening of November 20 in Pacific time. The running article was retitled **"WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 20, 2026"**. The live client moved to **1.60.1.71037** on November 20, the first new build since 70959 on November 17.

The headline is a quiet but significant change to dungeon farming: resets are now capped at **five per hour**, down from ten.

## Dungeon resets are halved

The whole note is one line:

> Players can now reset and enter each dungeon a maximum of 5 times per hour (was 10).

That is a change to how often a single character can re-enter an instance, and it cuts the previous ceiling in half. For anyone farming a specific boss or a rare drop, the practical throughput is now lower. We do not know why Blizzard made the change, because the pass has no developer note attached to it, so we are not attributing a reason.

The same section fixes a **rebel stone pillar in Blackrock Spire** that could trap players.

## Two class fixes, one of them an exploit

- **Combat Rogue:** Blade Flurry now correctly causes Shank's damage to hit an additional target.
- **Demonology Warlock:** Blizzard fixed a bug where the effects of **Demonic Aegis** could be snapshotted into Demon Armor or Demon Skin and then kept after unlearning the talent. Both the talent effects and Demon Skin are now removed when talents are unlearned.

The Warlock line is a genuine exploit fix: the bug let a player keep a talent's benefit after spending the point elsewhere.

## Stormshroud Armor nerfed

The 3-piece bonus changed twice in one line:

- chance on melee attack: **3 percent**, down from 4;
- energy restored: **18**, down from 30.

That is a substantial reduction in the value of the set's signature effect, and it is the only item line in the pass.

## Professions, quests and the group finder

- **Toron Rockhoof** has repaired his forge and anvil at **Summit of Eternity**; both are usable for blacksmithing and smelting again.
- **The Hinterlands:** Witch Doctor Ti'ik now drops multiple heads when several players on "Witch Doctor Ti'ik" kill him together. Blizzard's own note adds, "It be da voodoo, mon."
- **Feralas:** Perfect Yeti Hide can no longer drop again while you are already on the quest it starts.
- **Tanaris:** Gor'marok respawns faster for "The Dunemaul Compound".
- **Riverglades:** Dunston Willis no longer runs partway through "Dunston Checks Out".
- **UI:** a bug that made some players see fewer groups than intended in the group listing has been fixed.

The Riverglades and Blackrock Spire fixes continue a run of small repairs to the new and reworked content.

## The two outstanding items

The **Auction House changes** promised for the November 17 maintenance and the return of **Item Restoration** are still not in the notes. The running article still carries the November 6 line disabling Item Restoration, with no restoration note. We continue to withhold both.

## What the new build tells us

The live `wowf` track moved to **1.60.1.71037** with a manifest timestamp of **23:26 UTC on November 20**. Blizzard does not key hotfix notes to build numbers, so we record the two facts side by side rather than treating the build as part of the patch list.

ForeverDB's changelog still lists **70959** as its newest extracted build at our November 21 read. We did not pair individual lines from this pass with client diffs.
