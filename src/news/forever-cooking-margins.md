Market data shared by **Evan Luza through ForeverEcon** and reported by Duskforge gives a rare look at WoW Forever's auction house by profession. The figures are dated **November 12, 2026** and may have moved since, especially after the economy events we have covered.

## The headline numbers

- Cooking accounts for just **0.73 percent** of auction house activity.
- Around **33,500 cooked meals sell every day**, making Cooking the **third most active profession by unit sales**.
- The total value of those daily trades is only about **414 gold**.
- Tailoring, by comparison, moves roughly **2,688 gold** a day.

The mismatch is the point. Cooking moves a lot of units at very low individual prices. A high percentage margin on a cheap recipe does not necessarily produce much gold.

## Two example recipes

| Recipe | Material cost | Reported sale price | Reported margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Longjaw Mud Snapper | about 6 copper | about 68 copper | very high percentage |
| Steaming Stag Steak | about 25 silver | about 75 silver | about 46 silver after the AH cut |

Cooking also has a structural advantage: it is a secondary profession, so taking it does not cost you one of your two primary slots.

## What we are not claiming

These are market observations from one data source on November 12, not a guaranteed return. Duskforge notes that prices change quickly and that a recipe with an excellent percentage margin may not sell. We have not independently sampled the auction house.

The timing also matters. Our November 17 enforcement post covered the Blackmaw Hold gold exploit and the removal of gold from accounts, so the economy may not match the November 12 readings.
