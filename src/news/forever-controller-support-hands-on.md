September 14, 2026: a BlizzCon footnote with real weight. Per [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/en/wow-playing-forever-with-controller-this-apparently-works-really-well,1587811/) (Karsten Scholz, Sep 14) summarizing a [playday.one](https://playday.one/2026/09/13/i-played-wow-with-a-controller-officially/) hands-on, Forever ships **official controller support**, currently in alpha, playable by attendees on the show floor.

How it works: one button toggles between keyboard-and-mouse and controller, and the interface reflows automatically, with standard menus switching to a **circular radial menu**. Abilities sit on the action keys combined with the two back shoulder buttons, plus a freely assignable D-pad, for **over 20 assignments**. Targeting runs on the two front shoulder buttons: left for allies, right for enemies, repeated presses cycling targets, holding opening direct selection. **Area effects cannot be placed manually yet**; they trigger centered on the current target.

The hands-on verdict: workable after a short adjustment period, with one honest fumble, a healer struggling to snap-select themselves in hectic moments. Practice should close that gap, but it marks where the alpha stands.

What it means stays carefully fenced. Previously, pad play needed external helpers; official support ends that need. Whether this is a test case for retail WoW or a first step toward a console port is unconfirmed speculation, and this piece claims neither. Blizzard has confirmed only that Forever gets it.

SOURCES: Mein-MMO (Sep 14); playday.one hands-on (Sep 13). Alpha status and limits as reported.
