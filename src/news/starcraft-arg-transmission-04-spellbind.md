## What changed

- **New layer:** censorship framing. Earlier transmissions were leaked fragments; this one is the Dominion telling you what to believe. The cover-up is now part of the fiction.

- **Pattern confirmed:** every disaster gets a mundane cause. Gas leak here, mine fires elsewhere. The ARG is teaching you to distrust the official cause before the game even announces.
- **Outside corroboration:** Corden's footage report (Marines vs Zerg, open world, cinematic campaign) plus Dan Hay leading. The shooter theory is now sourced, not just speculated.
