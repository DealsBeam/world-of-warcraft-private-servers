## WoW finals

**MDI: Team Liquid.** The dungeon race favorite lived up to the bracket billing from [our preview](/news/awc-mdi-blizzcon-2026-preview/) — the team that beat Mandatory in the semi took the championship Sunday.

**AWC: Gators Back over Echo.** This is the headline result. Echo came in as the three-time defending champion and the strongest name in the bracket, with a title run on the table that would have made it four straight. Gators Back — the clear underdog in most pre-event coverage — won the series and denied the streak.

## Beyond WoW

**Overwatch World Cup:** Korea beat Saudi Arabia in the grand final, keeping the World Cup's strongest national program on top. **Hearthstone Masters Tour:** OTGxhho beat XiaoT. **Blizzard Classic Cup:** Team Tasteless claimed the Classic showcase.
