September 22, 2026: the set-bonus picture widens past the crafted tier. classicwowforever's [Sep-22 series](https://classicwowforever.com/updates/forever-deep-dive/#series-wow-forever-dungeon-sets-discoveries) reports beta-client bonuses for the classic dungeon sets: **Defias, Postmaster, Necropile and Bloodmail**, with the tradeoffs they suggest and explicit datamine limits.

This sits beside, not inside, the [Artisan crafted tier](/news/forever-datamine-round-two-crafted-tier/): dungeon sets are world-drop and dungeon-drop bonuses with their own acquisition logic, and their values test differently (leveling gear you outgrow versus endgame pieces you plan around). The series states the limits up front: beta client, subject to change, gaps where the files are sealed.

What to watch: whether any dungeon-set bonus survives contact with the sealed loot tables, and whether Blizzard tunes them as leveling flavor or genuine pre-raid targets.
