Multi-expansion networks are where a single provider keeps parallel realms for several eras under one account. As of September 2, 2026 the tracker lists 109 servers and 1 of them is classified directly as Multi — [WoW Circle](/servers/wow-circle/) — though the true multi-realm experience also appears through large networks that span eras without using the Multi tag. This page is reviewed September 2, 2026 and each Multi card links to its [/servers/…](/) detail and related history entries. Start at the [full tracker](/) to compare Multi against single-era filters like [Vanilla](/vanilla/), [TBC](/tbc/), [WotLK](/wotlk/), [Cataclysm](/cataclysm/), [MoP](/mop/), [Legion](/legion/) and [TWW](/tww/). The canonical example, [WoW Circle](/servers/wow-circle/), operates 13 realms ranging from TBC through Dragonflight with a large Russian-speaking community and is the only realm in the data set currently tagged Multi, making it the simplest way to stay inside one ecosystem while swapping expansions. Beyond that tag, the same pattern is visible in networks tracked elsewhere: TwinStar runs [Apollo II](/servers/apollo-ii/) in Cataclysm, [Helios](/servers/helios/) and the upcoming [Perseus](/servers/perseus/) and [Whitemane — Stormstout](/servers/whitemane-stormstout/) in MoP, plus [Whitemane — Sunwell](/servers/whitemane-sunwell/) in TBC; Atlantiss threads [Atlantiss](/servers/atlantiss/) Cata together with MoP and TBC realms; Whitemane itself covers Cataclysm via [Whitemane — Gilneas](/servers/whitemane-gilneas/) and [Whitemane — Maelstrom](/servers/whitemane-maelstrom/) and WotLK via [Whitemane — Frostmourne](/servers/whitemane-frostmourne/); Firestorm bridges [Firestorm](/servers/firestorm/) TWW and legacy; Tauri bridges [Tauri](/servers/tauri/) Legion with its broader network. If you value one login, shared forums and cross-era events, Multi networks reduce friction versus juggling separate providers. If you prefer era-specific polish, a dedicated [WotLK](/wotlk/) or [Vanilla](/vanilla/) realm may still script that era more deeply than a broad network. For network explainers see [Whitemane network](/blog/whitemane-network-explained/) and browse [news](/news/).

 Practical guidance for Multi networks: if you like to respec expansions without re-registering, start with WoW Circle or a networked suite like Whitemane or Atlantiss where forum accounts and Discord carry across realms. If you are era-committed, a dedicated single-tag realm still often wins on scripting depth, because its dev team only maintains one core version. Compare Multi utility against staying put in [WotLK](/wotlk/) for population, [Vanilla](/vanilla/) for purism, or [Legion](/legion/) and [TWW](/tww/) for modern systems.
