Wrath of the Lich King (3.3.5a) is the busiest expansion in private servers, and the numbers show it. As of September 2, 2026 the tracker lists 109 servers in total and 37 of them sit in the WotLK era, the single largest pure-expansion bucket. That scale comes from a mature core ecosystem — TrinityCore and AzerothCore have been iterated for over a decade — so WotLK realms tend to be the most script-complete, best documented, and most varied. Review date for this page is September 2, 2026, with each realm card showing status, population tier and last-checked date. Start at the [full tracker](/) and filter to WotLK to see playable, in-development and dead together, then open any [/servers/…](/) detail for related history. If you want blizzlike, consider [Warmane](/servers/warmane/) for sheer population, [Chromiecraft](/servers/chromiecraft/) for staged, upstreamed fixes, [Sunwell.pl](/servers/sunwell-pl/), [Dalaran-WoW](/servers/dalaran-wow/), [TrueWoW](/servers/truewow/), [WoW-Mania](/servers/wow-mania/) and the ultra-clean [Elwynnkeep](/servers/elwynnkeep/) with zero custom content. Progressive options include [Eternal Gaming](/servers/eternal-gaming/), [Maelstrom (Millenium)](/servers/maelstrom-millenium/) which caps at 60 until late August, and [Project Alterac](/servers/project-alterac/) in phased rollout. For solo or small-group play, see [TheraWoW](/servers/therawow/), [OnlyFunsWoW](/servers/onlyfunswow/), [Stormgarde](/servers/stormgarde/), [Galaxyofdrone WoW](/servers/galaxyofdrone-wow/), [Warstorm](/servers/warstorm/) and [Triumvirate-WoW](/servers/triumvirate-wow/) which added ICC and Mythic+ Season 2 in August. High-rate and fun varieties include [Sirus](/servers/sirus/) with 6–13k online and custom races, [UnlimitedWoW](/servers/unlimited-wow/) at level 255, and [Azeroth Universe](/servers/azeroth-universe/). Watch for upcoming launches like [Whitemane — Frostmourne](/servers/whitemane-frostmourne/) slated September 9. When picking, weigh population over feature lists, check pay-to-win policies, and cross-compare with [Vanilla](/vanilla/), [Cataclysm](/cataclysm/) and [Legion](/legion/) to avoid choosing solely on nostalgia. Deeper guides live at [best WotLK servers 2026](/blog/best-wotlk-private-servers-2026/) and [best WotLK for solo](/blog/best-wotlk-for-solo/).

 Beyond raw count, distribution matters: WotLK covers blizzlike, progressive, solo-scaling, high-rate and fun varieties in one era, so you can stay within 3.3.5a while changing playstyle entirely. That flexibility keeps queue times low and guild recruitment easier, especially on cross-faction realms like [Eternal Gaming](/servers/eternal-gaming/) and [TheraWoW](/servers/therawow/). Before committing, filter the tracker by popTier and check recent updates — a realm with 0 online and a stale updated date is a warning. For perspective, WotLK at 37 dwarfs [TBC](/tbc/) and [Cataclysm](/cataclysm/) at 7 each and [MoP](/mop/) at 4, so breadth is not the problem, focus is.
