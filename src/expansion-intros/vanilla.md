Vanilla (1.12.1) remains the foundation of the private-server scene, and our tracker reflects that. As of September 2, 2026 we list 109 total servers, with the Vanilla era — Vanilla, Vanilla+ and Classless combined — accounting for 42 tracked realms, the largest grouping on the site. Within that, 16 are tagged pure Vanilla for players who want unmodified 1.12, while 21 are Vanilla+ and 5 are Classless, each adding custom systems without leaving the old world. The list is reviewed September 2, 2026 and statuses shift as realms launch or close, so this page is a snapshot rather than a permanent ranking. If you are new, start from the [full tracker](/) and filter by Vanilla, then narrow by population and status. Pure blizzlike choices include [Elysium Project](/servers/elysium-project/), [Kronos V](/servers/kronos-v/), [Stonetavern](/servers/stonetavern/), [NycterMoon](/servers/nyctermoon/), [Ashen WoW](/servers/ashen-wow/) and [RetroWoW](/servers/retrowow/) for instant-60 fun, plus [SandWorlds](/servers/sandworlds/) and [Greyhaven](/servers/greyhaven/) for fresh progressive takes. For solo-friendly Vanilla, check [SoloCraft](/servers/solocraft/) which offers PartyBots and BattleBots companions. Vanilla+ widens the same world with new quests and balance — see [Project Legacy](/servers/project-legacy/), [Valanior](/servers/valanior/), [Vanilla+](/servers/vanilla-plus/), [Ravencraft](/servers/ravencraft/), [Felskorn](/servers/felskorn/) and [Octo WoW](/servers/octo-wow/) with its guide on [OctoWoW setup](/guides/octowow/). Classless experiments include [GrimfallWoW](/servers/grimfallwow/) and [Centurion — Legionnaire+](/servers/centurion-legionnaire/). Practical picks depend on pace: choose x1 progressive for the long road, x6 or instant-60 if you want raids immediately, and check Discord activity before committing because population matters more than feature lists. Dead or shuttered projects like Everlook are kept in the archive with shutdown dates, so you can see what failed and why. Use the era filter to compare Vanilla against [TBC](/tbc/), [WotLK](/wotlk/) and [Cataclysm](/cataclysm/) and watch the [status summary](/) for playable versus in-development versus dead. For a broader survey of custom Vanilla design, see our [Classic+ overview](/blog/what-is-classic-plus-plain-english/) and the [best Vanilla+ roundup](/blog/best-vanilla-plus-private-servers-2026/).

 Choosing wisely means matching your schedule to rates and rules: pure x1 realms reward patience and guild stability, while high-rate and instant-60 realms let you test specs and raids within hours. Check each card for popTier and last-checked date, then open the [/servers/stonetavern/](/servers/stonetavern/) or [/servers/elysium-project/](/servers/elysium-project/) detail to see historical notes and related shutdown coverage. The Vanilla era dwarfs every other grouping — compare it to [WotLK](/wotlk/) at 37, [TBC](/tbc/) at 7 and [Cataclysm](/cataclysm/) at 7 — so even a conservative shortlist still offers more variety than most later expansions combined. When in doubt, favour active Discord, transparent changelogs and a clear anti-pay-to-win stance. See [tracker](/) and [servers](/servers/) for live status updates and filtering.
