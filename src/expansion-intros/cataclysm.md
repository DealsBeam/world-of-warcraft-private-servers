Cataclysm (4.3.4) has rebounded into a stable niche, anchored by a few large networks and several smaller blizzlike realms. As of September 2, 2026 the tracker counts 109 total servers and 7 of them are grouped under the Cataclysm era, a compact but active set where most population concentrates on two or three networks. Status on this page is reviewed September 2, 2026 and each card exposes status, population and last-checked date with a link to its [/servers/…](/) detail. If you are deciding, open the [full tracker](/) and isolate Cataclysm, then scan playable versus in-development to avoid waiting on a realm that has not launched. The leading cluster is [Whitemane — Gilneas](/servers/whitemane-gilneas/) and [Whitemane — Maelstrom](/servers/whitemane-maelstrom/) on the Whitemane network, both 4.3.4 with 4k+ concurrent and extensive QoL, custom legendaries and solo-queue PvP; they are the default recommendation for players who want a crowded, modernized Cata experience. The independent alternative is [Atlantiss](/servers/atlantiss/) which acts as the flagship of the Atlantiss-Tauri network after its February 2026 relaunch and also operates connected [MoP](/mop/) and [TBC](/tbc/) realms. TwinStar's dedicated Cata realm [Apollo II](/servers/apollo-ii/) remains playable for those who prefer a lighter custom footprint, while [WOW-COLOMBIA](/servers/wow-colombia/) and [WarSages](/servers/warsages/) serve the long-running Spanish-language Cata community with seasonal arena and dragon-era content. One notable in-development Cata project is [Hour of Twilight](/servers/hour-of-twilight/) targeting 2026. When weighing Cata against neighboring eras, compare the 7 Cata realms to 37 in [WotLK](/wotlk/) and 42 grouped in [Vanilla](/vanilla/), and consider whether you want Cata systems like transmog, reforging and LFR versus the older talent trees. For new players, start on a high-population network if you value dungeon finder speed, or pick Atlantiss or Apollo II if you prioritize blizzlike scripting over QoL extras. Follow-up context and network explainers live in our [news](/news/) and [blog](/blog/) sections, and network-wide posts such as the Whitemane explainer connect the dots between expansions.

 If you are new to private servers, a quick way to pick Cata is to decide on language community first — English-global networks versus Spanish ecosystems like [WOW-COLOMBIA](/servers/wow-colombia/) — then decide on rate and QoL tolerance. Blizzlike purists lean toward Apollo II or Atlantiss, while QoL-heavy players gravitate to Whitemane where solo-queue and custom legendaries shorten the grind.
