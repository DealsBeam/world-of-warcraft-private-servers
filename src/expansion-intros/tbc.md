The Burning Crusade (2.4.3) occupies a focused but durable corner of the tracker. As of September 2, 2026 we list 109 total servers and 7 of them are grouped under the TBC era, covering pure 2.4.3 realms plus select progressive takes that start in Outland. That makes TBC smaller than Vanilla or WotLK but large enough to offer real choice between blizzlike, high-rate and instant-70 playstyles. For orientation, begin at the [full tracker](/) then filter by TBC and sort by status to separate playable from in-development. Playable highlights include [Rising Gods - Back2Basics](/servers/rising-gods-back2basics/) with its long-running German PvX community, [Moonwell](/servers/moonwell/) which peaked above 3k on its fresh realm, [Faebright](/servers/faebright/) running TBC on a WotLK client as RP-PvE, [BloodWoW (TBC)](/servers/bloodwow-tbc/) at x15 with BattleBots and cross-faction, and [Tirisgarde](/servers/tirisgarde/) offering instant-70 and custom PvP events. In development, the [Whitemane — Sunwell](/servers/whitemane-sunwell/) TBC realm under the Whitemane network is the most visible upcoming launch, sharing the network's QoL stack seen on [Whitemane — Gilneas](/servers/whitemane-gilneas/) in Cataclysm. If you want historical context, TBC emulation has moved from older MaNGOS cores to modern backports, and our timeline covers setbacks like [Felmyst](/servers/felmyst/) which remains offline since 2023. When choosing, decide pace first: x1 blizzlike for attunement chains and Karazhan progression, x15 or instant-70 if you want to raid quickly, and always verify population tier and last-checked date on the card. Links between eras help: compare TBC sizing against [Vanilla](/vanilla/) at 42 grouped realms and [WotLK](/wotlk/) at 37, or step forward to [Cataclysm](/cataclysm/) and [MoP](/mop/) for later expansions. For methodology notes on how we track status, see the tracker home and the [news archive](/news/) for TBC launch posts.

 For first-time TBC players, a simple workflow works: skim the [TBC phase guide](/blog/tbc-phase3-private-realms-guide/) if present, confirm the realm supports your desired XP rate, then verify Battleground and arena scripting quality via recent news posts. Because TBC realms reuse much of the Vanilla world plus Outland, they benefit from shared tooling yet still need solid attunement and heroic-dungeon scripting, so favour providers with public bug trackers.
