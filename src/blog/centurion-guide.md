Centurion runs Vanilla mechanics on WotLK engine. Since September 18, 2026 it is one realm: the old Legionnaire+ (PvP) and Barracks+ (PvE) merged, characters carried over. Two character types share the account: world characters for the long run, tournament characters for straight fighting.

This guide covers the merged realm, who it fits, and limits. Start at [Centurion](/servers/centurion/).

## At a glance

**One realm:** Vanilla 1.12 ranks and CC via 3.3.5, PvP balance, collision, factionless, world vs tournament characters.

**Systems:** BG cooldown separation, tiered battleground AI, Gurubashi chest on a timer, PvP ladder.

**Status:** playable, merged Sep 18 2026.

**Pop:** tiny.

**Tag:** Vanilla+

**Tracker:** [Centurion](/servers/centurion/)

Data: Instant 60 Vanilla 1.12 mechanics on 3.3.5 client — ranks, CC, cast times via WotLK engine, full collision.

Custom PvP balance keeps Vanilla classes viable. No paywall keeps arena and BGs fair.

Factionless quest support means you quest where you want. No faction gate on hubs.

Opt in hardcore and transmog add spice without forcing it. Pick your risk and style.

No-bind loot changes economy. Loot trades freely, pugs share, alts gear faster. (Carried over from the Barracks+ ruleset in the Sep 18 merge.)

Full collision matters for PvP. Line of sight and body block feel like Vanilla, not WotLK lite.

Playerbots fuel world PvP when pop is thin. Warmode flag controls risk.

Twin rule sets share tech under one realm now. PvP and PvE crowds self select wisely.

## Who it is for

Vanilla PvP purists who want modern engine. Vanilla feel without old client pain.

Instant 60 fans who hate leveling but love ranks and CC. Skip grind, keep depth.

Duelers and arena teams who want balance and collision. Fair fights, no shop gear.

Collectors who like transmog without retail. Show sets while playing Vanilla kits.

PvE players who like tiny community plus bots. The merged realm fits this niche.

## Getting started

Client is 3.3.5 with Centurion patch. Follow site steps, set realmlist, log in fast.

Pick world characters for the long run, tournament characters to just fight. Same account, same patch, choice is character not reinstall.

Learn rank system early. Vanilla ranks matter: pick rank by mana and CC needs.

Try warmode to taste world PvP with bots. Toggle risk as you like.

Track live via [Centurion](/servers/centurion/) and [/servers/?tag=Vanilla+](/servers/?tag=Vanilla+).

Keep a large alt like [Warmane](/servers/warmane/) for mass pugs when tiny feels too quiet.
