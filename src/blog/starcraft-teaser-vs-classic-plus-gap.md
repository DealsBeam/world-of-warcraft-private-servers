TL;DR
- StarCraft site glitches on Remastered section, Sep 3, flagged on Bluesky.
- Nexon deal reported Sep 2 via Chosun, plus Dan Hay shooter and Unreal hire.
- Same 1-hour gap after What's Next that Classic Plus needs. Opening Sep 12 10:30 PDT.

Something is glitching on the StarCraft site. Five outlets reported it Sep 3-4 — Gamespot, GamesRadar, IGN, PC Gamer and Wowhead — all within hours.

The glitch sits on the Remastered marketing copy. Images twitch, text flashes. Not a normal web error. Designed static.

It was flagged on Bluesky by John Carson. The page still sells 2017 Remastered. The effect is new — Wayback Aug 23 shows clean.

Console holds 8 hidden messages — medical reports, Cargo Manifest, access without credentials, Lunar Mining Complex on Roxara, secret Theta Squadron, over a dozen unidentified lifeforms, year 2575 about 70 years after Legacy of the Void.

Reddit calls it an ARG start, with a Day 1 lore dive already up. Effect worsens over time — signal coming through, not a one-off.

BlizzCon map shows a StarCraft II and Remastered Demo section. Odd to demo an old game — unless the demo is new.

StarCraft has been dormant since Remastered 2017. Last original was StarCraft 2 in 2010, expansions through 2015. Ghost was announced 2002, cancelled 2014.

2026 brought two threads. One is a shooter reportedly in works at Arc Raiders publisher, with a BlizzCon reveal report from months ago.

The other is Nexon. Chosun reported Sep 2 that Nexon is close to finalizing a deal to develop a new StarCraft game. Blizzard declined comment. Partnership unconfirmed.

Blizzard also has its own shooter thread. A 2024 job post for open-world shooter pointed to StarCraft, led by ex-Far Cry producer Dan Hay. Hay joined 2022 and is still at Blizzard per LinkedIn.

An Unreal open-world hire, a major StarCraft 2 patch earlier this year, and digital goodies with 15-day co-op XP all add to the pile. Small alone, louder together.

BlizzCon opens Sep 12 10:30 PDT Hall D. The schedule shows a 45-minute What's Next, then a 1-hour gap before Diablo. Only such gap.

Our [hidden-floor post](/blog/blizzcon-hidden-floor-playable-demo/) reads that gray as the playable demo. Our [schedule post](/news/blizzcon-2026-schedule-dates-venue-stream/) has venue, free stream and drops.

PC Gamer notes the floor map shows nothing carved for StarCraft. Even if announced, likely not playable. The gap could still fit a trailer.

For our tracker, the angle is competition. The same 1-hour gap Classic Plus needs is the gap StarCraft needs. Our [Classic+ tracker](/classic-plus/) sits at build 69638 Sep 1 with 41 entries. Our [leak roundup](/blog/blizzcon-2026-leak-roundup/) ranks Camelot above StarCraft on builds, StarCraft above on newspaper sourcing.

If both land, WoW-heavy panels plus StarCraft trailer means a split stage. If one lands, the gap tells you which.

Watch Sep 12 10:30 for what fills the hour. Until then, glitch plus Nexon plus gap is smoke, not fire.
