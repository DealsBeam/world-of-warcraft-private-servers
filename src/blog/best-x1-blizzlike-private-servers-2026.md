Rates are the first thing a private server puts on its front page, and **x1** is the one that claims authenticity: experience, loot and gold at the pace the original expansion shipped them. What that means in practice varies a lot, because the scene now contains two very different kinds of x1 server. Some enforce it, some offer it as one setting among several, and the difference matters more than the number.

A note on bands first: **x1 realms skew smaller**, and many publish no counter at all. Where we have a number we give it, where we do not the tracker says unknown rather than guessing.

## Strict x1: the museum servers

These are the projects where x1 is the design, not an option.

- **[Stonetavern](/servers/stonetavern/)** is the clearest example on the tracker. Its ELWYNN realm is an **unaltered Vanilla 1.12.1 server at the final content patch, opened June 2026, never wiped, at x1 with no XP boost, no quality-of-life automation and no dungeon finder at all**. It is a deliberately preserved experience, and two realms share one account with hand-moved characters between them.
- **[Lighthaven](/servers/lighthaven/)** is an educational, open-source, non-profit AzerothCore Wrath realm with **strict x1 defaults** and opt-in per-character rates via a `.rate` command. There is no shop. Its census showed roughly 6 human players against about 2,004 bots, which is a real number and worth reading as one.
- **[Ashen WoW](/servers/ashen-wow/)** runs Blizzlike Vanilla on the **original 2004 to 2006 patch timeline**, with 1.8 live in September 2026 and 1.12 due September 2027, at x1, no pay-to-win, launched September 2025, in EN, RU and ZH.
- **[Elderhide](/servers/elderhide/)** is a Vanilla 1.12.1 **PvP** realm on VMaNGOS at x1 with open registration. Its population reading was a single player on our last check, which is the risk of the strictest category.
- **[True Azeroth](/servers/true-azeroth/)** and **[GryffinWow](/servers/gryffinwow/)** round out the Vanilla x1 group that keep a blizzlike rate as their baseline.

## Blizzlike with an opt-out

This is where most x1 play actually happens: the rate is x1 by default, and the server lets you turn it up.

- **[Anachronos](/servers/anachronos/)** is a Brazilian Vanilla project that opened early access October 8 2026, capped at level 10, with official launch on **October 22 at a level 20 cap** and seven content phases on the way to 60. It runs **1x with 2x optional**, improved drops, accelerated rest and partybot assistance.
- **[TrueWoW](/servers/truewow/)** has run a **blizzlike PvE Wrath realm at x1 since 2010**, with an optional 3x XP mode and around 200 Discord online. Sixteen years of continuity is the selling point here.
- **[WoW Patagonia](/servers/wow-patagonia/)** runs a Latin American Wrath realm with **per-player configurable rates from x1 blizzlike to x4**, cross-faction grouping, and a front page reading **975 online** on our October 4 check.
- **[Frostbound](/servers/frostbound/)** is a Spanish-first custom Wrath realm at **x1 to x7 XP with x2 loot and x2 gold**, built on AzerothCore with playerbots and a Warband of four player-owned AI alts.
- **[TBC5Legends](/servers/tbc5legends/)** offers **an x1 Journey** alongside an instant level 70 option on a TBC realm built entirely for five-player raiding, with **Tier 6 gating Mount Hyjal on October 17 and Black Temple on October 24**.

## The vet who runs progressive x1

- **[Dalaran-WoW](/servers/dalaran-wow/)** is a twelve-year veteran Wrath realm running **x1 progressive with pre-nerf encounters**, historically one of the most respected blizzlike projects in the scene. The caveat is current: its site returns an **HTTP 503 maintenance notice**, which is a configured message rather than an abandoned host, so the entry stays live with an unknown band. Check before you commit.

## Large networks with an x1 room

If you want x1 with a crowd behind it, the big multi-realm networks are the compromise:

- **[WoW Circle](/servers/wow-circle/)** runs **13 realms**, one of which is a **WotLK 3.3.5a x1** board, inside a network whose front page read **22,558 total online** on October 3. You get a genuine x1 realm with the auction house and battleground population of a much larger operation.
- **[Warmane Lordaeron](/servers/lordaeron/)** is the x1 realm inside the Warmane network, and it is the most populated strictly x1 Wrath option available. It was also our own correction story: we initially published it as unverified and corrected that the same day.

## How to choose

| If you want | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| The strictest unaltered Vanilla | **Stonetavern ELWYNN** |
| Non-profit, no shop, open source | **Lighthaven** |
| Original patch cadence, no catch-up | **Ashen WoW** |
| x1 with an opt-out for alts | **Anachronos, TrueWoW, WoW Patagonia** |
| x1 with a big population behind it | **Warmane Lordaeron, WoW Circle** |
| x1 five-player raiding | **TBC5Legends** |

One honest rule: **on an x1 realm, verify the counter before you invest a character.** Slow levelling is a deliberate commitment, and the projects that enforce it hardest are also the ones most likely to be tiny, because that is who they are for. That is a feature for some players and a trap for others, and the tracker tells you which one you are looking at.
