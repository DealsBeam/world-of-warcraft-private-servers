He reviewed our tracker 13 days ago. We return the favor.

Same creator, plus a third channel Excalligator Reviews. Two channels, one craft: handmade armor and video.

## Why a private-server site spotlights a crafter

Because the private-server scene and the cosplay scene share the same economy. Both are handmade, both are small-team, both are one C&D or one storage-unit rat infestation away from losing the collection. His recent community post — "Some of my books didn't make it. Casualties of the rat infestation in the storage unit include: Lord of the Clans, Rise of the Horde, Blood and Honor, and Tides of Darkness" — reads like a Turtle WoW preservation post, just with paper instead of code.

And because his gaming channel is already doing the work our tracker does, but as video. He was on Ascension's shutdown within a day, on OctoWoW's DDoS for 8+ hours, on the private-server website discourse (his Aug 20 post: "I don't know if it was made by AI. Cool. Don't fucking care. Nearly everything in your house was factory made... Would you all stop using your chairs because they were mass produced?" — a defense of the tracker tool that outlasted the debate).

That video — `Awesome Private Server Tracker Website` — is why he is featured above the fold on our home today. He found a tool fans can use and told his audience. We found a creator fans can watch and are telling ours.
