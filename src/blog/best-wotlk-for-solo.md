WotLK 3.3.5a is the most crowded expansion on our tracker — 36 entries, 32 playable.

The best WotLK for solo is not the biggest. It is the one where the dungeon still opens when your friends don't.

Here are the WotLK solo-friendly rows that actually ship solo systems, ordered by how they solve the same problem.

## The solo problem

Original Wrath needed a roster. You stood in Dalaran for an hour forming a group, then the tank left.

Private WotLK in 2026 solves it two ways: **bots** and **scaling**.

Bots tank, heal, DPS, follow instructions and queue for dungeon finder like players.

Scaling makes dungeon and raid enemies scale with party size — health, damage and related tuning — so smaller groups can attempt content sized for larger rosters.

If you have limited or unpredictable play time, these two systems are the difference between seeing the content and reading about it.

## Top picks

**1. [Old Man Warcraft](/servers/old-man-warcraft/) — the 1.5x WotLK where 78 humans feels full**

Per-char Vanilla→WotLK to tier 18, bots for parties and raids, LLM chat for everyone, Mythic+-style dungeons, guild villages, 1.5x XP.

Solo 3v3 and 1v1 arenas, Nemesis rivalries. Best for adults who want Wrath with modern conveniences and room for playstyles. Small, not large — steady, not spike.

**2. [Warstorm](/servers/warstorm/) — the bot raid that clears ICC25 HC**

Solo WotLK since Sep 2025, ~200 pop, AI PlayerBots that execute ICC25 HC strats — spread for Sindragosa, valves for Putricide, gunship cannons.

Not scaled stats; the bots learn the dance. Best if you want the hardest proof that bots work.

**3. [SoloCraft](/servers/solocraft/) — Vanilla 1.12 PartyBots/BattleBots on 1.14**

Solo-friendly 1.12.1/1.14 with PartyBots + BattleBots AI companions, ~1.7k Discord.

Not WotLK, but the same solo stack on a Vanilla base. Best if you want solo on Vanilla, not WotLK.

**4. [Stormgarde](/servers/stormgarde/) — the WotLK with a bot since 2020**

Solo-friendly WotLK 3.3.5 with Playerbot AI companions since Feb 2020, ~130 Discord.

Longest-running bot companion system. Best if you want stability over novelty.

**5. [TheraWoW](/servers/therawow/) and [Galaxyofdrone WoW](/servers/galaxyofdrone-wow/) — small-group scaling**

TheraWoW: solo/small-group scaling, cross-faction, 500 5-star. Galaxyofdrone: WotLK+ with 40 bots, x5 rates, solo-friendly.

Best if you want a duo or trio without pugging.

## How to pick

- **If you want the most systems:** Old Man Warcraft — tier 18 per-char, bots, LLM chat, Mythic+, villages.
- **If you want the hardest bot proof:** Warstorm — ICC25 HC bots.
- **If you want Vanilla solo:** SoloCraft — PartyBots on 1.12.
- **If you want the oldest bot code:** Stormgarde — since 2020.

All are `small` or `tiny` except Old Man `small` and Warstorm `small`. That is intentional — solo servers stay small by design to keep CPU/DB sane.

Bots are Player objects with AI ticks; 40 bots is 40× pathfinding.
