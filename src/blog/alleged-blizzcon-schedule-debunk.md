An image is making the rounds that gives the community everything it wants at once.

StarCraft Infantry right after Opening Ceremony. A Northrend look for The Last Titan. Diablo What's Next. Then a Warcraft Fables deep dive — read as Classic+. Day Two opens with Overwatch, then a Warcraft Chronicles meet the team for a rumored RTS. Then StarCraft lore and Hearthstone to close.

Three new products. One image. Anonymous 4chan via r/blizzcon. That is the tell.

## What is in the image

The alleged two-day grid hits every rumor thread from the last three months: a StarCraft shooter long talked about, a Classic+ deep dive under a new name (Fables, not Camelot), and a Warcraft RTS (Chronicles) tied to an earlier roadmap graphic.

The image does not describe any of them. Infantry is not labeled shooter. Fables is not labeled Classic+. Chronicles is not described at all. Names without builds, licenses or vendor moves.

Our [leak roundup](/blog/blizzcon-2026-leak-roundup/) already ranks this shape at the bottom: HotS 2 and Cavill sit there for the same reason — single creator speculation, no corroboration.

## Why too perfect means fake

Real leaks are narrow. Stiven's Jun 16 Camelot find was two license strings — Heroic and Epic — on one branch. The Aug 25 Gear Store find was one CSS class — `wow-camelot` to `wow-2`. The Sep 3 Forever find was one bindings file — `DefaultForeverBindings.wtf` with `TOGGLELEGACYSYSTEM`.

Fake leaks are wide. This image gives you shooter, Classic+ and RTS in one grid, plus Northrend, Diablo and Hearthstone around them so the grid looks complete. Well-informed readers noticed: getting three new products out of one BlizzCon sounds too good to be true.

Continued 1.60 activity supports a Classic branch called Camelot. Schreier's book supports a StarCraft shooter existing somewhere. Neither establishes the names in this image or a BlizzCon announcement.

## Test it against the official schedule

As of Aug 27 the official event site said the full schedule is coming soon. Blizzard published its 2023 schedule one week before BlizzCon, so early September for 2026 is plausible.

The 2023 schedule did not reveal announcements — it listed What's Next and Deep Dive panels without naming the games. Expect the same this time: breadcrumbs, not titles.

The Sep 1 official Know Before You Go post published Day1/Day2 grids (`BC26_FullSchedule_16x9`) with the line “check back after Opening Ceremony for the full updated schedule” and a floor map with gray quadrants. Our [hidden-floor post](/blog/blizzcon-hidden-floor-playable-demo/) reads that gray as the playable demo.

If Fables, Infantry and Chronicles were real Day1 titles, they would appear after Opening Ceremony on `blizzcon.com/schedule` — not before, on 4chan. Watch Sep 12 12:00 PDT for what fills the gray. Until then, the image is a wishlist with a grid.
