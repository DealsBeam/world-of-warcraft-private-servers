Should Classic have transmog? MassivelyOP's Sep 3 Casually Classic puts the question plainly, and the comments split the way Classic always splits: purists say no, QoL players say yes, and both claim to speak for Vanilla.

Private servers already voted. On our tracker, transmog is 4-5 live at Classic scope — [Old Man Warcraft](/servers/old-man-warcraft/), [Warstorm](/servers/warstorm/), [Valanior](/servers/valanior/) and others ship it as standard. Achievements are 0. That split is not an accident. It is the line private Classic drew between looking different and playing different.

## Why transmog passed

Transmog does not change power. It changes looks. On a small private realm where the same 78 humans see each other every night, looking different matters more than on a 10k mega-realm where you are one plate set among hundreds.

Old Man Warcraft lists transmog next to AoE loot and guild villages — conveniences, not systems. Warstorm lists it next to dungeon finder and 1-5x XP — QoL, not progression. Valanior lists it as modernization of the 1.12 base — the world stays, the closet grows.

None of those servers report transmog as controversial. The controversy lives on official forums, not on private realm Discords. When the cost of the system is zero power and the benefit is identity, small communities say yes.

## Why achievements did not

Achievements change why you log in. They add a second progression track — points, dates, feats — on top of level and gear. Private Classic rejected that track almost unanimously. Our [wishlist gap](/blog/classic-plus-wishlist-gap/) has achievements at 0 live, transmog at 4-5 live, and the survey that asked for both ranks achievements as divisive and transmog as mid but passable.

The reason is pacing. Classic's loop is slow on purpose: walk, group, wipe, try again. Achievements add a fast loop — pop, toast, points — that belongs to retail's cadence, not Vanilla's. Transmog adds no loop at all. You still earn the item the same way. You just wear it differently.

That is why the same player who wants transmog often rejects achievements in the same post. One preserves the grind. The other gamifies it.

## What Balance WoW adds today

[Balance WoW](/servers/balance-wow/) — hardcore 3.3.5a, cap 30 via ChromieCraft timeline, added Sep 3 — ships transmog fully. Hardcore is the strictest ruleset private WoW has: one DC, one mistake, ghost, no ticket restore. If transmog belonged anywhere least, it would be there.

It belongs there anyway. The realm's feature list puts transmog next to world chat and cross-realm, not next to difficulty. Difficulty is mobs that Charge, Daze, Fear and Thunderclap. Looks are free.

That placement answers MassivelyOP's question better than any forum poll: even hardcore thinks looks are QoL.

## What Classic Plus should take

If Camelot is Classic Plus and it ships on Sep 12, the transmog question will be decided in the first patch note, not the tenth. Our take from the tracker:

Ship transmog. It is 4-5 live, zero controversy, QoL only.

Hold achievements. It is 0 live, divisive, second loop.

The survey agrees — transmog mid but passable, achievements split hard, pet battles and housing bottom rejected by both survey and tracker. At least everyone agrees on something.

Classic without transmog is still Classic. Classic with transmog is still Classic, just better dressed. Classic with achievements is a different game wearing the same map — the Minus test from our [Classic Minus piece](/blog/classic-minus-what-would-you-remove/) holds.

Watch Sep 12 for the closet, not the points.
