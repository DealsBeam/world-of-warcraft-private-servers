TL;DR
- Day1: icon 15m to loot box 8h, Cat skins, 500 currency.
- Day2: icon 15m to loot box 8h, Domina skins, 500 currency.
- 16h total split means weekend play, not retention. Watch Classic slot.

Overwatch gets two ladders, not one. Day1 on Sep 12, Day2 on Sep 13. Both 15 minutes to 8 hours.

Day1: Day Icon 15m, Loot Box 30m, Fish Tacos Spray 1h, Home Jetpack Cat 2h, Name Card 3h, Away Cat 4h, Spray 5h, Esports Box 6h, Sunset Spray 7h, Esports Box 8h.

Day2: Night Icon 15m, Loot Box 30m, Tacos Spray 1h, Home Domina 2h, Night Card 3h, Away Domina 4h, Selfie Spray 5h, Esports Box 6h, Celebration Spray 7h, Esports Box 8h.

Every reward also grants 50 Event Currency — up to 500 in a single day.

Compare: WoW 12h over 15 days is retention. Diablo 6h over 4 days is launch. Overwatch 16h over 2 days is weekend.

For our tracker, the split matters. Two ladders means Overwatch owns both days, not one. Our [Classic+ tracker](/classic-plus/) sits at 69638 with 42 entries. Our [schedule post](/news/blizzcon-2026-schedule-dates-venue-stream/) has Hall D Opening 10:30 PDT and Community Night 4:45-7pm.

If Overwatch takes Day1 afternoon and Day2 afternoon, Classic keeps the 1-hour gap after What's Next on Day1 morning. If Overwatch slips, Classic slips.

Watch Sep 12 10:30 for order. Overwatch early means Classic late. Late is prime.
