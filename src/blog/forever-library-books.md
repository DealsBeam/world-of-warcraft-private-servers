Books picked up in the world are not just flavour in WoW Forever. Community reporting says any class can donate them at a capital library for gear, even though the mechanic echoes the Mage book collection from Season of Discovery. Runes themselves have not been carried over.

The reward thresholds and item names below come from a Blizzard Watch guide published November 20. One part is independently confirmed: Blizzard's own **November 13 hotfix notes** mention **Owen Thadd** and **Garion Wendell** accepting books in Undercity and Stormwind again after a fix. We have not tested the rest in game, and there is no Blizzard guide publishing the reward table.

## Where to turn books in

| Faction | NPC | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Alliance | **Garion Wendell** | Top of the tower in Stormwind's Mage Quarter |
| Horde | **Owen Thadd** | Library in the Undercity's Magic Quarter |

The collectible books have the tooltip line "A fine addition to any library's collection". Both NPCs are named in Blizzard's November 13 notes, which is the first-party part of this guide.

## The reported reward thresholds

| Donations | Level | Reward choices |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | none stated | Scholarly Pendant or Erudite's Amulet |
| 20 | 20 | Field Researcher's Loop or Philanthropist's Ring |
| 25 | 30 | Truthseeker's Bow, Crest of Elucidation or Researcher's Night Light |
| around 36 | 50 | Antique Bulwark, Museum Keeper's Chain or Artifact Seeker's Pendant |

The final count varies by faction, according to the source. These are community-reported numbers, not values we have verified in the live game.

The same report says **Mages** unlock the **Study** ability on their first book turn-in. It can be used in the library to produce a bundle of time-limited scrolls.

## How certain is this?

| Claim | Grade |
|---|---|
| Owen Thadd and Garion Wendell accept books in the two capitals | Blizzard hotfix notes, November 13 |
| Thresholds, level requirements and reward names | Blizzard Watch community guide, November 20 |
| Mage Study ability and scroll bundle | Same community guide |
| Book tooltip text | Same community guide |

There is no Blizzard article listing the rewards. We are publishing this because the mechanic is confirmed and the guide is useful, not because every number in it is first-party.

## A practical note

The source makes a case for collecting books incidentally rather than following a route: check Mage towers, ruined houses and necromancy laboratories while you level, and save the books for a single turn-in session. That is advice, not a verified mechanic.
