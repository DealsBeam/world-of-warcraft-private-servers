Vanilla+ is Classic with custom content on top. Zones, classes, items and quests layered on the 1.12 base.

It made [Classic Plus](/blog/what-is-wow-classic-plus/) a genre before Blizzard confirmed one. After [Turtle WoW shut down in May 2026](/blog/turtle-wow-shutdown-explained/), Vanilla+ split into a family. Several are thriving.

Live status for all below is on [the tracker](/).

## The Turtle WoW successors

Turtle's code survived its shutdown. The 2024 leak meant the scene could carry on. Direct descendants:

- **[Octo WoW](/servers/octo-wow/)** — biggest. Restored 1.18.1 Jul 2026, three realms (N'Zoth PvE, C'Thun HC, Y'Shaarj PvP), phased raids 2026-27. See [modern client](/guides/octowow/).
- **[Capybara WoW](/servers/capybara-wow/)** — ex-Turtle SEA admins, two realms, ~10k online. Largest Turtle successor now.
- **[Ravencraft](/servers/ravencraft/)** — 1.18.1 continuation by former contributors, **launched Aug 22** — fresh start for Turtle loyalists. See [launch news](/news/ravencraft-launch/).
- **[Caverns of Time](/servers/caverns-of-time/)** — Turtle+ variant, opened Aug 15.

## Independent Vanilla+

Not everything traces to Turtle. Independent foundations:

- **[SandWorlds](/servers/sandworlds/)** — Brazilian Vanilla+ on 1.17.2, cross-faction, 13 custom zones, class reworks.
- **[Project Legacy](/servers/project-legacy/)** — VMaNGOS, 150+ new quests, cross-faction, dual spec, Prestige/Lorewalker.
- **[Incursion-WoW](/servers/incursion-wow/)** — instant-60 Vanilla→WotLK progressive, reworked MC/Onyxia, 500+ rebalanced items.
- **[Vanilla+](/servers/vanilla/)** — the PvP realm that named the category.

## The Ascension side

Ascension runs Vanilla+ seasons alongside classless mainline. See [classless explainer](/blog/ascension-classless-wow-explained/).

[Bronzebeard](/servers/bronzebeard/) is the Vanilla+ realm. [Conquest of Azeroth](/servers/conquest-of-azeroth/) runs custom classes.

## Picking a Vanilla+ server

**1. Turtle lineage = polish, check pop.** Octo, Capybara and Ravencraft run refined 1.17/1.18 — but fresh realms need players. Capybara ~10k is the safest large bet.

**2. Independent = different spirit.** Legacy (VMaNGOS, solo modes) and Incursion (instant-60, rebalanced endgame) are for those who want Vanilla+ without Turtle.

**3. Timing matters.** Ravencraft (Aug 22) and Caverns (Aug 15) are new — good for launch week, volatile after. Missed the window? Capybara's population beats a fresh economy you won't catch.

**4. Watch legal overhang.** Vanilla+ inherits Turtle's risk — see [crackdown explainer](/blog/blizzard-2026-private-server-crackdown/). Popular = visible.
