The private server scene loses realms every year, but 2026 has been heavier than most. Of the projects in [our tracker](/servers/), **nine shut down this year**, and they fall into two clear waves: three in May, five on one day in September, plus one independent closure later that month.

This is a reference post. It lists what closed, when, and why, drawn from our own tracker, where every dead entry carries a dated shutdown and a reason. If a date here changes, it changes in the tracker first, and this page follows.

## The September wave: the Ascension network

On **September 4 2026**, the Ascension network's whole line went offline at once under a Blizzard cease-and-desist, reached, our entries note, by mutual agreement in a case numbered 8:26-cv-01506.

- **[Ascension](/servers/ascension/)** - the classless hub itself, parent to the experiments below.
- **[Bronzebeard](/servers/bronzebeard/)** - Warcraft Reborn, the original nine classes plus Mystic Enchantments.
- **[Conquest of Azeroth](/servers/conquest-of-azeroth/)** - 21 custom lore-derived classes with three specs each, still in alpha at the time.
- **[Area 52](/servers/area-52/)** - classless free-pick with Mystic Enchantments, high-risk PvP and hardcore.
- **[Elune](/servers/elune/)** - the seasonal realm with Wildcard random abilities and Draft Mode.

Five realms, five different designs, one day. Note that **Conquest of Azeroth** is not the same project as the live **Conquest of AzerothCore** or the independent **Conquest Reborn**; the names collide, and the tracker keeps them apart.

## The May wave

On **May 15 2026**, three long-running realms closed under cease-and-desist:

- **[Turtle WoW](/servers/turtle-wow/)** - the Vanilla+ community that defined the category.
- **[Stormforge (Mistblade)](/servers/stormforge-mistblade/)** - the network's Mists of Pandaria realm.
- **[Stormforge (Wrath)](/servers/stormforge-wrath/)** - its Wrath realm.

## The independent closure

- **[Project Alterac](/servers/project-alterac/)** - a progressive WotLK realm whose Phase 3 launched August 8 2026, then closed on **September 15 2026**. No legal action is recorded here: the operator's stated causes, as our entry carries them, were the timing of the Forever beta and out-of-pocket CDN costs. It is the one 2026 shutdown on this list that "cease-and-desist" does not explain.

## Before 2026

For context, four realms in the tracker closed earlier and stay listed with their dates: **ArgusWoW** (May 15 2022), **Felmyst** (June 1 2023), **WORS, World of RuneScape** (June 1 2023) and **Everlook** (December 1 2023).

## Why we keep them listed

A closed server is not deleted from the tracker. The entry stays, marked dead, with a date and a reason, because the record is the point: it is how a reader who remembers a name finds out what happened to it, and how the count stays honest. We do not guess at a shutdown. Where a site carries a shutdown notice with no date on it, as one did this autumn, we withhold the entry rather than invent a day.

The wider enforcement story, from the May actions through the autumn economy holds, is in [our crackdown explainer](/blog/blizzard-2026-private-server-crackdown/).
