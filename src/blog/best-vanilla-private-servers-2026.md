Vanilla is the smallest column on [the tracker](/), and the one that keeps coming back. Of the 236 servers we track, 23 run straight Vanilla and another 25 are Vanilla+ projects that keep the 1.12 frame and add to it. Almost every Vanilla realm is x1, blizzlike, and deliberately slow, and the players who want that want exactly that.

This guide rounds up the Vanilla (not Vanilla+) servers we track as of October 2026, split by what you are actually looking for. For the modified side of the same era, see [the Vanilla+ hub](/blog/best-vanilla-plus-private-servers-2026/). Population bands come straight from the tracker and stay **unknown** wherever a project publishes no counter, which on this list is more often than not.

## Blizzlike x1: the purist lane

- **[Kronos V](/servers/kronos-v/)** is the oldest name still standing in the column, a TwinStar project running both the 1.12 and 1.14 clients with a localization update in July 2026. Login and server were online at our last read with 19 days uptime, an even faction split, and a donate shop that sells no gear. Tagged small.
- **[Stonetavern](/servers/stonetavern/)** runs two free, non-commercial, EU-hosted Vanilla realms on one account. Its museum realm holds unaltered 1.12.1 at the final content patch with no XP boost, no automation, and no dungeon finder; its progressive realm is at level cap 50 and puts every change to a player vote. It publishes a 51-addon shelf for the 1.14.2 client. Its one stated departure from 2006 is shared faction chat, groups, and dungeons.
- **[Ashen WoW](/servers/ashen-wow/)** runs the original 2004 to 2006 patch timeline: patch 1.8 went live in September 2026 and 1.12 is due September 2027. x1, no pay-to-win, EN/RU/ZH, launched September 2025. Player reports of developer turnover are recorded in the entry.
- **[Elderhide](/servers/elderhide/)** is a 1.12.1 PvP realm on VMaNGOS at x1 with open registration and continuing development. Tagged tiny.
- **[Origins WoW Asia](/servers/origins-wow-asia/)** is a free Vanilla realm for Asia and SEA, hosted in Singapore and progressing patch by patch from 1.2 toward 1.12.1. Its newest dated news was patch 1.7, which added Arathi Basin and Zul'Gurub as a 20-player raid.
- **[Greyhaven](/servers/greyhaven/)** launched August 14 2026 as a progressive 1.12.1 PvP realm and has held its level cap at 40 since September 4, with staged brackets, no world buffs in raids, a unified auction house, unlimited debuff slots, and free respecs.

## If you play solo

Vanilla's group content is the whole point, which is why the solo lane is a set of deliberate workarounds rather than a separate genre.

- **[SoloCraft](/servers/solocraft/)** runs 1.12.1 and 1.14 with PartyBots and BattleBots AI companions, and it is the busiest solo Vanilla community we track at about 1.7k Discord online.
- **[NycterMoon](/servers/nyctermoon/)** has run 1.12.1 PvE x1 on VMaNGOS since 2022, with AI Companions you hire from innkeepers. It rebranded in October 2026 and now calls itself **Microbot WoW** everywhere on its site, with no content, rate, or realm change found alongside the rename.
- **[Keldurn](/servers/keldurn/)** plays 1.12 in the browser over WebGPU with no install, alongside a Windows client, at original rates with faction autobalance and device guides for phones and Chromebooks.

## The browser and the impossible client

Two Vanilla projects are worth naming for their clients alone.

- **[Emberveil](/servers/emberveil/)** runs patch 1.12.1 inside a working Unreal Engine 5 client, opened August 15 2026. Its devlog records 340-plus bug reports closed, 97 client revisions, a nine hour DDoS weathered, and a client running on Android. Note its dated devlog was quiet after September 15 at our read.
- **Keldurn**, above, is the other one: the whole game in a browser tab.

## Where the people actually are

Vanilla populations are small by design, but two realms carry real numbers.

- **[Elysium Project](/servers/elysium-project/)** is a community-run 1.12.1 realm that launched June 27 2026 with 18.8k Discord members and roughly 980 online at our read. It is the largest Vanilla community on the tracker.
- **[SandWorlds](/servers/sandworlds/)** is a PvP realm with 213 Discord online at our read.
- **[Kronos V](/servers/kronos-v/)** and **[SoloCraft](/servers/solocraft/)** round out the small tier.

## Growing, dated, and worth watching

- **[Luminary](/servers/luminary/)** is a Russian 1.12 cozy server whose own news dates patch 1.7 and Zul'Gurub to October 31 2026.
- **[Anachronos](/servers/anachronos/)** is a Brazilian project that opened early access on October 8 2026 capped at level 10, with a fuller launch on October 22 and progression across seven content phases.
- **[BasedWoW](/servers/basedwow/)** runs a single 1.12.1 realm called Lightbringer on AzerothCore, with dated news through October 6 2026.
- **[Warlords of Azeroth](/servers/warlords-of-azeroth/)** is the unusual one: Vanilla-era at level 60 with **no factions at all**, everyone flagged everywhere outside sanctuaries, and a three tier territory system where the deadliest zones drop your gear into a chest your killer can loot. PvP sets are crafted rather than ground out. It states there is no cash shop.

## Hardcore

- **[WOW Hardcore](/servers/wow-hardcore/)** is a 1.12.1 permadeath realm at blizzlike x1, run by WoW-HC. We could not verify it first-party, because its site serves a Cloudflare challenge to both plain requests and a real browser. A third-party hub describes it as progressive hardcore in Phase 6 with 60 to 150 players, which we record as their claim and not our reading.

## What to be careful of

Three entries in this column are carrying an honest warning rather than a recommendation.

- **[Feenix](/servers/feenix/)** is marked unverifiable: seven candidate domains resolve to nothing, and a third-party hub lists it closed without a date. We are not calling it dead on an unconfirmed report.
- **[GryffinWow](/servers/gryffinwow/)** now points at a domain parking page reading "this domain may be for sale". We are not calling it dead, because a lapsed domain is evidence of no website, not of a shutdown.
- **[True Azeroth](/servers/true-azeroth/)** is unverifiable too: six candidate domains do not resolve, and a shutdown has been reported but never confirmed.

All three were downgraded to an unknown population band in October so that a server we cannot reach stops carrying a number we cannot measure.

## What's gone

**[Everlook](/servers/everlook/)** was abandoned in December 2023 and is the only dead Vanilla entry on the tracker. Compare that with the current wave: nine of our thirteen dead servers are cease-and-desist cases, most of them in 2026. The [2026 shutdown roundup](/blog/private-servers-shut-down-2026/) has the full list.

## Picking a Vanilla realm

1. **Decide how slow you want it.** Ashen WoW's 2004 to 2006 timeline and Stonetavern's museum realm are the slowest; Greyhaven and Origins WoW Asia walk you through the patches in stages.
2. **Decide whether you need other people.** Elysium Project is the biggest community; SoloCraft and NycterMoon are built for playing alone.
3. **Treat unknown population as unknown.** More than half the tracker publishes no counter we can read, and that includes several realms here. A small honest number beats a big unverifiable one.
4. **Check the date, not the listing.** Vanilla projects open and close fast, and a directory that shows a realm as live may be months behind. Every entry above carries the date we last read it.
