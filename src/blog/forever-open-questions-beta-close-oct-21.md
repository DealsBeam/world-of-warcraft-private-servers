The Forever beta ends today, the stress test runs this afternoon, and then the next time anyone logs in it will be for launch on **November 4**. That makes this the right moment to publish the list we have been carrying since September: the claims that never closed.

None of these are scandals. They are the ordinary gaps between what a company says in a video, what a storefront prints, and what players find in a build. We are listing them because the alternative, quietly picking the answer that reads best, is how a site stops being worth reading.

## 1. Onyxia's Lair: launch or December 9

Two first-party sources disagree. Blizzard's **September 23 official video** says, at 00:03:54, that "the 40-player raid, Onyxia's Lair, is available at launch". The **September 12 What's Next recap** says new raids unlock on **December 9**, and a designer interview describes Onyxia as the 40-player raid without giving a date.

**What would close it:** a launch-day realm or a December 9 unlock post. Until then both claims stay visible, and our roadmap piece keeps them both.

## 2. The beta close date

The roadmap graphic read **October 22**. Blizzard's own product text reads beta access from **September 17 through October 21, 2026 PST**, and the beta is being taken offline for today's stress test. We are treating **October 21** as settled because the product page is the more specific document, while noting the graphic still says 22.

## 3. The Heroic price

**$29.99** has been reported by outlets reading the storefront. Blizzard has never quoted it in a post we can read. That is not the same as false, and it is not the same as verified. It goes in the same bucket as every price we refuse to print until the seller prints it.

**What would close it:** the Battle.net shop page, at launch, in the buyer's own currency.

## 4. Timbermaw Hold or Blackmaw Hold

Blizzard's dungeon list and the beta data read **Blackmaw Hold**. In a September interview, lead designer Tim Jones referred to the gate in Azshara as "Timbermaw Hold", phrased as a question. A developer's aside is evidence, not a published list, so both names remain on our hub.

**What would close it:** the dungeon's own map or entrance text at launch.

## 5. Spearing Strike

A Warrior anti-mount talent, known only from a creator's footage: 40% weapon damage, a dismount, 15 Rage, 20 second cooldown. No official notes and no tooltip we have seen confirm it exists.

**What would close it:** a talent tree screenshot or a patch note line.

## 6. The Forsaken Paladin mount

The model question, not the class. Datamining suggested a Forsaken paladin mount among the sealed files, and sealed files are exactly what a beta cannot confirm.

**What would close it:** a Forsaken paladin on a mount at launch.

## 7. Bank size: 48 or about 96

Testers reported a **48-slot** bank. Lead designer Josh Greenfield estimated roughly **96** in an interview. Both numbers are sourced, they are not the same claim, and neither is a patch note.

**What would close it:** the bank window on a live character.

## 8. The teased Legendary

A single report says Blizzard teased a new Legendary with an unrevealed identity and an unrevealed raid link. Nothing in the official posts we read names it.

**What would close it:** the questline itself.

## 9. The 2027 roadmap fine print

The Spring and Summer 2027 items exist only as a transcription of the roadmap graphic. The beta contains launch content, not 2027 plans, so no amount of datamining settles this.

**What would close it:** a Blizzard post, or 2027.

## What did get settled

For balance, the beta answered more than it left open. The **stress test date** moved from an undated "later in the test" promise to **October 21** at 2:00 p.m. PDT and 19:00 CEST. **Name reservation** landed as **October 27 to November 3**, up to three characters, first come first served. **Launch** is confirmed at **3:00 p.m. PST on November 4**, and the first raid tier is dated **December 9** even with Onyxia's timing unresolved. The **Paladin** deep dive closed the nine-class series on October 15, and the **soundtrack** shipped on October 16.

That is a better record than most betas produce. The remaining list is short, specific and closeable by looking at a live client, which is exactly what launch is for.
