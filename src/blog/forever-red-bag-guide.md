A community guide reports that the **Red Bag**, a 16-slot bag reward from an old Season of Discovery questline, is now available to **every class** in WoW Forever. It used to be Warrior-only. The report comes from Duskforge, published November 22; we have not run the chain ourselves and there is no Blizzard note confirming the change.

The quest is worth doing because the bag arrives **halfway through** the chain, before the difficult final fight. Here is the route as reported, with the caveat that all coordinates and quest names are from that community source.

## The route to the bag

| Step | Location | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tanaris, 59.2, 91.5 | Find the **Half-Buried Mech** on the southern coast. Accept and complete **Beach Bot** for the **Guided Buoyancy Accelerant**. |
| 2 | Tanaris coast | Accept **Mechanical Romance**. Use the potion and swim toward the lighthouse, then to a small island out at sea. |
| 3 | The island | Find **Sebastian Jurgens**. Turn in and accept **Frosty Favors**. |
| 4 | Everlook, Winterspring, 61.2, 37.0 | Find **Nami** and turn in Frosty Favors. |
| 5 | Burning Steppes | Pick up **Red Bag Blues**. Climb from around 59, 29 and follow the path to a camp at 53.0, 24.8. |
| 6 | The camp | Interact with the **Red Bag** to complete the quest and claim the 16-slot bag. |

The source warns that the path up the mountain is easy to miss. Do not try to climb the sheer slope directly under the bag; find the smaller way up.

You can stop at step 6. The chain continues with **Poacher's Den** and eventually **Rift Away**, where an elite with over 200,000 health guards more rewards including the Sun Shades transmog and an Ingenious Toy. That fight is not required for the bag.

## The catch for Rogues and Druids

While equipped, the Red Bag reduces your **effective stealth level by 2**. The source says this affects only Rogues and Druids. Every other class gets a free 16-slot bag with no penalty.

For a Rogue or Druid, the bag is still an upgrade, but it is not free in the way the headline suggests. If stealth matters more than four extra slots, weigh it before equipping.
