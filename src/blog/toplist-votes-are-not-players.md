The top three World of Warcraft servers on xtremetop100 read 8,028, 8,027 and 7,990 votes. Three servers, separated by 38 votes out of 8,000, on a list that resets monthly. That clustering is not what organic preference looks like. It is what organized voting looks like.

## The paid layer, stated plainly

The toplist's own [advertise page](https://www.xtremetop100.com/advertise) sells **Gold Membership**: featured banner, gold title and badge, priority visibility, and a **bonus +100 votes monthly after the reset**. The top of the current chart is wall-to-wall gold badges. This is not an accusation of fraud; it is the documented business model. But it means the leaderboard confounds two variables (player enthusiasm and marketing spend) into a single number.

Add the mechanics: one vote per visitor per 12 hours, full reset monthly. That system does not reward the most-played server. It rewards the server with the most organized Discord willing to click twice a day in a sprint window.

## Votes vs heads, with our receipts

Our tracker checks populations, not votes, and the two series barely correlate:

- **GemstoneWoW**: 730 toplist votes against **4 players online** on its own public ladder at check. Respectable project, honest ladder, tiny realm. The votes describe a community that clicks, not one that logs in.
- **SoloCraft** (8,028) claims 1000+ online; **Warstorm** (7,990) sits near 200 by our reads. Same vote band, fivefold population gap.
- **WoW Circle** (5,599) claims 45,000+ across 12 realms: a network total presented as if it were one server's crowd.

None of this makes toplists useless. They are excellent discovery feeds: roughly two-thirds of the serious top-50 overlaps our tracker, and it is how we found GemstoneWoW; they are terrible censuses. Read them for names, never for numbers.

## How we read them

Votes answer "who campaigned best this month." Population answers "who will be online Tuesday night"; our [census](/census/) uses ladders, counters, API telemetry and Discord presence, each labeled with what it actually is. When a server publishes its own ladder, like GemstoneWoW and Centurion do, that number beats any vote total ever printed.
