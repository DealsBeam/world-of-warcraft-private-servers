World of Warcraft: Forever launches on **November 4 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST**. Between now and then there are four dates that actually require you to do something, and one of them is tomorrow. Everything below comes from Blizzard's own posts, and where the company contradicts itself we say so.

## October 20 (today): invite codes start arriving

Pre-purchase **Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes** are emailed "beginning October 20, 2026, PST". The Skyborne Heroic Pack includes one, and the Skyborne Epic Pack and Warcraft Forever Collection include three each. Gift purchases are excluded.

The codes grant launch-week access from **November 4 through November 11** to somebody who does not otherwise have a subscription, so they are worth claiming before somebody else in your group does.

## October 21: the stress test, and the beta's last day

**The stress test is on October 21**, and anyone can join. No subscription, Game Time, invitation or beta access is required.

| Region | Local start |
|---|---|
| North America | 2:00 p.m. PDT |
| Europe | 19:00 CEST |
| Korea | 06:00 KST, October 22 |
| Taiwan | 05:00 CST, October 22 |

To get in: update your graphics drivers, open the Battle.net app, select World of Warcraft, open the **Game Version** menu above the Play button, choose **World of Warcraft: Forever Stress Test** under In Development, install, then Play and pick a realm. **Installing today is the whole point of this paragraph.** Tomorrow is when everyone else will be downloading.

The beta itself runs through **October 21, 2026 PST**, and Blizzard says it will be taken offline shortly before the test and is "expected to return shortly after it ends".

**Everything in the stress test is deleted.** Blizzard is explicit: "At the end of the Stress Test, all created characters, names, and guilds will be wiped from the Stress Test servers." That is deliberate, because the wipe clears the field for name reservations.

## October 27 to November 3: name reservation

**Early Name Reservation** runs from **October 27 through November 3, 2026 PST** and is included with any upgrade purchase, including the Heroic pack. You can create and customize **up to three characters**, and names are first come, first served. Blizzard states plainly that "desired names cannot be guaranteed".

Two things follow from that. The reservation is the only way to lock a name before launch, and it is also the only reason left to buy an upgrade right now: the beta access in the Epic pack is worth nothing after tomorrow. Our [pack comparison](/blog/forever-editions-which-pack-to-buy/) is still the place to work out which edition, if any, you want.

## November 4: launch

Forever launches **globally on November 4 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST**. Access is included with a World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time, so the upgrade editions buy extras and early access to character creation, not the game itself.

The Epic Pack and the Warcraft Forever Collection also add **30 days of Game Time** to your account starting November 4. Note the timezone: November 4 is after US daylight saving ends on November 1, so 3:00 p.m. PST is 6:00 p.m. EST, not 7:00 p.m.

Invite codes are valid from **November 4 through November 11**.

## December 9: the first raids

Blizzard's roadmap puts the first new raids on **December 9 2026**: **Hyjal Summit** (20-player) and **the Barrow Deeps** (10-player, maximum level). There is one open contradiction in the official material that we have kept on the record since September: the roadmap says new raids unlock on December 9, while the official explainer and a designer interview place **Onyxia's Lair** at launch. We have not resolved it, and we will not pretend it is settled.

## The short version

1. **Today:** claim your invite codes if you have them, and install the stress test client.
2. **Tomorrow:** log in, expect a wipe, and do not plan around anything you do there.
3. **Next week:** reserve up to three names if you bought an upgrade.
4. **November 4, 3:00 p.m. PST:** launch, with a subscription or Game Time.
5. **December 9:** the first raid tier, subject to the Onyxia's Lair date conflict above.
