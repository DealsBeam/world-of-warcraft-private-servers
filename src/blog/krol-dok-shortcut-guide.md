A community guide reports a shortcut through **Krol'dok Stronghold** that skips most of the dungeon. The chain rewards the **Krol'dok Compound Key**, which opens two doors leading directly to the final boss, **Overlord Mugg**. The original hint was shared on Wowhead and relayed by Duskforge on November 21. We have not run it in game, and there is no Blizzard note about it.

## Getting the key

1. Inside the dungeon, find and kill **Tunk**. Loot the **Krol'dok Moulding** to start the quest of the same name.
2. Take the item to your faction's contact in the Riverglades:
   - **Horde:** Kreza Darkthorn in **Rog'mar**
   - **Alliance:** Alphonse Dumas in **Farholde Keep**
3. Accept the follow-up, **Key Ingredients**, which asks for **4 Thick Leather** and **12 Iron Bar**.
4. Hand them in to receive the **Krol'dok Compound Key**.

Bring the leather and bars with you before entering the dungeon, so you can finish the quest as soon as you return. The two quests reportedly award about **7,000 XP** combined.

## Using the shortcut

Back in the dungeon, the first door is directly behind the second boss, **Twilight Commander Zarmok**. Past it, follow the path toward the Krol'dok Gate, on the left of the area where you found Tunk. Use the key on the second door and follow the path to Overlord Mugg.

The source notes that the boss can drop the **Emblem of Slamming**, a trinket useful for anyone farming ogres.

## How certain is this?

Every step above comes from the Wowhead hint as reported by Duskforge. There is no first-party Blizzard confirmation, no patch note and no in-game verification by us. The faction NPC names, material counts and door locations are reported values. Treat this as a lead to try on your next run rather than a settled guide.
