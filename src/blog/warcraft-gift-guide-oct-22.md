Buying a gift for a World of Warcraft player is hard, because the hobby is enormous, the taste is specific, and the person who loves it already owns the four things they actually use. This guide is organised by what the gift is actually for, with the price we saw on 22 October 2026 next to each one, so you can buy in a tier that fits.

## Tiers, if you are buying for a birthday

**Under $25.** The raid mug at about $16, or the art book at about $14, either of which is a good gift for somebody who has everything else.

**$25 to $60.** The Chronicle box set at about $27, the mouse pad at about $22, the puzzle at about $30, the hoodie at about $40.

**$60 and up.** The 20 Years of Music record at about $69, the Wrath soundtrack at about $78, the vinyl at about $190, or the chair from the ergonomics guide.

## When the right gift is nothing

Two cases, and they are the common ones.

If they are the person who already has a headset, a good mouse, a decent chair and the books, then another object is a worse gift than the thing nobody thinks to give: **time**. Buy them game time on the game they play, which for a private server player means supporting the realm they are on, or for a retail player means the subscription. Our [shop page](/shop/) has the physical end of it, and the honest truth is that the best gift for a dedicated player is the one that makes their existing setup better, and often that is not a purchase at all.

If you do not know what they play, ask. A WoW player who gets a WoW gift they already own is a slightly worse outcome than a WoW player who gets a gift they will use, and the difference is one question.
