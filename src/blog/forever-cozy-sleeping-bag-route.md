The Cozy Sleeping Bag is back in the WoW Forever beta. Current route data points to a level-14 start, a six-quest chain across both continents, and a small experience bonus that is most useful while leveling.

This is beta documentation, not a launch guarantee. Item values, quest availability, and coordinates can change before release. The route below follows the current beta and uses Blizzard Watch's original Season of Discovery route as a cross-check.

## Quick facts

- **Start level:** 14.
- **Effect:** Current beta data reports 1 percent bonus experience per minute inside the bag, up to 3 stacks.
- **Duration:** The rested effect lasts two hours after the final stack.
- **Cooldown:** The bag has a one-hour cooldown after use.
- **Food synergy:** Well Fed adds 5 percent kill experience, taking the reported bag total to 8 percent kill XP.
- **Quest reward:** Sturdy Lunchbox, a 12-slot bag.

## Route

### 1. Start on your faction's side

- **Alliance:** Alexston Farmstead in Westfall, around **37,50**. Inspect the burned-out remains for *...and that note you found*.
- **Horde:** Southern Barrens, south of Camp Taurajo, around **46,74**. Inspect the matching burned-out remains.

Turn the first quest into the other faction's starting zone. Alliance players go to the Barrens location, and Horde players go to Westfall. Both paths converge on *Stepping Stones*.

### 2. Stonetalon Mountains

Head to the empty camp above Sun Rock Retreat at **40.6,52.4** and complete *Stepping Stones*. Then follow *Scramble* to the dirt mound just over the hill at **39.6,49.8**.

### 3. Loch Modan

At Stonewrought Dam, climb to the second stone head and inspect the spot at **49.4,12.9** for *Wet Job*. The route is easy to miss from the dam floor, so check the upper ledge before moving on.

### 4. Hillsbrad Foothills

Use the cart and tunnel along Thoradin's Wall on the Hillsbrad side. At approximately **87.3,49.6**, interact with the bag and satchel on the tunnel wall to finish *Eagle's Fist* and *This Must Be the Place*.

## What to bring

The chain also hands out a Swiftness Potion and Student Fodder along the way. Expect a little climbing and some backtracking around Stonewrought Dam, but the route stays within low-level zones and does not require a dungeon run.

The practical value is modest. A fully stacked bag adds 3 percent experience, and food raises the kill-XP portion to 8 percent. The route is still worth doing for the 12-slot reward, especially on a fresh leveler, but it should not replace quest completion or first-clear dungeon rewards.
