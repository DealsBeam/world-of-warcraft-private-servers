The [BlizzCon skeleton](/news/wotlk-anniversary-reveal/) is drafted and the [predictions post](/news/blizzcon-predictions/) is live — so let's get practical. When Wrath of the Lich King Anniversary launches (presumably late 2026 or early 2027), where should you actually play?

## The official WotLK Anniversary — what we know

The [Classic+ hub](/classic-plus/) timeline has tracked every hint since January's State of Azeroth. Based on precedent from Vanilla and TBC Anniversary editions, the official WotLK release will likely feature:

- **Fresh-start realms** — everyone begins at level 1 on equal footing
- **Phase schedule** — ICC, Ulduar, and other content gates unlock over months
- **QoL improvements** — dual spec, dungeon finder, guild banks all come standard in 3.3.5a, so the Anniversary just has to not regress
- **Blizzlike balance** — no custom content, no playerbots, no cross-faction shenanigans

## The private WotLK landscape

Warmane, Sirus, and Chromiecraft have been running WotLK for years. The [community lists](/news/community-lists-compared/) we scanned this week show a mature ecosystem:

| Server | Pop Tier | Character | Launch |
|--------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Warmane** | Large | 4 realms (Icecrown, Lordaeron, etc.), 2.5k-10k online | ~2012 |
| **Sirus** | Large | Custom content, 6-13k online, Russian-language dominant | ~2014 |
| **WoW Circle** | Large | Multi-expansion network, 13 realms, RU | 2007+ |
| **Chromiecraft** | Large | Non-profit AzerothCore, hyper-progressive | 2020 |

Plus 20+ smaller realms with distinct flavors — solo-scaled (TheraWoW, Stormgarde), Spanish (UltimoWoW, 890 Discord), Polish (Sunwell.pl), instant-255 funservers.

## The trade-off

**Choose the Anniversary if you want:**
- Official stability and Blizzard-grade infrastructure
- A fresh start on equal footing with everyone else
- The "authentic" progression experience as Blizzard intends it

**Choose private WotLK if you want:**
- No wait — log in and raid ICC today
- Custom content (Triumvirate's level-60 endgame, Sirus custom races, Chromiecraft's non-profit model)
- Regional communities (RU on Sirus/WoW Circle, Spanish on UltimoWoW, Polish on Sunwell.pl)
- Playerbot-powered solo play (Stormgarde, Warstorm, TheraWoW)

**The middle ground: Chromiecraft**
[Chromiecraft](/servers/chromiecraft/) occupies a unique spot: non-profit, open-source, hyper-progressive (patches unlock on schedule, no content gates), and all fixes upstreamed to AzerothCore. If you want a WotLK realm that feels "official" in discipline but "private" in accessibility, it's the best-in-class option — and it will survive the Anniversary because it doesn't compete on the same axis.

## What the private scene does better

Private WotLK is a decade-deep ecosystem. It offers things the Anniversary won't:

1. **Solo content** — Playerbot AI on Stormgarde and Warstorm lets you 40-man ICC alone
2. **Custom progression** — Triumvirate-WoW's level-60 WotLK endgame with Mythic+ dungeons
3. **Niche communities** — ERP (Azerotica), Romanian (Eternyum), Ukrainian (FreedomWoW), ERP-PvE (Azerotica)
4. **Instant access** — no subscription, no download queue, no character transfer drama

## What the Anniversary does better

1. **Trust** — Blizzard won't shut down its own product
2. **Balance** — thousands of hours of tuning vs emulated systems with edge-case bugs
3. **Cross-platform** — Modern client, no custom patches, no private launcher headaches
4. **The crowd** — the launch week spike will be real; every realm will feel packed

## Verdict

The private WotLK scene isn't going anywhere after the Anniversary — it hasn't after Vanilla or TBC Anniversary launches. But the *center of gravity* will shift. Players who are on Warmane or Sirus for the population will likely sample the official realms; players who are there for custom content or regional communities will stay.

If you're picking one right now: **Warmane's Icecrown for population**, **Chromiecraft for integrity**, **Stormgarde for solo play**, or wait for the official Anniversary for the fresh-start dopamine hit.
