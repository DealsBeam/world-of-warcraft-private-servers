Most private-server groups run one expansion, one realm, one community. Whitemane is different: it's a network running multiple expansions at once, with some realms live and several more in development. As of August 2026 that makes it one of the most ambitious multi-realm operations in the scene.

## The live realms

- **[Whitemane — Gilneas](/servers/whitemane-gilneas/)** — a Cataclysm expansion realm, live now.
- **[Whitemane — Maelstrom](/servers/whitemane-maelstrom/)** — Cataclysm 4.3.4 with x3/x2 rates and a PvP focus.

Both Cataclysm realms are playable today, which makes Whitemane the network to watch if that expansion is your era.

## In development

The rest of the roster is building toward launches:

- **[Whitemane — Frostmourne](/servers/whitemane-frostmourne/)** — a WotLK+ realm, the flagship project, targeting **September 9, 2026**.
- **[Whitemane — Stormstout](/servers/whitemane-stormstout/)** — Mists of Pandaria.
- **[Whitemane — Sunwell](/servers/whitemane-sunwell/)** — The Burning Crusade.

So the network spans four eras: TBC, WotLK+, Cataclysm, and MoP — with two realms live and three more on the way.

## Why a multi-realm network matters

Running several expansions under one brand is a real advantage for players:

1. **One account ecosystem.** Hop between eras without managing a separate account per server.
2. **Shared infrastructure.** Cross-era work on cores, launchers, and web tooling gets amortized across the network.
3. **Population stability.** If one era dies down, the network as a whole doesn't — players can move eras instead of leaving.

## What to watch

- **Frostmourne's September 9 launch** is the network's biggest test. A successful WotLK+ launch would make Whitemane the rare group with strong Cataclysm *and* WotLK populations simultaneously.
- **Launch gaps matter.** Gilneas and Maelstrom are running today; Stormstout and Sunwell have no public dates. The development-to-playable pipeline is uneven.
- **Legal exposure scales with size.** Multi-realm networks are bigger targets — and as [the 2026 crackdown](/blog/blizzard-2026-private-server-crackdown/) showed, visibility invites attention.
