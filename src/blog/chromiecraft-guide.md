Details from our data: Unique non-profit WotLK on AzerothCore, hyper-progressive phased releases, all fixes upstreamed.

Discord shows 3.3k+ online. That signals a healthy, active community, not just launcher numbers.

Hyper-progressive means content unlocks in phases. Vanilla era, then TBC era, then WotLK tiers in order.

Each phase gets tested before next unlock. Bugs are fixed live, then pushed upstream for every AzerothCore server.

No shop, no donations for profit. Costs are covered by volunteers. No pay-to-win pressure on tuning.

This also limits marketing spend. Growth is organic, not bought. Community vets newcomers quickly.

Script quality is high. Upstream flow means fixes are public and audited, not hidden behind a paywall.

## Who it is for

Blizzlike purists who want clean WotLK. No custom races, no funserver stats.

Developers and purists who value open source. You can read every fix in the repo.

PvE players who like phased progress. Tier by tier feels like a real timeline.

Players burned by closures. Non-profit and transparent funding lowers landlord risk.

New players who want steady groups without shop noise. Leveling feels fair throughout.

## Pros and cons

Pros: open source, no shop, strong scripting, phased testing, large pop, 3.3k Discord, stable ethic.

Cons: no ICC on day one, pure Blizzlike only, slower growth than commercial, no custom content.
