Cataclysm (patch 4.3.4) is a smaller scene than Wrath, but 2026 has been kind to it: two large realms from one network, several long-running Spanish communities, and a few projects bolting Mythic+ onto a 2011-era expansion.

Everything below is in [our tracker](/servers/). Check the individual cards, because Cataclysm has more unverifiable entries than most expansions and we flag them.

## The big two

- **[Whitemane Gilneas](/servers/whitemane-gilneas/)** - Cataclysm 4.3.4 on the Whitemane network (free to play, no pay-to-win, a launcher for Windows, Mac, Linux and Steam Deck, quality-of-life features, custom legendaries, solo-queue PvP and rated battleground matchmaking). One of the largest Cataclysm populations we track.
- **[Whitemane Maelstrom](/servers/whitemane-maelstrom/)** - the network's second 4.3.4 realm on the same stack. Between the two, Whitemane is the default Cataclysm pick if you want a big population and a modern launcher.

## Veteran and regional communities

- **[WarSages](/servers/warsages/)** - Spanish blizzlike 4.3.4 in the Dragon Soul era, with a Magma Front zone added in July 2025, a large Discord and roughly 125 online at our October 3 2026 check.
- **[WOW-COLOMBIA](/servers/wow-colombia/)** - running since 2007, Spanish, Latin American, and running Cataclysm 4.3.4 and Vanilla 1.12.1 side by side. About as veteran as the scene gets.
- **[Valkyria](/servers/valkyria/)** - Spanish and unusual in running patch 4.0.6 rather than 4.3.4, with a progressive Europa realm, character migrations and hardcore-fallen rankings.

## Mythic+ experiments

- **[Proudmoore](/servers/proudmoore/)** - TwinStar's Cataclysm realm, billed as the first Mythic+ Cataclysm server. It is reachable through TwinStar's shared multi-realm page rather than a dedicated site.
- **[Starfall WoW](/servers/starfall-wow/)** - a progressive 4.3.4 realm in Phase 2 (patch 4.1, with Zul'Aman and Zul'Gurub heroics) and a custom 15-affix Mythic+ with keys to +15 and gear at the current heroic raid item level.

## Smaller and flagged

- **[IceCold](/servers/icecold/)** - a Wrath-era Cataclysm 4.3.4 realm with a conventional forum and support stack, confirmed operating on October 14 2026.
- **[Warriorland](/servers/warriorland/)** - a French blizzlike 4.3.4 realm, home-hosted on dynamic DNS, advertising sophisticated PvP and PvE scripting. Tiny by its own counter.
- **[Apollo II](/servers/apollo-ii/)** - associated with TwinStar, but our address points at TwinStar's shared page, which on September 29 2026 listed four realms and did not include Apollo II. Treated as unverified.
- **[Atlantiss](/servers/atlantiss/)** - historically the Cataclysm flagship of the Tauri network, relaunched in February 2024. We could not locate a live site for it, so it is flagged unverifiable and its claims are historical.

## In development

- **[Hour of Twilight](/servers/hour-of-twilight/)** - a custom project whose address now resolves to its Discord, which reported thousands of members at our last read. Not playable.

## How to choose

If population and stability matter most, Whitemane Gilneas or Maelstrom. If you want a Spanish community, WarSages or WOW-COLOMBIA. If Mythic+ is the draw, Starfall WoW and Proudmoore are the two actively pushing it. Cataclysm carries more unverifiable entries than most expansions, so lean on the tracker flags and on [how we review a server](/blog/how-we-review-a-private-server/).
