On July 29 a dataminer surfaced two textures that looked like separate Alliance and Horde versions of a Lordaeron tabard. They appeared in the same pipeline that carries Project Camelot's encrypted 1.60.1 builds — the branch that reached 1.60.1.69328 on Aug 14, 1.60.1.69407 on Aug 20 and 69638 on Sep 1 on wowdev2 — and the pairing was enough to tie them to Camelot in the community. Lordaeron as a Classic Plus setting, with revised faction rewards, was the inference that followed.

It is worth slowing down on that inference, because tabards are the weakest setting signal a datamine can produce and the one most likely to be wrong for interesting reasons.

## What was observed

Two textures, one read as an Alliance Lordaeron tabard, one as Horde. The framing around them included familiar leak language: "separate Alliance and Horde versions," "revised faction rewards or a fresh Classic-era setting," and a cross-reference to the Dwarf and Skyborne reward thread from the same mid-August window — the Dwarf Bear mount, the Skyborne Cat in four colors, the Shen'dorei sets, the toy, the housing pieces. The textures were not part of the encrypted Camelot builds themselves; they were promotional cosmetics in a pipeline that also carries those builds.

That pipeline carries a lot. By Sep 1 the 1.60 line has two parallel branches (1.60.0 on wowdev5 and 1.60.1 on wowdev2), a China vendor CDN variant, and encrypted promotional assets with no confirmed home. Some of those assets are Classic-shaped, some are Retail-shaped, some could be either. The Lordaeron textures landed in the second bucket.

## What tabards signal and what they don't

Tabards in WoW are cosmetics with an unusually low bar for reuse. A recolored tabard can be:

- A faction reward for a new Classic Plus setting (the inference)
- A Trading Post recolor of an existing tabard
- A retail promotion tied to a novel, anniversary or in-game event
- A housing or transmog-set piece with no faction meaning at all
- A test texture that never ships

Lordaeron makes the first of those feel right because Lordaeron is where the community has been looking. Mount Hyjal, Gilneas, Uldum and the pirate islands near Booty Bay dominate every "most requested zone" list — ours included in the [300k-vote wishlist gap](/blog/classic-plus-wishlist-gap/) — and a Lordaeron tabard reads as a zone hint for the Ruins of Quel'Thalas / Tirisfal / Lordaeron corridor that sits next to those zones on every fan map. But the tabard is a chest slot, not a zone file. No map, no quest, no NPC, no WMO, no ADT accompanied it. Datamined zones are WMO/ADT/WMO groups; tabards are a square of cloth.

Compare the signal strength to stronger Classic Plus leaks in the same window. Patch 1.60.0 appearing on a vendor environment in late July is a branch signal — it tells you a build left internal and hit distribution. Build 1.60.1.69407 appearing as a China CDN variant on Aug 21 is a distribution signal — it tells you the vendor pipeline reached a regional CDN. 1.60.1.69638 on Sep 1 is a build signal — it tells you wowdev2 ticked. All three are about the build, not the content. Tabards are about content, but about the cheapest content to fake, recolor or repurpose.

## Why Lordaeron still matters even if the tabards are noise

Even if the two textures turn out to be a Trading Post bundle and nothing else, the setting question they raise is the right one for Classic Plus and the one the tracker has been circling since May: **is Classic Plus a new place or a new time?**

Most 2026 private Classic+ answers that question by being a new place. Turtle WoW was a new place. The 18 Vanilla+ rows on the tracker that are alive or in dev are all, in one way or another, a new place: a restored Frostmane Hollow at level 1, a Tortoise WoW continuation, a VMaNGOS Classic+ with 150 new quests woven into the old world. The other answer — a new time — is harder: same map, earlier or later, with the story moved so that Lordaeron is alive again, or Gilneas is open, or Quel'Thalas is not a ruin. Lordaeron tabards as faction rewards would be a "new time" signal: the map stays, the clock moves, the tabard you wear says which Lordaeron you belong to.

That is why the community reached for the tabards even though the evidence is thin. The Dwarf Shaman thread (see our [Dwarf Shamans piece](/blog/dwarf-shamans-would-it-break-classic/)) was a race/class leak that doubled as a faction-identity question. The Lordaeron thread is a cosmetic leak that doubles as a setting question. Neither is a fact about Camelot until Blizzard ships it as Classic.

## Where we land

Our timeline treats July 29 Lordaeron tabards as a datamine, not a build. It belongs in the [Classic+ tracker](/classic-plus/) alongside the 69638 tip, but with the label it deserves: **encrypted promotional textures of unknown home**. If they reappear as a Lordaeron faction reward on a Classic Plus reveal on Sept 12, we will re-tag them as a setting signal retroactively. Until then they are a Rorschach test for what kind of Classic you want — same map, new time — and a reminder that the weakest cosmetic can carry the strongest setting story if the community wants it to.

Watch Sept 12 for map, not for cloth.
