> *Scheduled publish — Sep 4 19:00 PT verification pending. All 5 Ascension realms (Area 52, Elune, Bronzebeard, Conquest) are marked dead Sep 4 per mutual agreement with Blizzard.*

Ascension wasn't a blizzlike realm. It was the place you built a character from any ability in the game, and for nearly ten years it held the largest classless community in private WoW. On September 4, 2026 at 7:00 PM Pacific, all of its emulated realms — [Area 52](/servers/area-52/) (Classless free-pick), [Elune](/servers/elune/) (Seasonal Wildcard/Draft), [Bronzebeard](/servers/bronzebeard/) (Warcraft Reborn) and [Conquest of Azeroth](/servers/conquest-of-azeroth/) — are scheduled to go offline under a mutual agreement with Blizzard. The story of how that happened, and what rose from the wreckage, mirrors Turtle WoW four months earlier.

## How it ended

Blizzard filed *Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. v. Derek S. Powell et al.* on June 12 in the Central District of California (8:26-cv-01506), alleging copyright infringement, DMCA violations, false designation of origin and a RICO enterprise and conspiracy built around Ascension's custom launcher and infrastructure. The complaint pointed to large-scale commercial exploitation — millions in cash-shop currency — and to hosting via a Russian company under U.S. sanctions, a setup many players assumed made the project untouchable.

The docket moved fast. Summons requests went out late June, waivers were executed July 9 for Powell, Bryan Thomas Mannion and two entities, answers were due September 8, and defense counsel appeared July 2 and July 10, winning an early skirmish when Blizzard withdrew a request for early discovery. No injunction was entered. The seven realms stayed online and the shop stayed open.

Then, like Turtle, the case settled. On August 29 project lead Dutch posted on Discord:

> "It has been almost 10 years since Ascension emulated WoW realms began, and the final chapter of this particular journey is coming to an end. All of Ascension's emulated WoW realms will shut down on September 4, 2026 at 7:00 PM Pacific as part of Ascension's and Blizzard's mutual agreement. Support will remain active until the very last day. Remaining content on the timeline will release and receive support before shutdown, then all WoW-related material will be removed from the website and social channels as development shifts to something new, unrelated to WoW or any Blizzard game."

Support through the final day, final timeline drops still released, then a full wipe of WoW material from the site, socials and launcher — repurposed for a new non-WoW project. Financial terms, code disposition and claim releases were not disclosed, as with Turtle's confidential settlement after the April 13 ruling and May 15 consent judgment. The difference: Turtle closed after a court ruling and permanent injunction; Ascension closed after 11 weeks by agreement, without a ruling, eight days before BlizzCon.

## The hook that made it a target

Ascension held population because official Era didn't offer what it did:

- **Area 52: Classless** — free-pick any ability/talent from any class, Mystic Enchantments, High Risk PvP with gear loss, Hardcore.
- **Elune: Seasonal** — time-limited Wildcard (random abilities) and Draft Mode (pick 1 of 3 per level), characters transfer to Classless when the season ends.
- **Bronzebeard: Warcraft Reborn** — the OG 9 classes, rebuilt with Mystic Enchantments and RPG overhaul.
- **Conquest of Azeroth (Alpha)** — 21 brand-new lore-derived classes ×3 specs.

All share new dungeons, RPG creature overhaul and expanded open world. That's the feature list official Classic lacks — and why Blizzard treated Ascension as a business, not a nostalgia server.

## Where the community goes (no leak safety net)

Unlike Turtle WoW's September 2024 leak of its 1.17 core (which spawned [Octo WoW](/servers/octo-wow/), [Capybara WoW](/servers/capybara-wow/), [Ravencraft](/servers/ravencraft/) and [Everwood WoW](/servers/everwood-wow/)), Ascension has no code dump. The 5 realms simply go dark. The launcher, website and socials will be repurposed, not archived.

For classless players, the closest live alternatives tracked here are:

- **GrimfallWoW** — Classless at 60, Wildcard random rolls, scaled Vanilla through Cata as evergreen endgame.
- **Everwood WoW** — Classless Vanilla+ (formerly Caverns of Time), no launch date.
- **GryffinWow** — fresh Vanilla, tiny but alive, and the Turtle 1.18.x heirs ([Capybara](/servers/capybara-wow/), [Octo WoW](/servers/octo-wow/), [Ravencraft](/servers/ravencraft/)) for Vanilla+ horizontal content.

## What Ascension's death means

The 2026 pattern is not subtle: Stormforge May 14 → Turtle WoW May 15 (consent judgment) → Ascension Sep 4 (mutual agreement). The playbook is identical — RICO over the launcher/infrastructure, subpoenas to Discord, hosts and payment processors, cost absorption. The Russian-hosting shield failed.

It lands eight days before BlizzCon 2026 Sep 12-13. Holly Longdale already teased Classic's future on the January State of Azeroth stage before a staged blackout cut her off mid-sentence — the blackout was deliberate. Black Temple went live Aug 27, and the encrypted Project Camelot branches (Dev 2 `1.60.1.69609`, Dev 5 `1.60.0.69606`) received three updates in 25 hours Aug 28-29. The sweep plus the Camelot trail makes a Classic+ reveal feel inevitable, but as with Nostalrius April 2016 → Classic Nov 2017 (~19 months), resemblance is not evidence.

If you played Ascension, verify any "character transfer" claims — none were announced. Treat repacked clients as malware risk, and don't reuse Blizzard passwords anywhere.
