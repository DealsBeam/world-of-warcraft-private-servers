Old Man Warcraft is the small server that decided small is the point.

Custom WotLK 3.3.5a, 1.5x XP and rep, 78 humans online at peak on our Sep 1 check, and a design brief that reads like a life-stage: "for people who still love Wrath but want less friction around scheduling, grouping and progression.".

It is on our tracker as [Old Man Warcraft](/servers/old-man-warcraft/) — `playable`, `WotLK`, `small`, `oldmanwarcraft.com`.

## What it ships

Per-character progression to **tier 18**: Vanilla → TBC → Wrath, one character at a time, not a server-wide phase. Your tier, your world — NPCs and objects reflect where you are.

**Playerbots and scaling:** dungeons and raids scale with party size. Bring friends plus bots when you don't have a full guild. Warstorm proved bots can clear ICC25 HC; Old Man makes that the default.

**LLM chat for everyone:** large language model dialogue is on for all players — bot banter, directions, strategy, roleplay replies.

**Mythic+-style dungeons:** affixes, timers, keystones — repeatable scaling challenge on supported instances.

**Guild spaces:** villages and housing for shared identity, plus AoE looting, transmog, cross-faction chat, and anticheat on top of core.

Rates: **1.5x** XP and rep, weekend XP boost stacks on top, plus Twitch XP bonus when linked. Not blizzlike 1x, not funserver instant — faster than 1x without turning leveling into a blur.

## Who it fits

Solo players who still want dungeons, raids and progression systems. Couples and trios who want to play together without pugging every slot.

Returning players who know the content but want flexible scheduling. Completionists who want multiple tiers to stay worthwhile.

The realm is explicit: if you want RPG progression without "LF1M tank 30 min" every night, this direction is intentional.

## What it doesn't try to be

Not a Turtle successor (no 1.18.1), not a blizzlike museum (not 1x, not no-custom), not a large-pop city (78 online, `small`, not `large`).

The pitch is steady progression with bots where humans are scarce, plus long-term goals that don't require a perfect roster.

VIP perks are via vote points — no subscription. That matters for the private scene's trust after 2026's RICO sweep: big + monetized + custom launcher is most exposed; small + vote-only is least.

Live: `oldmanwarcraft.com`, Discord `discord.gg/thecgn`, 1.5x base. If you want Wrath with more flexibility and more life off-peak, Old Man is your lane.
