Blizzard announced October 7 that **Standard Definition Skyborne player characters, including SD druid forms, arrive in early 2027**, while the HD druid forms get revised fur, saturation and detail work now.

A launch race shipping with a known visual gap and a dated fix is worth examining, because it tells you how the roadmap is actually prioritized.

## What was said

The team aligned on "the right range of fidelity and what feels like it belongs in the WoW: Forever world", found some explorations did not meet their cohesion bar, and is revisiting the HD Skyborne druid forms now: less softness, more detailed fur, adjusted saturation.

The SD toggle was built for pre-existing races, whose models players are attached to. Feedback said HD Skyborne next to SD world content is jarring; Blizzard agrees. So SD Skyborne is in development for early 2027, with a work-in-progress image and a promise of more information closer to the date.

## Why a dated slip is good information

Most launch content problems surface as silence. This one surfaced as **a dated commitment with work-in-progress art**, which is the most checkable form a delay can take. Early 2027 is a quarter, not a date, but it is on the record and we will hold it there.

It also tells you the launch priority order. Playable systems first (classes deep-dived, Legacy documented, PvP progression published this week), visual cohesion second, and the toggle audience, players who prefer SD models, explicitly served after launch rather than at it.

## The risk it names

"Ambitions that grew and evolved over time" is Blizzard's own description of the development. A scope that grew during development and is still being aligned a month before launch is the standard shape of a project deciding what to cut, and visual cohesion is what got cut first.

That is a healthy cut as cuts go: nobody's character stops working because a fur texture ships in February. But it establishes that **the launch client is not the finished visual product**, and readers evaluating day-one reviews should know which version of the models the reviewer saw.

## What to watch

Whether the HD revisions land before November 4 or with it, and whether early 2027 holds once the launch crunch reallocates the art team. Both are dated claims now, and both get re-checked.
