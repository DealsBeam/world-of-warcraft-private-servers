## What this column is

Deal coverage on most sites repeats the seller's numbers: "47 percent off", with no way to know whether the list price was ever real. This column is the opposite. Every deal gets the same five checks:

1. Read the price from the product's own Amazon page, not a headline.
2. Record the displayed list price and the current price, with the date.
3. Anchor the list price against what the product is known to cost, from its launch price or established street pricing.
4. Verdict: genuine discount, or inflated-list fake sale.
5. Record the caveats, including currency and delivery-destination effects.
