WoW Classic Plus is the community name for a rumored new version of Classic World of Warcraft that would keep the original game's world and pacing while adding new content. No product named Classic Plus exists. Project Camelot is the name dataminers tied to an encrypted 1.60 development branch — build 69638 on wowdev2 as of Sep 1 — that many players believe is Classic Plus. Blizzard has confirmed neither the name nor the link. This is the tracker edition: same five questions every broad guide answers, but with our 108-server, 37-entry, 69638-tip data instead of a paragraph of “Blizzard hasn’t said.”

## 1. Overview — what is Classic Plus?

Classic Plus is not a patch, not a season, not a difficulty toggle. It is the idea of a permanent Vanilla that grows. Same Azeroth, same 60, same slower travel, same guild that took two weeks to get the second tank, plus new zones where the map is empty, new dungeons and raids at 60, new class quests, and quality-of-life that does not make the world smaller. The phrase covers every version of that idea from “add Hyjal and stop” to “rebuild the class table and add housing.” The only thing the 300,000 survey responses at wowclassic dot plus agree on is that the list exists: 57 questions, three buckets — The World, The Player, The Systems.

Our [Classic Plus wishlist gap](/blog/classic-plus-wishlist-gap/) put the survey next to the tracker: Mount Hyjal is the #1 zone request and 0 for 107 live private Classic+ rows ship it; Dwarf Shaman is the #1 race/class request and 0 ship it; Heroic dungeons is the #1 system and 0 ship it at Classic scope. That gap is the clearest definition of what Classic Plus would be if it shipped: the content the private scene wants to ship and hasn't.

## 2. What is Project Camelot?

Project Camelot is an unannounced World of Warcraft project name found through datamining, tied to an encrypted branch using version 1.60.

- **Branch:** Encrypted WoW Patch 1.60, with two parallel lines — 1.60.0 on wowdev5 and 1.60.1 on wowdev2. Both ticked in the Sep 1 window; wowdev2 is the tip.
- **Latest tip:** 69638 on wowdev2, Sep 1, via our automated build watcher on Blizzard's own CDN (BlizzTrack wowdev2). Previous tip 69609 Aug 29 — three builds in ~25 hours across two envs.
- **Other signals:** Heroic and Epic licensing labels, brief vendor-environment appearances (Western and China — 69407 China CDN variant Aug 21), Armory adding Classic games Aug 27 (not tied by Blizzard, but useful infra), encrypted Dwarf and Skyborne promotional assets under investigation, 29 encrypted builds logged since Oct 2025.
- **Not a public name:** Internal codenames change. Even if Camelot ships, the store name will not be Camelot.

Our [Classic+ tracker](/classic-plus/) is the live view: count-up to BlizzCon 2026 Sep 12, status snapshot (37 entries: news/leak/rumor), timeline from Oct 2025 first 1.60 activity to Sep 1 69638, and the three builds in 25 hours that make the Sep 12 show-realm theory plausible. None of it proves Camelot is Classic Plus. All of it is why the community thinks it might be.

## 3. Why do players want Classic Plus?

2019 proved there is still a huge audience for the original pacing. The official line since — Vanilla 2019, TBC 2021, Wrath 2022, Cata, MoP, Anniversary realms — moved the player through the same expansions again. Each step was popular, each step moved further from Vanilla. Anniversary tried to fix it with a fresh Vanilla → TBC corridor and a handful of QoL (Dual Spec, Group Finder) but the corridor is still a corridor.

Season of Discovery tried the other answer: keep Vanilla, add abilities, gate with level caps (25, 40, 50, 60), hide Scarlet Enclave from dataminers. It proved runes work, caps gate better than phases, hidden content works once, and seasonal has a half-life. Our [SoD legacy piece](/blog/sod-legacy-classic-plus/) is the longer read. Classic Plus is the permanent version of that experiment: the lab was seasonal, the wish is permanent.

Private servers lived the same arc. Our tracker has 108 servers: WotLK 36, Vanilla 16, Vanilla+ 18 (8 playable, 7 dev, 3 dead). Vanilla+ in 2026 is 10 unknown pop — the column whose identity is “new content in the old world” has not shipped a new zone. That is the gap Classic Plus would fill on day one.

## 4. What do players want from Classic Plus?

The same 8-bullet list every broad guide prints, but here with the gap:

- New dungeons and raids — survey #1 system is Heroic dungeons, 0 at Classic scope on tracker.
- New or expanded levelling zones — Mount Hyjal #1 zone, 0 live.
- More class quests and open-world content — 2-3 partial (Legacy custom quests, Centurion talent rework).
- Improvements to weak classes/specs — 4-5 live (Legacy, Centurion, Turtle-derived custom talents).
- Additional race/class combos — Dwarf Shaman #1, 0 Classic-era live (see our [Dwarf Shamans piece](/blog/dwarf-shamans-would-it-break-classic/)).
- Improved professions — 2-3 partial.
- Dual Spec + QoL — cross-faction already 8 live, guild banks 1 live (Legacy Aug 26).
- Refreshed PvP — solo 3v3, 1v1, city sieges on private; official Midnight has the scheduled counterpart.

The challenge every guide names is the same one your wishlist gap names: add without removing the slower, social style that makes Classic different from retail. Private Classic+ already decided one part of that — cross-faction yes, pet battles/housing no — and the survey agrees.

The community census behind this is the Classic+ Project at wowclassic dot plus: 300,000 responses across 57 questions, World (zones, dungeons, raids), Player (races, classes, level cap), Systems (Heroic, buffs, finder, transmog). Results are not a Blizzard roadmap, but they are the scale of the ask.

## 5. Release date — when could it be?

No confirmed date. The next window is BlizzCon 2026, September 12-13 at Anaheim Convention Center (badge pickup Sep 11), free live stream of Opening Ceremony and main-stage panels, drops Sep 12 09:30 PDT → Sep 13 19:00 PDT, AWC/MDI and Classic Cup on floor. Blizzard teased Classic players have more to look forward to and the community reads that as the Classic Plus slot.

Some players point to November — SoM, SoD and Anniversary all launched in November — as a pattern. There is no evidence Classic Plus follows it, and the 1.60 branch name should not be read as a level cap. Project Camelot could be months or years away, or something different from the Classic+ the community imagines. Watch the level on stage and whether the gate is a cap or a phase. That tells you permanent or seasonal.
