## TL;DR

- Classic Plus means Vanilla stays at 60 while gaining zones, dungeons, quests.
- It is not progressive re-release, not seasonal reset, not private Vanilla+.
- BlizzCon September 12-13 may clarify scope, with survey and tracker framing asks before Anaheim stage reveal soon.

If you quit after Classic 2019 queues, Molten Core, and the Naxx wall, this is for you. Classic Plus answers what comes after Vanilla besides Vanilla again.

**Classic Plus is Vanilla that keeps growing instead of moving on.**

Same Azeroth from 2004, same 1 to 60 path. Same Deadmines at 18 and Stranglethorn at 30, plus new places and 60 raids.

Not a cap lift to 70. Not a reset season. A permanent Vanilla that gets wider, not taller.

## What it is not

It is not **progressive re-release**. That path runs Vanilla to TBC to Wrath in order. Anniversary does this now with Phase 3 live.

[TBC Anniversary Phase 3](/news/black-temple-aug-27/) brought Black Temple and Hyjal again. Classic Plus would stay in Kalimdor and Eastern Kingdoms instead.

It is not **Season of Discovery**. SoD was the lab with healing Mages, tanking Warlocks, and runes. Level caps gated play at 25, 40, 50, and 60.

Our [SoD legacy piece](/blog/sod-legacy-classic-plus/) covers that run. SoD ended by design, while Plus would persist as a world.

It is not **private Vanilla+**. The tracker lists 18 Vanilla+ rows: 8 playable, 7 in dev, 3 dead. Biggest is [Capybara WoW](/servers/capybara-wow/) near 10k online.

None ships Hyjal or a full new 60 raid. None ships Dwarf Shaman or Heroic Classic dungeons at scope. See [Dwarf Shamans](/blog/dwarf-shamans-would-it-break-classic/) for that gap.

## Three questions it answers

Every thread and the 300k survey ask the same three things. World, Player, and Systems frame the whole debate.

**World means where to go.** New zones, questlines, dungeons, and 60 raids. Hyjal leads zones with 0 of 107 live shipping it.

Uldum, Gilneas, Booty Bay isles, Quel'Thalas, and Silithus round out asks. Our [wishlist gap](/blog/classic-plus-wishlist-gap/) maps 7 of 7 unshipped.

**Player means who to be.** Combos, tuning, cap, and professions matter. Dwarf Shaman leads combos, still 0 live at Classic era.

The cap splits narrowly toward staying 60. Every tracked Vanilla row stays 60, so the stage level number matters most.

**Systems means how to play.** Heroic dungeons leads with 0 at Classic scope. Cross-faction sits at 8 live, already standard.

Guild banks sit at 1 live. Transmog is mixed, while pet battles and housing rank last with broad agreement.

## The feel

The feel is slower pace, real travel, mystery, and group discovery. Challenge stays, while UI, mail, and auction get welcome QoL.

Era rows with Finder and Dual Spec show the small version. Classic Plus would be the large version of that deal.

[Classic Minus](/blog/classic-minus-what-would-you-remove/) sharpens the lens. Remove flight and it stays Classic, but remove levels and it breaks.

Plus must avoid touching Mounts, bags, or curve. If it hits those Minus items, it misses the ask.

## Until September 12

Until the ceremony ends, Plus is a term, Camelot is a branch, and BlizzCon is near. Facts are survey votes, tracker counts, and stage.

Read Plus as map stays, clock moves, level stays, world widens. Compare any reveal to the gap when the stage goes dark.
