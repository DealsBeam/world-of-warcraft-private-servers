If you quit in 2019 after Classic launched, logged for the queues, cleared Molten Core, hit the Naxx wall, and haven't logged since, Classic Plus is the one-sentence answer to “what's next for Vanilla that isn't just Vanilla again.”

**Classic Plus is Vanilla that keeps growing instead of moving on.**

Same Azeroth you walked in 2004. Same 1-60. Same Deadmines at 18 and Stranglethorn at 30. Plus new places where the map is empty, new dungeons and raids at 60, new class quests, and quality-of-life that does not make the world smaller. Not a new expansion that lifts the cap to 70. Not a season that resets you. A permanent Vanilla that gets wider, not taller.

## What it is not

It is not **progressive re-release**. That is Vanilla → TBC → Wrath → Cata → MoP, one expansion at a time, same patch cadence, same content order. Anniversary does that now — TBC Phase 3 went live Aug 27 with Black Temple and Hyjal again, same two raids for the same two reasons. Classic Plus would not move you to Outland. It would keep you in Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms and fill the holes.

It is not **Season of Discovery**. SoD was the lab: Mages healed, Warlocks tanked, runes gave classes a second way to play, level caps of 25, 40, 50, 60 gated the world, Scarlet Enclave hid from dataminers. Our [SoD legacy piece](/blog/sod-legacy-classic-plus/) is the longer read. SoD was seasonal — it ended. Classic Plus as the survey imagines it is permanent — a world that waits, not a season that punishes you for stepping away.

It is not **private-server Vanilla+**. Private Vanilla+ in 2026 is 18 rows on our tracker — 8 playable, 7 in dev, 3 dead, 10 unknown population. The biggest, [Capybara WoW](/servers/capybara-wow/), is a Turtle 1.18.1 continuation with about 10k online. The newest small, [Project Legacy](/servers/project-legacy/), has 1,000 accounts and a custom Guardian of Hyjal world boss. None ships Mount Hyjal. None ships a new 60 raid with a full loot table. The private column wants the same wider Vanilla, but it has been conservative where the ask is permissive — no Dwarf Shaman at trainer, no Heroic toggle for Classic dungeons. The Dwarf Shaman thread (see our [Dwarf Shamans piece](/blog/dwarf-shamans-would-it-break-classic/)) and the Heroic question are the one-notch bends private teams chose not to make. An official team would not face that constraint.

## The three questions Classic Plus answers

Every “what would Classic Plus be?” thread, every 57-question survey at wowclassic dot plus (300,000 responses, three buckets — The World, The Player, The Systems), every leak thread from IronSeer's alternate timeline to the Oct 13 rumor, all ask the same three questions with different words.

**The World — where to go.** New old-world zones and questlines. Unfinished Vanilla locations. New dungeons and level-60 raids. The survey's #1 zone is Mount Hyjal, 0 for 107 live. Uldum, Gilneas, pirate islands near Booty Bay, Quel'Thalas and expanded Silithus round the top. The gap we mapped in the [300k-vote wishlist gap](/blog/classic-plus-wishlist-gap/) is 7 of 7 unshipped on live. If Classic Plus opens with one new zone, the map answer is given.

**The Player — who to be.** New races or race/class combos, class tuning, level cap, professions. Dwarf Shaman is the #1 race/class ask and 0 Classic-era live ships it. The level-cap ask — stay 60 forever vs raise — splits narrowly to 60. Every tracked Vanilla/Vanilla+ live stays 60. The level number on the BlizzCon stage will tell you permanent or not before any zone does.

**The Systems — how to play.** Heroic dungeons is the #1 system and 0 at Classic scope on the tracker. Cross-faction is 8 live — already standard. Guild banks is 1 live (Legacy Aug 26). Achievements split, transmog 4-5 live, pet battles/housing bottom and also 0 — at least everyone agrees. The system that is most wanted is the one most private Classic projects chose not to ship. The system that is most divisive is the one private already decided.

## The feel — why Vanilla at all

Every guide that tries to define Classic Plus uses the same paragraph for the feel and means it: slower pace and meaningful travel, depth over breadth, mystery and community discovery, challenge without modern conveniences, and welcome QoL for UI, mail and auction. Private Era with Group Finder and Dual Spec (your Era rows already have it) is the small version of that paragraph. Classic Plus is the large version.

The feel is also why Classic Minus (see our [Classic Minus piece](/blog/classic-minus-what-would-you-remove/)) is a good lens. Classic without flight is still Classic. Without an auction house is still Classic. Without world buffs is still Classic. Without mounts is almost Classic, thinner. Without levels is a different game wearing the same map. If Plus touches the level curve, the bag, the mount — the Minus items — it missed the ask even if the trailer is good.

## How to think about it until Sept 12

Until the opening ceremony wraps, Classic Plus is a community term, Project Camelot is a 1.60 branch at 69638 on Sep 1, and BlizzCon is 11 days away. The only Classic Plus facts are the survey (300k votes want Hyjal and a new class table), the tracker (107 servers, Hyjal 0, Dwarf Shaman 0, Heroic 0), and the stage. After that, one of those three numbers will move.

For now, read Classic Plus as: the map stays, the clock moves, the level stays, the world gets wider. Everything else — which zone, which class, which system — is a paragraph you can fill yourself and compare to the wishlist gap when the stage goes dark.

If you want the wider Vanilla tonight, start on any of the 16 tracked Vanilla servers. If you want the wider Vanilla tomorrow, watch the badge, not the trailer.
