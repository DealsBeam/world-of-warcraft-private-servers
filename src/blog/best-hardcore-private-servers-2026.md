Hardcore stopped being a niche mode and became a checkbox. A dozen realms on [the tracker](/) now advertise a permadeath option, and a smaller number build their entire identity on it. This guide covers both: the realms where death is enforced, the far larger group where hardcore is a mode you switch on, and the rulesets that get close to it without the permadeath at all. Population bands come from the tracker and stay **unknown** wherever a project publishes no counter.

A quick definition, because "hardcore" is used loosely in this scene:

- **Enforced hardcore:** the realm is permadeath. One death ends the character, for everyone, by design.
- **Optional hardcore:** the realm runs normally, and you can choose a challenge at character creation. Most hardcore play in 2026 happens here.
- **Hardcore-adjacent:** no permadeath, but a ruleset where losing has real teeth, usually through lootable gear or full PvP flagging.

## Enforced permadeath

- **[WOW Hardcore](/servers/wow-hardcore/)** is the one that simply is hardcore: a Vanilla 1.12.1 permadeath realm at blizzlike x1, run by WoW-HC. We **could not verify it first-party**, because its site serves a Cloudflare challenge to both plain requests and a real browser. A third-party hub describes it as progressive hardcore in Phase 6 with 60 to 150 players, which we record as their claim and not our reading. Check it yourself before committing a character.
- **[Balance WoW](/servers/balance-wow/)** is a hardcore WotLK 3.3.5a realm with a level 30 cap that advances along a ChromieCraft-style timeline, with smarter mobs, transmog, cross-realm play, and 1.5x experience. Its own counter read 3 online at our last check, so the population band is tiny and that is worth knowing before you invest.
- **[Hijos del Tiempo](/servers/hijos-del-tiempo/)** is a Spanish multi-expansion project offering a Northrend adventure with **permanent character death** alongside a second progression path, for players who want hardcore as one option among several rather than the whole server.
- **[Everwood WoW](/servers/everwood-wow/)** is the strangest entry: a Hard-RP Classic project in development with **no levelling, raids, quests, or dungeons at all**, a pre-Classic timeline, hex turn-based combat, a fief simulator with a player economy, and permadeath. Announced in September 2026, EN/RU. Not playable yet.

## Optional hardcore: where most of it actually happens

These are the realms where you can switch hardcore on and play a normal server otherwise. That combination, not a dedicated permadeath realm, is what most hardcore players are actually using in 2026.

- **[Hellgarve](/servers/hellgarve/)** is a Legion 7.3.5 solo realm with working artifact questlines, x1 to x3 rates with double-experience weekends, custom questlines, and an optional hardcore mode. Tagged tiny.
- **[UwowSA](/servers/uwowsa/)** is a Spanish and Portuguese Legion 7.3.5 x5 realm that launched in June 2026 with 3,100-plus players, custom bosses, allied races, and a hardcore mode. The largest realm on this list.
- **[LegionPlus](/servers/legionplus/)** runs Legion 7.x with fated raids, custom bosses, allied races, timewalking, and hardcore, at 173 Discord online.
- **[Valkyria](/servers/valkyria/)** is a Spanish Cataclysm realm running patch **4.0.6** rather than the usual 4.3.4, with a progressive Europa realm and **hardcore-fallen rankings**, which is a neat trick: it turns hardcore into a public ladder without making the whole server permadeath.
- **[TuWoW](/servers/tuwow/)** is in development as a hardcore Classic+ concept: a level 60 Azeroth journey rebuilt with Wrath talents, abilities, and class balance, self-described as Classic+ by design. No launch date.

## Hardcore-adjacent rulesets worth naming

If what you want is risk rather than permadeath, two projects deliver it.

- **[Warlords of Azeroth](/servers/warlords-of-azeroth/)** has no factions and no safe world outside sanctuaries. Its three-tier territory system means the deadliest zones **drop your gear into a chest your killer can loot**. There is no permadeath, but there is real loss.
- **[Project Ebonhold](/servers/project-ebonhold/)** is a WotLK roguelite: level-1 runs, pick one of three Echoes per level, an account-wide skill tree fuelled by Soul Ashes, and 35,000-plus extra world creatures. It is the permadeath feeling rebuilt as progression rather than punishment.
- **[Torment](/servers/torment/)** goes further and rebuilds WotLK as a Diablo III style action RPG, with Diablo III stats, damage, mitigation, and loot systems on the standard Wrath client. In development.

## Dead, and worth remembering

Hardcore was part of the Ascension pitch. **[Area 52](/servers/area-52/)** listed High Risk PvP and hardcore among its features before the whole network was shut down by a Blizzard C&D on September 4 2026. Any hardcore project that sells on risk and competition now runs with that precedent in the background; our [2026 shutdown roundup](/blog/private-servers-shut-down-2026/) has the list.

## Picking a hardcore realm

1. **Decide enforced or optional first.** A dedicated permadeath realm with 3 players is a different product from a large Legion server where hardcore is a checkbox. Only one of those is a real community.
2. **Check the counter, not the feature list.** Most hardcore realms here are tiny or unknown. A hardcore mode on an empty realm means dying alone.
3. **Treat the anti-cheat and revives honestly.** Any permadeath server that visibly revives characters on request, or sells a way around death, has redefined hardcore. We record what a project publishes and say when we cannot verify it.
4. **Back up nothing, that is the point.** If you want a safety net, play a hardcore-adjacent ruleset like Warlords of Azeroth or Project Ebonhold instead.
