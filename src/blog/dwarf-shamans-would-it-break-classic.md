In mid-August a string of encrypted promotional assets tied to Project Camelot was pulled out of Blizzard's build pipeline: a Dwarf Bear mount, a Skyborne Cat mount in four colors, two Shen'dorei-themed transmog sets, a 3D Adventurer cape, a Shen'dorei Windwell toy, two tabards and five Skyborne housing pieces. The thread that caught the most airtime was the Dwarf assets, because Wildhammer Dwarves are one of the few lore-reasonable hooks for a new Alliance Shaman, and Dwarf Shaman is one of the few race/class combos the Classic community actually wants.

Stripped to what was observed versus what was inferred, the steps look like this:

**Observed:** encrypted assets with Dwarf and Skyborne motifs inside a pipeline that also carries Project Camelot's encrypted 1.60.1 builds.

**Inferred:** those assets are a Classic Plus Dwarf Shaman and a new Skyborne-like race, shipping as the Marquee feature of whatever Camelot becomes.

The first half is real. The second half is aCommunity-driven leap that depends on two unstated assumptions: that every encrypted promotional asset in a pre-release pipeline ends up as an in-game system, and that the system it ends up as is the one the dataminer guessed. Neither assumption is safe.

## Why Dwarf Shamans feel plausible

Dwarf Shaman passes three Classic ask filters at once:

- **Lore-coherent.** Wildhammer Dwarves have a Shamanic tradition that does not require a retcon of the starting zones. You do not need a new continent to justify it, just a trainer and a quest chain.
- **Low blast radius.** One new race/class combo touches the class table without rewriting the faction map. It does not add a neutral hub or a new starting zone, it just fills a hole in an existing one.
- **Popular without being divisive.** Every survey of Classic players that ranks race/class requests puts Dwarf Shaman near the top of the Horde/Alliance mirror list, usually right next to Tauren Paladin and Undead Shaman. It is the kind of addition that feels like a fix, not a pitch.

Skyborne, however, is where the inference gets thin. A Skyborne Cat mount in four colors and a handful of transmog sets could be a separate Retail promotion, a Trading Post bundle, a cross-game cosmetic, or a housing pack. The Classic one-ness of the mount — "the Classic one lower-poly" — is a read of the asset pipeline, not of the design doc. Housing pieces and capes are even weaker as Classic signals; housing is not even announced for Classic.

## The faction-identity problem the leak is actually about

Even if Dwarf Shamans never ship, the conversation the leak triggered is worth having, because Classic Plus will have to answer the same question whether it does or not: **what is an Alliance race and a Horde race in the game you are about to ship?**

Classic's faction map is stricter than Retail's ever was after Cataclysm. In Classic, a Dwarf without a Shaman and an Orc without a Paladin is not a bug; it is the premise. The two factions are not just a PvP bit; they are a class table, a set of racials, a set of totem versus blessing asymmetries that made Alterac Valley and early Arena feel different depending on which side you queued on.

Add Dwarf Shaman without touching the Horde and you have taken one step toward Retail's per-race class table, where the map is cosmetic and the real split is what covenant you picked. Do it once and the next question is: why not Tauren Paladin, why not Troll Druid, why not Human Shaman for symmetry? Do all of them and you have rebuilt the class table in a way that makes the next faction question — neutral Dalaran, neutral auction house, cross-faction guilds — feel inevitable rather than optional.

That is why Dwarf Shaman is not a one-line addition. It is a design-direction marker. A team that ships Dwarf Shaman and stops has said: we will bend the original table one notch to honor Wildhammer lore and then hold. A team that ships Dwarf Shaman plus three more has said: the table is a suggestion now, not a rule. The community can live with either — polls show they do — but only if the choice is made openly, not inferred from a datamined bear mount.

## Where we land

Our tracker treats Classic Plus as two tracks at once: the observed Camelot builds (69609 tip on Aug 29, China CDN, vendor env) and the community's wishlist of what Classic should become. Dwarf Shaman lives entirely in the second track. No build string, no branch number and no encrypted promotional asset proves a class, a race, or a mount is Classic-bound until Blizzard ships it as Classic.

Write Dwarf Shaman as a Classic change. Argue for it if you want it — the Wildhammer case is strong — and argue against it if you think the faction rule matters more. But do not write it as a Camelot fact. The only Camelot facts in August 2026 are branch 1.60.0 and 1.60.1, build 69609 on Aug 29, and a lot of encrypted assets with no confirmed home.

When Camelot's name becomes a product, the Dwarf Shaman thread will be one of the first things we re-read. Until then it is a Rorschach test for what kind of Classic you want.
