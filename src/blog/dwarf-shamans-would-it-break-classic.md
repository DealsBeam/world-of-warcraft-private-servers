## TL;DR

- Mid-August assets showed Dwarf and Skyborne motifs inside Camelot-linked pipeline builds in August.
- Dwarf Shaman fits Wildhammer lore cleanly, while Skyborne race link stays unproven today.
- One combo bends faction table slightly alone, while full unlocks rewrite Classic identity rules.

In mid-August encrypted assets tied to Camelot surfaced in build pipeline reads. A Dwarf Bear mount led the list.

A Skyborne Cat in four colors joined it. Two Shen'dorei transmog sets, a cape, a toy, tabards, and housing followed.

The Dwarf thread drew most airtime. Wildhammer lore offers a clean hook for Alliance Shaman play.

Dwarf Shaman also tops community combo asks. That overlap made the leak spread fast.

## Observed versus inferred

**Observed:** Dwarf and Skyborne motifs in a pipeline carrying encrypted 1.60.1 builds. That part is documented asset flow.

**Inferred:** those assets equal a Classic Plus Dwarf Shaman and Skyborne race. That part is a community leap.

The leap needs two assumptions. First, pipeline assets always ship as systems in the same game.

Second, the guessed system is the shipped one. Neither assumption is safe before reveal.

Promo assets often split across Retail, Trading Post, or housing. A mount read is not a design doc.

## Why it feels plausible

Dwarf Shaman passes three Classic ask filters at once. Lore, scope, and popularity all align.

**Lore-coherent.** Wildhammer tradition needs no retcon or new continent. A trainer plus quest chain suffices.

**Low blast radius.** One combo touches the class table only. No new hub, zone, or faction map rewrite is required.

**Popular but calm.** Surveys rank Dwarf Shaman near Tauren Paladin and Undead Shaman. It reads as fix, not pitch.

Skyborne is thinner as signal. Mount colors and capes fit many homes, while housing is not a Classic system.

## The faction problem

Even if it never ships, the debate matters. Classic Plus must define Alliance and Horde races clearly.

Classic factions are stricter than Retail after Cataclysm. Dwarf without Shaman and Orc without Paladin is premise, not bug.

Totems versus blessings shaped Alterac Valley and early Arena. Side choice changed how queues felt each night.

Add Dwarf Shaman alone and Retail drift begins. Next asks follow: Tauren Paladin, Troll Druid, Human Shaman symmetry.

Ship one and hold, and the table bends once. Ship four and the table becomes suggestion, not rule.

Polls accept either path if stated openly. A datamined bear mount is not an open statement.

## Where we land

Our tracker holds two tracks at once. One is Camelot builds like 69609 on August 29.

The other is wishlist demand for what Classic should become. Dwarf Shaman lives in the second track today.

No build string or asset proves a class until Blizzard ships it. Branch 1.60.0 and 1.60.1 remain the only branch facts.

Argue for Wildhammer lore if you want it. Argue faction purity if you prize it, but skip Camelot certainty.

When Camelot gains a product name, revisit this thread first. Until then it tests what Classic you want.
