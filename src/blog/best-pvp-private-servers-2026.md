PvP used to be a label on the character screen. In 2026 it is a spectrum, because private server operators have spent a decade splitting the idea apart. This guide sorts the tracked realms by **what kind of PvP they actually offer**, from the battleground queues of the largest servers down to full-loot rulesets where your killer takes your gear. Population bands are recorded as of our last check, and stay **unknown** where a project publishes no counter.

## The four kinds of PvP server

1. **Volume PvP.** Big populations, fast battleground queues, world PvP that happens because there are enough people online to make it happen.
2. **Ladder PvP.** Arenas and rated battlegrounds with seasons, matchmaking and rewards. This is the closest to retail's structure.
3. **Bracket PvP.** Realms built entirely around a level cap, usually 19, where every ability and piece of gear is scaled to the bracket.
4. **High-risk PvP.** Full loot, open flagging, no factions, or boss bounties. Small but intense.

## Volume PvP: where the queues actually pop

- **[Whitemane Gilneas](/servers/whitemane-gilneas/)** and **[Whitemane Maelstrom](/servers/whitemane-maelstrom/)** are Cataclysm 4.3.4 realms on a network that rates **large**, with a live rated battleground matchmaking system. Cataclysm is a strong PvP era, and this is the biggest place to play it.
- **[Warmane](/servers/warmane/)** is the incumbent Wrath giant, and it treats PvP as infrastructure: an entry reworked the **Wintergrasp queue to stop multiboxers holding multiple of the 120 available spots**. That is the sign of an operator who cares about the mode.
- **[Sirus](/servers/sirus/)** is the other Wrath giant, with custom races and content since 2010 and a self-reported counter of **18,468** on October 3. Its archived news covers rated battlegrounds and arena seasons.
- **[WoW Circle](/servers/wow-circle/)** runs 13 realms, including a WotLK x1 board, and its front page read **22,558 total online** on October 3. Scale like that means battlegrounds pop on its own schedule rather than yours.
- **[uWoW](/servers/uwow/)** runs Legion and Pandaria realms with large populations, which puts modern-era PvP on the table for players who want it.

## Ladder PvP: seasons, arenas, rated battlegrounds

- **[Freedom WoW](/servers/freedom-wow/)** is a Ukrainian Wrath realm at **arena season 8** with **120-player Wintergrasp** and original 3.3 balance. Its widget read **385 online** on our October 14 recheck.
- **[Rising Gods Back2Basics](/servers/rising-gods-back2basics/)** runs the German TBC realm for the rising-gods network, with **Arena Season 4** and Tier 6 endgame confirmed live on October 3.
- **[Project Legacy](/servers/project-legacy/)** is a Vanilla+ project on VMaNGOS that added a **Gadgetzan arena** in its August patch, alongside cross-faction grouping and dual spec. It reported 1,000-plus accounts in open beta.
- **[WoW Patagonia](/servers/wow-patagonia/)** runs cross-faction grouping for raids and arenas on a Latin American Wrath realm, with a front page reading **975 online** on our October 4 check.

## Bracket PvP: level 19 and proud of it

- **[19PvP](/servers/19pvp/)** is a Wrath level-19 twink realm that runs cross-faction Warsong Gulch plus arena, replaces libram arcanums with gems, lets you buy suffixes from an NPC, and **backfills quiet games with bots**. It is open source, non-commercial, and built on AzerothCore.
- **[Titans League](/servers/titans-league/)** scales **all Wrath content to level 19** rather than bracketing one mode, with a live bugtracker and a Return to Glory development push that makes an in-game scoreboard the central PvP menu.

## High-risk PvP: full loot, no safety net

- **[Azekai Reincarnated](/servers/azekai-reincarnated/)** is the extreme end: a strict text-RP Wrath realm with **full-loot PvP where gear drops**. Founded July 15 2026.
- **[Wrathstorm](/servers/wrathstorm/)** is a Classless Wrath realm with **high-risk PvP, boss bounties, 8v8 rated battlegrounds, Mythic+ raids and ranked zones**, plus an onsite talent builder and changelog.
- **[Warlords of Azeroth](/servers/warlords-of-azeroth/)** removes factions entirely: everyone groups and trades together, and everyone is **flagged for PvP everywhere outside sanctuaries**. Its tiered territory system drops your gear into a chest your killer can loot.

## Vanilla open world

- **[Greyhaven](/servers/greyhaven/)** is a progressive Vanilla 1.12.1 PvP realm launched August 14 2026, capped at 40 since September 4, with staged brackets, **no world buffs in raids**, free respecs and no pay-to-win, at an **80 to 100 EU peak**.
- **[SandWorlds](/servers/sandworlds/)** is a Vanilla PvP realm with **213 Discord online**, small but active.
- **[Elderhide](/servers/elderhide/)** is a Vanilla 1.12.1 PvP realm on VMaNGOS at x1, open registration.

## Two honest warnings

1. **Many PvP realms on the tracker are tiny.** A full-loot server with a handful of players means you loot nobody and get looted by nobody. Check the band before you roll on a high-risk realm specifically, because the whole design depends on other people being online.
2. **We record what a project publishes.** Where a PvP feature is listed only on the server's own site, that is the operator's claim. Where we could not read a counter, the band says unknown, and that includes projects whose PvP pages we could not verify.
