Launch dates are the least stable fact in the private server scene, and the next three weeks have more of them than any stretch this year. This page keeps the dates we can stand behind in one place and separates them from the ones that are only announced.

It is split in two on purpose. **Verified** means we have read the date at its source or reported it from our own check. **Announced** means the date comes from a server announcement or a listing site and we have not independently confirmed it. If a date moves, it moves here with a note, not quietly.

## Verified

**Private server launches**

- **October 15:** [Sulvus Reforged](/servers/sulvus-reforged/) (WotLK 3.3.5a PvP) opens at 16:00 UTC at wowsulvus.es. See [the launch note](/news/sulvus-reforged-opens-oct-15/).
- **October 16:** [Lordaeron.eu Rimeheart](/news/lordaeron-eu-opens-october-16-oct-12/), a custom WotLK progressive realm, at 17:00 UTC. Both its realms returned online in its own status feed on October 17, the day after the gate. See [the launch-day addendum](/news/october-16-launch-day-uwow-mop-nozdor-dune/).
- **October 16:** uWoW's high-rate Mists of Pandaria realm, `MoP x10`.
- **October 22:** [Anachronos](/news/tempest-anachronos-launches/), a blizzlike Vanilla progressive realm.
- **October 23:** [Project Epoch Reborn](/news/project-epoch-reborn-launches-october-23/) relaunches a Classic+ realm at 16:00 GMT.
- **December 15, 2027:** [Shadowburn](/servers/shadowburn/), a non-commercial custom WotLK 3.3.5a project, states this date in its own launch countdown. It is the furthest-out launch in the field and is still in development.

**WoW: Forever milestones (Blizzard's own dates)**

- **October 20:** Invite-a-Friend launch codes begin going out by email.
- **October 21:** the beta's last full day.
- **October 27 to November 3:** early name reservation, three characters, first come first served.
- **November 4:** global launch at 3:00 p.m. PT (23:00 UTC).
- **December 9:** first Forever raids. Blizzard has named Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit; a same-day Onyxia's Lair is reported from the roadmap slide but is not confirmed in Blizzard's text.

The full Forever timeline and its sourcing sit in our [roadmap post](/news/forever-roadmap-2026-2027/), and the October private server cluster is rounded up in [the October dates post](/news/october-dates-calendar-oct-8/).

## Announced, not yet verified

- **October 17:** WoW Circle opens a fresh Legion x5 realm, with Battle for Azeroth and Shadowlands planned to follow.
- **November 28:** Frontier, a custom Vanilla+ project.

These come from server announcements and listing sites. They are useful for planning, but do not book a day off around them until they firm up.

## Why some things are not here

Several projects in testing have **no launch date at all**, so they cannot go on a calendar: public test realms and in-development projects are excluded until they publish a date. That is deliberate. A project in alpha or beta is not a launch, and listing it as one would be the exact kind of soft claim this site avoids.

If you want the servers rather than the dates, the [full tracker](/servers/) is the place to start, and [new and recently opened realms](/servers/) are filterable there.
