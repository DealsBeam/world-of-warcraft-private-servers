Launch dates are the least stable fact in the private server scene. This page keeps the dates we can stand behind in one place and separates them from the ones that are only announced. It began in October 2026 as a forward calendar for Forever's launch; after November 4 we keep it as a past and future record, so an expired date stays visible instead of disappearing.

It is split in two on purpose. **Verified** means we have read the date at its source or reported it from our own check. **Announced** means the date comes from a server announcement or a listing site and we have not independently confirmed it. If a date moves, it moves here with a note, not quietly.

**WoW: Forever milestones (Blizzard's own dates)**

- **October 15:** Blizzard published the final class deep dive in the series, **Paladin**, closing out the nine Classic-era classes. See [our read of it](/blog/forever-paladin-deep-dive-read/).
- **October 20:** Invite-a-Friend launch codes begin going out by email.
- **October 21:** the **free server stress test**, open to anyone with no subscription, Game Time, invitation or beta access. Blizzard announced it for **October 19** in an October 15 post, then moved it to **October 21** in an October 16 update. Windows are North America 2:00 p.m. PDT and Europe 19:00 CEST, with Korea 06:00 KST and Taiwan 05:00 CST on October 22. The beta is taken offline shortly before it starts and is expected back shortly after it ends, the window may shift, and every stress test character, name and guild is wiped afterwards. See [the stress test note](/news/forever-stress-test-october-21/).
- **October 27 to November 3:** early name reservation, three characters, first come first served. The stress test wipe clears the field for it.
- **November 4:** global launch at 3:00 p.m. PST (23:00 UTC). The live client was 1.60.1.70790 on launch day and reached 1.60.1.70862 on November 7.
- **November 8:** Blizzard's November 6 hotfix pass, the November 8 dungeon kill experience increase, and the first group and solo level 60s. The Known Issues thread is dated November 5. See [the first weekend brief](/news/forever-first-weekend-nov-8/) and [the dungeon XP brief](/news/forever-dungeon-xp-50-percent-nov-8/).
- **November 10:** Blizzard published its class tuning pass, covering Hunter pets, Warlock pets and Affliction, and Enhancement Shaman, alongside gold rewards on level 48 and higher quests. **Corrected November 13:** the Shaman changes were delayed and went live on November 12, and the Auction House deposit cut in the same notes was pulled and moved to the November 17 maintenance. The live client reached 1.60.1.70888 on November 11 and 1.60.1.70938 on November 13. See [the tuning brief](/news/forever-tuning-nov-10/).
- **December 9:** first Forever raids: the 10-player Barrow Deeps, the 20-player Hyjal Summit and the 40-player Onyxia's Lair. In Wowhead's November 8 interview coverage, Blizzard said all three open simultaneously in every region. Earlier roadmap text named only two, and the tracker had carried Onyxia as unconfirmed.

The full Forever timeline and its sourcing sit in our [roadmap post](/news/forever-roadmap-2026-2027/), and the October private server cluster is rounded up in [the October dates post](/news/october-dates-calendar-oct-8/).

## Why some things are not here

Several projects in testing have **no launch date at all**, so they cannot go on a calendar: public test realms and in-development projects are excluded until they publish a date. That is deliberate. A project in alpha or beta is not a launch, and listing it as one would be the exact kind of soft claim this site avoids.
