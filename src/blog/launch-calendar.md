Launch dates are the least stable fact in the private server scene. This page keeps the dates we can stand behind in one place and separates them from the ones that are only announced. It began in October 2026 as a forward calendar for Forever's launch; after November 4 we keep it as a past and future record, so an expired date stays visible instead of disappearing.

It is split in two on purpose. **Verified** means we have read the date at its source or reported it from our own check. **Announced** means the date comes from a server announcement or a listing site and we have not independently confirmed it. If a date moves, it moves here with a note, not quietly.

Battle for Azeroth x5 is planned for around December 2026 and Shadowlands x5 for around February 2027. See [the opening note](/news/wow-circle-legion-x5-opens-october-17/). **Gate result:** the project's own status page, read at 18:58 UTC, listed the realm online with 1,978 players and a reported uptime of 2 hours 50 minutes, no queue, 10 ms latency, and 2,055 online across the 7.3.5 line.
- **October 16:** [GrimfallWoW](/servers/grimfallwow/) opened Conquest of Azeroth Phase 1, a second product line beside its Classless realm, with a level 20 cap and 21 new custom classes. Read at source on October 17. See [the news post](/news/grimfall-conquest-of-azeroth-phase-1/).

- **October 23:** [Stonetavern](/servers/stonetavern/) takes its progressive Project Barrens realm through its next gate, the level 60 cap, on Friday October 23 at 18:00 Vienna time (16:00 UTC). Confirmed at source on October 17: the site listed 155 changes on record with the latest dated October 13 and five open proposals.
- **November 6:** [Project Reforged](/servers/project-reforged/) carried a Classic+ launch countdown for **18:00 CET (17:00 UTC)**, reading "Starts 06.11.2026 18:00 CET". See [the Ruby Sanctum note](/news/warmane-ruby-sanctum-november-7/).

The mobile closed test realm retires the same day. Announced on the project's own news page on November 18. See [the report](/news/emberveil-november-20-android-shop-phase-two/).

Characters move automatically, the client runs 12.0.7 with 12.0.2 content, Season 1 follows **December 7**, and The War Within stops being available. Read at Firestorm's own site on November 17. **WoW: Forever milestones (Blizzard's own dates)**

- **October 15:** Blizzard published the final class deep dive in the series, **Paladin**, closing out the nine Classic-era classes. See [our read of it](/blog/forever-paladin-deep-dive-read/).
- **October 20:** Invite-a-Friend launch codes begin going out by email.
- **October 21:** the **free server stress test**, open to anyone with no subscription, Game Time, invitation or beta access. Blizzard announced it for **October 19** in an October 15 post, then moved it to **October 21** in an October 16 update. Windows are North America 2:00 p.m. PDT and Europe 19:00 CEST, with Korea 06:00 KST and Taiwan 05:00 CST on October 22. The beta is taken offline shortly before it starts and is expected back shortly after it ends, the window may shift, and every stress test character, name and guild is wiped afterwards. See [the stress test note](/news/forever-stress-test-october-21/).
- **October 27 to November 3:** early name reservation, three characters, first come first served. The stress test wipe clears the field for it.
- **November 4:** global launch at 3:00 p.m. PST (23:00 UTC). The live client was 1.60.1.70790 on launch day and reached 1.60.1.70862 on November 7.
- **November 8:** Blizzard's November 6 hotfix pass, the November 8 dungeon kill experience increase, and the first group and solo level 60s. The Known Issues thread is dated November 5. See [the first weekend brief](/news/forever-first-weekend-nov-8/) and [the dungeon XP brief](/news/forever-dungeon-xp-50-percent-nov-8/).
- **November 10:** Blizzard published its class tuning pass, covering Hunter pets, Warlock pets and Affliction, and Enhancement Shaman, alongside gold rewards on level 48 and higher quests. **Corrected November 13:** the Shaman changes were delayed and went live on November 12, and the Auction House deposit cut in the same notes was pulled and moved to the November 17 maintenance. The live client reached 1.60.1.70888 on November 11 and 1.60.1.70938 on November 13. See [the tuning brief](/news/forever-tuning-nov-10/).
- **November 13:** Blizzard's November 13 hotfix pass fixed creature-stun diminishing returns in both directions, stopped Wild Growth from being cast while moving and raised its healing coefficient to **6.6 percent**, made Judgement of Command count as melee again, and added a zero-XP rule for trivial dungeon enemies. Blizzard also published the short story **"Cost of Honor"** that day, and dated its refund notice for training lost on November 9 to November 13 in Pacific time. See [the hotfix brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-13/), [the short story brief](/news/forever-cost-of-honor-nov-13/) and [the refund brief](/news/forever-training-refunds-nov-14/).
- **November 14 (November 15 UTC):** Blizzard narrowed the dungeon XP nullification so it applies only when a high-level player is more than **12 levels** above the enemy, and said runs already underway would keep the old rule. See [the 12-level note](/news/forever-dungeon-xp-12-level-gate-nov-15/).
- **November 16 (November 17 UTC):** Blizzard's November 16 hotfix pass fixed Windfury Totem's armor bypass and missing internal cooldown, stopped Cleave and Heroic Strike from consuming Flurry stacks and from skipping Unbridled Wrath and Reckless Swings, and bracketed a Vampiric Embrace change for the November 17 maintenance. See [the November 16 brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-16/).
- **November 17:** the live client moved to **1.60.1.70959**, the first new build since November 13. The Auction House changes Blizzard pulled from the November 11 notes were due with the November 17 weekly maintenance; at our read on November 18 at 00:55 UTC they had not reappeared in the running hotfix article. See [the November 16 brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-16/).
- **November 17 (November 18 UTC):** Blizzard's November 17 hotfix pass corrected Upper Blackrock Spire's low enemy health and damage, changed Soulforge Armor from Strength to Spell Power for Paladin tanks, repaired Reagent Economy and the Cozy Sleeping Bag, and fixed a long list of profession, quest and UI bugs. The Auction House and Item Restoration were still absent from the notes. See [the November 17 brief](/news/forever-hotfixes-nov-17/).
- **December 9:** first Forever raids: the 10-player Barrow Deeps, the 20-player Hyjal Summit and the 40-player Onyxia's Lair. In Wowhead's November 8 interview coverage, Blizzard said all three open simultaneously in every region. Earlier roadmap text named only two, and the tracker had carried Onyxia as unconfirmed.

The full Forever timeline and its sourcing sit in our [roadmap post](/news/forever-roadmap-2026-2027/), and the October private server cluster is rounded up in [the October dates post](/news/october-dates-calendar-oct-8/).

## Why some things are not here

Several projects in testing have **no launch date at all**, so they cannot go on a calendar: public test realms and in-development projects are excluded until they publish a date. That is deliberate. A project in alpha or beta is not a launch, and listing it as one would be the exact kind of soft claim this site avoids.
