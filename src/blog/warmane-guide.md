Warmane is the oldest large WotLK network still live. It traces back to Molten WoW. One detail sums it up: WotLK, playable, large popTier.

This guide covers what Warmane offers, who it fits, and trade-offs. See live status on [Warmane](/servers/warmane/) and all WotLK on [/servers/?tag=WotLK](/servers/?tag=WotLK).

Warmane runs multiple realms on 3.3.5a. Icecrown is high-rate with instant catch-up. Lordaeron is 1x, no shop, tuned hard.

Both share solid scripting and uptime. The network has survived merges, DDoS, and legal pressure for over a decade.

Population is the draw. Peak counts dwarf most rivals. You find groups at 3am, not just prime time.

Cash shop exists on Icecrown. Lordaeron stays Blizzlike with no pay-to-win. Choice of realm equals choice of economy.

Script quality is good but not perfect. Older raids work well. New fixes land slower than upstream-heavy cores.

Community is vast and mixed. Veteran guilds, fresh alts, PvP queues, and GDKP all coexist. Guild recruitment never stalls.

## Who it is for

Players who want a safe, populated home first. Features second. Warmane suits this mindset best.

New players who need help fast. Large chat means answers, boosts, and dungeon runs appear quickly.

Returners who want stable economy. Large pop keeps auction house stocked and raid pugs alive weekly.

Purists who pick Lordaeron. One time XP and no shop keeps leveling and gear meaningful.

PvPers who want queues that pop. Large pool means short waits for battlegrounds and arena.

## Pros and cons

Pros: huge population, long track record, two rulesets, stable uptime, active market, fast leveling option.

Cons: shop on Icecrown, queue times at peak, less open-source transparency, tuning can feel pay-adjacent.

Compare to small realms: Warmane trades intimacy for certainty. You log in and find a group.

Compare to non-profit: Warmane keeps fixes private. [Chromiecraft](/servers/chromiecraft/) upstreams all fixes. Different ethic.

## Getting started

Pick Icecrown for fast entry. Pick Lordaeron for Blizzlike grind. Both use 3.3.5a client.

Create account on site, set realmlist, log in. Check [/servers/warmane/](/servers/warmane/) for current popTier and status.

Join a leveling guild early. Large servers reward social links. Guild perks and summons save time.

Read realm rules before buying. Know what the shop sells on your realm and what stays earned.
