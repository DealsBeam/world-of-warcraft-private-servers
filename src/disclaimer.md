## Player risk disclosure

Playing on private servers carries real risks you should understand before choosing one:

- **Terms of Service:** Playing on private servers generally violates Blizzard's Terms of Use. Accounts used for private-server play may be subject to suspension or permanent closure.
- **Security:** Third-party game clients and launchers come from sources outside Blizzard's control. Malware distributed through fake clients is a known problem in this scene. Scan what you download and never reuse passwords from other accounts.
- **Loss of progress:** As our coverage shows, private servers shut down — sometimes overnight, sometimes by court order. Anything you build on a private server can vanish without notice.
- **Legal status:** Operating a private server raises copyright questions; enforcement against operators has increased sharply since 2025. Individual players have not been targeted, but the environment shifts.

Decisions about whether to play, where to play, and what to download are yours alone. This site describes the landscape; it cannot make the risks disappear.
