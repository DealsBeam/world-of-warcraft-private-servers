# Tracker writing guide (adapted from competitor teardown)

## Titles carry numbers

Comparisons and analyses put one real quantified gap in the title
(build number, day count, price, split). No number, no publish —
unless the gap is confusion itself (disambiguation posts).

## Openings

No throat-clearing. First 2 sentences state a fact the reader may not
know, tied to a dated event (patch, price, release, shutdown).
Delete any sentence that survives a subject swap.

## E-E-A-T minimums per post

- One dated fact per section (patch/build number, date, price + day checked).
- Named source for every claim not verified firsthand.
- Firsthand tests stated explicitly (what, on what).
- Verdict split by reader type. No both-sides waffle.

## Internal links

At least one per section to a same-cluster post, descriptive anchor text.

## Explicitly NOT adopted

- Em-dash ban: house style uses them; no change.
- 2,000+ word targets: our posts run 150–1,000 by design (news beats,
  not features). Length follows the story.
- Blogger HTML: Eleventy Markdown it is.
